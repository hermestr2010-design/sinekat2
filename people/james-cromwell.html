Biyografi

James Oliver Cromwell Amerikalı bir aktör ve aktivist. En iyi bilinen filmlerinden bazıları şunlardır: Babe (1995), Star Trek: First Contact (1996), LA Confidential (1997), The Green Mile (1999), Space Cowboys (2000), Spirit: Stallion of the Cimarron (2002), The Queen (2006), Spider-Man 3 (2007), W. (2008), Secretariat (2010), The Artist (2011), Still Mine (2012), Big Hero 6 (2014) ve Jurassic World: Fallen Krallık (2018)dır.. Cromwell ayrıca Angels in America (2003), Six Feet Under (2003–2005), American Horror Story: Asylum (2012–2013), Boardwalk Empire (2012–2013), Halt and Catch gibi televizyondaki performanslarıyla da tanınır. Fire (2015), The Young Pope (2016), Counterpart (2018–2019) ve Succession (2018–2023) rol almıştır.
