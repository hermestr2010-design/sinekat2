Biyografi

Leopoldo Trieste was an Italian actor, film director, screenwriter and playwright. He worked with directors such as Federico Fellini, Francis Ford Coppola, Pietro Germi, Giuseppe Tornatore, Mario Bava, Tinto Brass, Charles Vidor, Jean-Jacques Annaud and René Clément.
