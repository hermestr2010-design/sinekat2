Konu

Bazil, a video-shop clerk, lost his father to a landmine and is later hit in the head by a stray bullet. He is taken in by a band of inventive junkyard scavengers, and together they plan to bring down the two weapons manufacturers behind both events by turning them against each other. Jean-Pierre Jeunet's 2009 comedy, with Dany Boon, is a cartoonish heist full of gadgets.
