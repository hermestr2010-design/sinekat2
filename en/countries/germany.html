Germany

German cinema peaked in the 1920s with Expressionism: Murnau, Lang and Wiene used distorted sets and shadows to show inner states, and Ufa built an industry that rivalled Hollywood. The Nazi era turned film into propaganda and drove many talents abroad. After the war the New German Cinema of Fassbinder, Herzog and Wenders confronted the past, and the reunified country has returned to history, memory and the Berlin Republic.
