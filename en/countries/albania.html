Albania

Albanian cinema was a state industry under communism, with the Kinostudio Shqipëria producing patriotic and partisan films under tight control. After the regime fell in 1991 the sector declined, yet filmmakers such as Kujtim Çashku have used the freer climate to explore the legacy of isolation, migration and memory.
