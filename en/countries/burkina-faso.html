Burkina Faso

Burkina Faso is a capital of African film: Ouagadougou hosts FESPACO, the continent's biggest film festival, held since 1969. Directors Idrissa Ouédraogo and Gaston Kaboré made village-based stories that mix oral tradition with modern concerns, and both won major international prizes.
