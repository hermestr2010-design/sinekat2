Mali

Mali is one of the major cinemas of Africa. Souleymane Cissé's Yeelen, a powerful film based on Bambara myth, won the Jury Prize at Cannes in 1987 and became a landmark of African film. Mali's cinema draws deeply on oral tradition, ritual and the politics of the postcolonial state.
