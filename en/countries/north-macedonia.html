North Macedonia

North Macedonian cinema was shaped within Yugoslavia and has since become known through two internationally recognised directors. Milčo Mančevski's Before the Rain won the Golden Lion in 1994, and Teona Strugar Mitevska's films have drawn attention for their treatment of women and tradition.
