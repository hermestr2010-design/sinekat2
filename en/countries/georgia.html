Georgia

Georgian cinema has a distinct tradition within Soviet film, known for colour, folklore, satire and humane irony. Tengiz Abuladze's Repentance, a dark parable about tyranny, became a landmark of the Soviet thaw, while Otar Iosseliani developed a gentle, comic style, much of it later made in France. Today a new generation is again winning attention.
