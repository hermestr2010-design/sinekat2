Spain

Spanish cinema was shaped by civil war and the Franco dictatorship, whose censorship pushed directors such as Berlanga, Bardem and Saura toward irony and allegory. Luis Buñuel built an international career in exile, and Víctor Erice produced rare, poetic work. After 1975 came the Movida and Pedro Almodóvar's colourful melodramas, and today Spain is known for strong horror, thriller and auteur traditions.
