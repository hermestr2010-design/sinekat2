Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnian cinema is closely tied to the Yugoslav years and the siege of Sarajevo, when filmmakers shot on the front lines. After the war, Danis Tanović's No Man's Land won the Oscar and Jasmila Žbanić's Grbavica the Golden Bear, putting wartime memory, trauma and reconciliation at the centre of the country's film identity.
