China

Chinese cinema began in Shanghai in the 1920s and 30s, with left-leaning studios and a thriving star system. After 1949 film served the state, and the Cultural Revolution almost stopped production. The Fifth Generation, graduates of the reopened Beijing Film Academy such as Chen Kaige and Zhang Yimou, brought lush images and historical allegory to the world in the 1980s, and later directors like Jia Zhangke turned to modern China's social change.
