USA

American cinema grew from nickelodeons and the New York and New Jersey studios to Hollywood, where the studio system of the 1920s to 1950s refined classical storytelling, genre and the star. Sound, colour and widescreen arrived there first. The 1970s brought a director-driven generation, the 1980s the blockbuster, and independent cinema has pushed against both ever since. Immigrant filmmakers from Europe shaped the style, and its films have influenced nearly every national cinema.
