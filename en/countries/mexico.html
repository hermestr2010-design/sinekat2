Mexico

Mexican cinema had a golden age in the 1930s to 50s, with stars, rancheras and melodrama that reached all of Latin America, and Emilio Fernández and cinematographer Gabriel Figueroa gave the country a distinctive look. Luis Buñuel made some of his finest films there in exile. In the 2000s Cuarón, del Toro and Iñárritu, 'the three amigos', became major figures in world cinema.
