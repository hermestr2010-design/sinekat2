Hong Kong

Hong Kong cinema was a self-contained industry, making Cantonese-language action, comedy and melodrama for audiences across Asia. In the 1980s and 90s John Woo's gunplay, Jackie Chan's stunts and Wong Kar-wai's romantic style gave it global fame. The 1997 handover and later pressures have made identity and memory recurring themes, and the 'Hong Kong New Wave' remains a touchstone for directors worldwide.
