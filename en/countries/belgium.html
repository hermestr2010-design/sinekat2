Belgium

Belgian cinema sits between French and Dutch-language traditions and is known for its documentary realism. The Dardenne brothers developed a rigorous, handheld style that won them two Palmes d'Or, Chantal Akerman reshaped feminist and experimental film with Jeanne Dielman, and a younger generation including Lukas Dhont continues to look at intimacy and class.
