Slovenia

Slovenian cinema is small but has its own tradition, shaped by the Yugoslav period and then independence in 1991. Directors such as Franci Slak, Metod Pevec and Janez Burger have made modest, locally rooted films, and the Slovenian Film Centre supports a mix of features, shorts and documentaries.
