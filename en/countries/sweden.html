Sweden

Swedish cinema had a brilliant silent period with Sjöström and Stiller, whose landscapes and psychological drama influenced Hollywood. Ingmar Bergman then made it a cinema of faith, doubt and relationships, working with a stable ensemble of actors and cinematographers such as Sven Nykvist. Public funding through the Swedish Film Institute has sustained a steady auteur and children's cinema since.
