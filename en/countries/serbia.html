Serbia

Serbian cinema grew inside Yugoslavia, with Belgrade as a major production centre, and then carried on through the wars of the 1990s. Emir Kusturica, though born in Sarajevo, made some of the region's best known films, and filmmakers such as Goran Paskaljević and Srđan Dragojević looked at war, corruption and the legacy of the break-up with black humour and bite.
