Norway

Norwegian cinema has long been small but steady, with state funding that supports both mainstream and arthouse films. Joachim Trier's Oslo trilogy and Dag Johan Haugerud's recent work show a modern, intelligent drama about urban relationships, while the country is also known for thrillers, dark comedy and nature-based storytelling.
