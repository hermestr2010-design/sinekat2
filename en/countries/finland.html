Finland

Finnish cinema had its golden age in the studio years and a long, quiet period afterwards. Aki Kaurismäki revived it in the 1980s with a deadpan, minimalist style, working-class characters and a love of old films and rock and roll, and made Finland a recognisable name on the festival circuit.
