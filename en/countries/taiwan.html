Taiwan

Taiwanese cinema found international fame with the New Cinema of the 1980s, led by Edward Yang and Hou Hsiao-hsien, who used long takes and complex ensembles to show a society negotiating its history and modernisation. Tsai Ming-liang later developed an even slower, spare style, and Ang Lee moved between Taiwan and Hollywood, winning Oscars for films from both worlds.
