Poland

Polish cinema produced a postwar school, with Andrzej Wajda at its head, that turned national trauma and resistance into images. Under communism censorship pushed directors to metaphor, and the 'cinema of moral anxiety' of the 1970s examined compromise and power. Polanski, Skolimowski, Kieślowski and later Holland and Pawlikowski carried Polish film across Europe and beyond.
