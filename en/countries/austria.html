Austria

Austrian cinema has long been tied to Vienna and to emigration. Directors such as von Stroheim, Sternberg, Pabst and Zinnemann made their mark in Berlin and Hollywood. Today the country is known for a cool, unsparing auteur tradition, with Michael Haneke and Ulrich Seidl examining violence, bourgeois comfort and social hypocrisy.
