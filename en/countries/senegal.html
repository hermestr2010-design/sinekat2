Senegal

Senegal is often called the cradle of African cinema. Ousmane Sembène, a novelist turned filmmaker, made Black Girl in 1966, among the first major features by a sub-Saharan African director, and insisted on telling African stories to African audiences. Djibril Diop Mambéty added a lyrical, experimental strand, and Safi Faye was among the first African women to direct a feature.
