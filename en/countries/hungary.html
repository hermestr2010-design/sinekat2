Hungary

Hungarian cinema has been unusually influential for a small country. Michael Curtiz began in Budapest before directing Casablanca in Hollywood, and after the war the state school nurtured Miklós Jancsó's long takes, Márta Mészáros's women's stories and István Szabó's study of compromise. Béla Tarr's slow, black-and-white films became a modern cinematic landmark, and Ildikó Enyedi won the Golden Bear.
