Guinea-Bissau

Guinea-Bissau's cinema was born with its liberation struggle, and Flora Gomes is its best-known director. His films, such as Mortu Nega and Nelisita, combine documentary, folk tales and music to treat war, memory and rebuilding, working with minimal means in one of the world's poorest countries.
