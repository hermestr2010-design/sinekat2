Greece

Greek cinema had a popular studio era of comedies and melodramas in the 1950s and 60s, along with Michael Cacoyannis's Zorba and international art films. During the junta of 1967 to 1974 filmmakers turned to allegory, and Theo Angelopoulos developed a poetic cinema of history, borders and long takes. Since the 2010s the 'Greek Weird Wave' of Lanthimos and Tsangari has used deadpan strangeness to describe crisis and control.
