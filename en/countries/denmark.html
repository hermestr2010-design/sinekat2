Denmark

Danish cinema was among the first industries in Europe, with Nordisk Film, and Carl Theodor Dreyer later made it a cinema of stillness and spiritual intensity. In 1995 Lars von Trier and Thomas Vinterberg launched Dogme 95, a vow of chastity that stripped film of artifice. State funding has helped a small country sustain an unusually strong record in drama, crime and television.
