Switzerland

Swiss cinema is multilingual and small, with distinct French, German and Italian currents. The 1970s New Swiss Cinema, led by Alain Tanner, took a critical, political look at a prosperous society, and the country has been a base for international co-productions and the Locarno Festival. Documentary and animation are strong.
