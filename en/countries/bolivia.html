Bolivia

Bolivian cinema is closely associated with Jorge Sanjinés and the Ukamau Group, who from the 1960s made militant, collective films with Indigenous communities, often in Quechua and Aymara. Their 'cinema made with the people' became a major reference for political film across Latin America.
