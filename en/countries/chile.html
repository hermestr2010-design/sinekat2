Chile

Chilean cinema developed a politically engaged 'New Chilean Cinema' in the 1960s and was disrupted by the 1973 coup, after which many directors worked in exile, among them Raúl Ruiz. Patricio Guzmán's documentaries on memory and the Atacama became a central body of work, and Pablo Larraín has since led a new wave of films on the dictatorship and its aftermath.
