Romania

Romanian cinema survived decades of communist censorship by working in metaphor, then reinvented itself after 1989. The 2000s Romanian New Wave, led by Cristian Puiu, Cristi Mungiu and Corneliu Porumboiu, favours long takes, small budgets and dry, precise stories about corruption and everyday compromise under and after the old regime. It has won top prizes at Cannes and Berlin.
