Biography

David Keith Williamson is an Australian playwright, who has also worked in film and television. He became known in the early 1970s with his political comic drama Don's Party, and subsequently wrote the plays The Club, Travelling North, and Emerald City. He is also known for writing the 1981 war film Gallipolli.
