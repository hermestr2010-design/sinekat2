Biography

Paul Laverty is an Irish and Scottish screenwriter and lawyer. He is best known for his collaborations with Ken Loach, including the screenplays for the Palme d'Or winning films The Wind That Shakes the Barley (2006) and I, Daniel Blake (2016).
