Biography

Andrea Checchi was an Italian actor, who appeared in over 180 film and television roles between 1934 and 1977. He won two Nastro d'Argento Awards during his career, Best Actor for Two Anonymous Letters (1945) and Best Supporting Actor for Parola di ladro (1957).
