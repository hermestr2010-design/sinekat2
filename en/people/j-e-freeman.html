Biography

James E. Freeman was an American actor. He was known to film audiences for his portrayals of intimidating villains, like Marcelles Santos in David Lynch's Wild at Heart (1990), Eddie Dane in the Coen brothers' Miller's Crossing (1990), and Dr. Wren in Alien Resurrection (1997).
