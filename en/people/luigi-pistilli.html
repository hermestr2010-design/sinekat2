Biography

Luigi Pistilli was an Italian actor. During his lifetime, he was one of Italy's most respected actors of stage, screen, and television; on stage, he was considered one of the country's finest interpreters of Bertolt Brecht's plays, including The Threepenny Opera and St Joan of the Stockyards.
