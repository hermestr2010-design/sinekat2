Biography

Kevin Michael Costner is an American actor, filmmaker, and musician. In a career spanning more than five decades, he has received various accolades, including two Academy Awards, three Golden Globe Awards, and a Primetime Emmy Award as well as nominations for three British Academy Film Awards.
