Biography

Radomir "Rade" Marković was a Serbian actor. He performed in more than ninety films. He was married to actress Olivera Marković from 1945 until they divorced in 1964. After the divorce he had a long affair with the Bulgarian actress Nevena Kokanova, whom he met during the filming of The Peach Thief.
