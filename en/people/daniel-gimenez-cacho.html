Biography

Daniel Giménez Cacho García is a Spanish and Mexican actor. Giménez Cacho made his film debut in Camino largo a Tijuana (1989). Early in his career, he portrayed Tito the Coroner in both Cronos (1993) and We Are What We Are (2010). He has also starred in Lucrecia Martel's period slow cinema film Zama (2017), Apichatpong Weerasethakul's slow cinema film Memoria (2021), and Alejandro González Iñárritu's epic psychological comedy-drama film Bardo, False Chronicle of a Handful of Truths (2022).
