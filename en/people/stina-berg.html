Biography

Stina Berg was a Swedish silent film actress. She appeared in more than 40 films between 1912 and 1931. She was also a stage actress, appearing in revues and folklustspel productions. She was married to actor Martin Karlsson until her death at Maria Hospital, Stockholm.
