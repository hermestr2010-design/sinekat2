Biography

Javier Ángel Encinas Bardem is a Spanish actor. In a career spanning over three decades, he has received various accolades, including an Academy Award, a BAFTA Award, a Golden Globe Award, a Critics' Choice Movie Award, two Actor Awards, and seven Goya Awards, in addition to a Cannes Film Festival Award and two Volpi Cups.
