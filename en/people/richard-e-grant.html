Biography

Richard E. Grant is a Swazi and English actor and presenter. He made his film debut as Withnail in the comedy Withnail and I (1987). Grant received critical acclaim for his role as Jack Hock in Marielle Heller's drama film Can You Ever Forgive Me? (2018), winning various awards including the Independent Spirit Award for Best Supporting Male. He also received Academy Award, Actor Award, BAFTA Award, Critics' Choice Movie Award, and Golden Globe Award nominations for Best Supporting Actor.
