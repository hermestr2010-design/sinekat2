Biography

Kirsten Caroline Dunst is an American actress and singer. She made her acting debut in the anthology film New York Stories (1989) and has since starred in many film and television productions. Her accolades include nominations for an Academy Award, a Primetime Emmy Award, and four Golden Globe Awards.
