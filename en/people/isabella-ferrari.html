Biography

Isabella Fogliazza, better known by her stage name of Isabella Ferrari, is an Italian actress. She's best known for her role as Chief Giovanna Scalise in the first two seasons of Italian police procedural series Distretto di Polizia (2000–2002). At the 1995 Venice Film Festival she won a Volpi Cup award for Best Supporting Actress in the film The Story of a Poor Young Man (1995).
