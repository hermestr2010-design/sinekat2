Biography

Jack Kehoe was an American film actor appearing in a wide variety of films, including the crime dramas Serpico (1973), The Sting (1973), The Pope of Greenwich Village (1984) and Brian De Palma's The Untouchables (1987), as well as the cult favorites The Friends of Eddie Coyle (1973), Car Wash (1976) and Midnight Run (1988), the popular Western Young Guns II (1990), and On the Nickel (1980).
