Biography

Morgan Wallace was an American actor. He appeared in more than 120 films between 1914 and 1946. He is perhaps best known to today's audiences for two W.C. Fields comedies. In It's a Gift (1934) he persistently asks grocer Fields for "Kumquats!" In My Little Chickadee (1940), he is a suspicious gambler who challenges Fields in a poker game. Wallace is also known for his performance as the sinister, international menace in Charlie Chan at the Olympics (1936).
