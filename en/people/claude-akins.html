Biography

Claude Aubrey Akins was an American character actor. He played Sonny Pruit in Movin' On, 1974–1976 American drama series about a trucking team; Sheriff Lobo on B.J. and the Bear and The Misadventures of Sheriff Lobo, 1979–1981 American action comedy television series; and in a variety of other roles on television as well as in feature films.
