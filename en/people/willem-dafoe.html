Biography

William James "Willem" Dafoe is an American actor. Known for his prolific career portraying diverse roles on stage and screen, he is the recipient of various accolades including a Volpi Cup Award for Best Actor, with nominations for four Academy Awards, a British Academy Film Award, and four Golden Globe Awards. He received an Honorary Golden Bear in 2018.
