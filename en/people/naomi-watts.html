Biography

Naomi Ellen Watts is a British actress. Known for her work in predominantly independent films, she has received various accolades, including an Actor Award, a Critics' Choice Award and two Saturn Awards, as well as nominations for two Academy Awards, a Primetime Emmy Award, and two Golden Globe Awards.
