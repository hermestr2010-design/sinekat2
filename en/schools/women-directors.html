Women Directors

Filmmakers who brought women's inner lives and experience to the centre of cinema.

Women directed films from the first years of cinema, with Alice Guy-Blaché and Lois Weber among the pioneers, but for decades the industry shut them out. Agnès Varda in France, Larisa Shepitko in the USSR and Chantal Akerman in Belgium opened the way for a more visible generation in the 1970s and 80s, including Margarethe von Trotta, Lina Wertmüller, Jane Campion and Kathryn Bigelow, the first woman to win the Best Director Oscar in 2010. Today Greta Gerwig, Chloé Zhao, Céline Sciamma, Lynne Ramsay, Claire Denis and many others work at the highest level. The list is less a style than a history of people making room for themselves.

What to look for

  • A wide range of styles rather than one look
  • Women's experience, desire and work as frequent subjects
  • Often independent or festival-based, with growing access to larger budgets
