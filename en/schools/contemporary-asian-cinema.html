Contemporary Asian Cinema

Stylish, genre-crossing and formally ambitious work from Hong Kong, Taiwan, Korea and Japan.

Since the 1980s, East Asia has produced some of the world's most inventive cinema. Hong Kong's Wong Kar-wai and John Woo mixed genre and style, Korean directors such as Park Chan-wook, Bong Joon-ho and Lee Chang-dong turned thrillers and dramas into sharp social portraits, and Bong's Parasite became the first non-English film to win Best Picture in 2020. Japan gave Hirokazu Kore-eda's family dramas and Hayao Miyazaki's animated worlds, Taiwan and mainland China added Ang Lee, Edward Yang and Jia Zhangke. Shared strengths are visual flair, genre play and close attention to class and family.
