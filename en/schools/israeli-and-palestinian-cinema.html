Israeli and Palestinian Cinema

Filmmakers of Israel and Palestine, from the war films and documentaries of the 1980s to the satires, dramas and animations that have made their way to Cannes, Berlin and the Academy Awards.

Film from Israel and Palestine is closely tied to the conflict and to questions of memory and identity. In the 1980s and 90s directors such as Amos Gitai made political films, and in the 2000s a new wave of Israeli filmmakers, including Ari Folman, Joseph Cedar and Samuel Maoz, turned to the army, the wars and personal trauma, with Folman's animated Waltz with Bashir (2008) a landmark. Palestinian directors such as Elia Suleiman and Hany Abu-Assad created films of deadpan humour and urgent drama, often made with great difficulty under occupation and travel limits. Nadav Lapid carries on a sharp, restless Israeli critique.
