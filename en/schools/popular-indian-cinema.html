Popular Indian Cinema

The great showmen and storytellers of mainstream Indian cinema, from the melodramas and social epics of the golden age to the genre-bending Hindi and Tamil films of today.

India makes more films each year than any other country, and its mainstream cinema is a tradition of its own, with songs, melodrama, romance and big emotion as essential ingredients. In the 1950s Guru Dutt, Mehboob Khan and Raj Kapoor made popular films with real social feeling, among them Pyaasa, Mother India and Awaara. Later generations stretched the form: Mani Ratnam in Tamil and Hindi, Anurag Kashyap and Vishal Bhardwaj with darker, grittier stories, and Zoya Akhtar with modern urban dramas. Bollywood, the Hindi-language industry in Mumbai, is only one part of a larger family that includes Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Malayalam cinemas.
