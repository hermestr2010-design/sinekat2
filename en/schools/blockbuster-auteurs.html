Blockbuster Auteurs

Directors who combine large-scale popular filmmaking with a personal vision.

Some directors have managed to make films that fill multiplexes and still carry a recognisable personal signature. Steven Spielberg and George Lucas created the modern blockbuster with Jaws and Star Wars, and James Cameron, Ridley Scott and Robert Zemeckis kept pushing the technology of spectacle. Christopher Nolan, Denis Villeneuve, Alfonso Cuarón, Peter Jackson and Tim Burton made large-scale films that also hold complex ideas, from time and memory to politics and mythology. The category is rather a question: how much room for personal vision is left when a film costs a hundred million dollars or more?

Where
United States, with directors from Britain, Mexico, Canada and New Zealand
When
1970s to the present

Steven Spielberg, George Lucas, James Cameron, Ridley Scott, Christopher Nolan, Denis Villeneuve, Alfonso Cuarón, Peter Jackson
Landmark films
Jaws (1975), Star Wars (1977), Blade Runner (1982), Inception (2010)
Legacy
Set the template for the global event film while keeping the idea of the director as author

What to look for

  • Large budgets and effects used to serve personal themes
  • Strong visual world-building
  • Big spectacle combined with feeling, wonder or ideas
