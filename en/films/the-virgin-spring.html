Plot

In medieval Sweden a farmer takes revenge after his daughter is attacked and killed. It won the Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bergman's medieval ballad of rape, murder and revenge is stark and punishing. It wrestles with faith and brutality, and it inspired Wes Craven's The Last House on the Left.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
