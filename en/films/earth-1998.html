Plot

Set around the 1947 Partition of India and Pakistan, based on Bapsi Sidhwa's novel. India submitted it for the Oscars.

Sinekatalog’s take

Deepa Mehta's Partition drama follows a Parsi girl watching friendships crumble in 1947 Lahore. Powerful and heartbreaking, with Aamir Khan and Nandita Das, it makes history intimate and tragic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
