Plot

In a provincial Russian town in the 1930s, the police chief Ivan Lapshin hunts a gang of bandits, and falls in love with an actress, Natasha. The story is seen through the eyes of a boy who lives in the same communal apartment. Aleksei German's 1984 film, shot in black and white with a crowded, vivid texture, was banned for two years in the Soviet Union, and is now regarded as a masterpiece.

Sinekatalog’s take

Aleksei German's masterpiece follows a 1930s police chief hunting bandits and loving an actress, seen through a boy's memory. Dense, crowded and extraordinary in its texture, it is a gritty, tragic portrait of Soviet life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
