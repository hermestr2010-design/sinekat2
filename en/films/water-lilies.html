Sinekatalog’s take

Céline Sciamma's debut explores teen desire among synchronised swimmers. Quiet, sensual and honest, with an early role for Adèle Haenel, it announced a distinct voice in coming-of-age cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
