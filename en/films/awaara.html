Plot

Raju, the son of a judge, is brought up in the slums as a thief after the judge, in a fit of jealousy, abandons his pregnant wife. As an adult, he meets Rita, a lawyer, and falls in love, but his criminal mentor Jagga schemes to destroy the judge through him. Raj Kapoor's 1951 film, with Nargis and Prithviraj Kapoor, was a huge success across the Soviet Union and Asia, and was in competition at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Raj Kapoor's classic follows a judge's son raised as a thief and in love with a lawyer. Song-filled and sentimental, with Nargis radiant and a famous dream sequence, it was a vast international hit and a landmark of Hindi cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
