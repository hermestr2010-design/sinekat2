Plot

Pasqualino, a small-time Neapolitan hoodlum and ladies' man, kills his sister's pimp, is sent to a mental asylum and then to the army. During the war he is captured by the Germans and sent to a concentration camp, where he tries to survive by seducing the formidable female commandant. Lina Wertmüller's 1975 film, with Giancarlo Giannini, is a grotesque and darkly comic tale that was Oscar-nominated for directing and writing.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lina Wertmüller's tragicomedy follows a Neapolitan hustler through war and a concentration camp. Giancarlo Giannini is superb in a bold, grotesque and darkly funny film that provoked fierce debate.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
