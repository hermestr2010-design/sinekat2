Plot

In a strict Scottish Calvinist community in the 1970s, a devout and naive young woman, Bess, marries an oil rig worker, Jan. After he is paralysed in an accident, he asks her to take lovers and tell him about it, and she believes that this will save him. Lars von Trier's 1996 film, with Emily Watson, is an emotional, controversial drama on faith and sacrifice.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lars von Trier's first of the 'Golden Heart' trilogy has Emily Watson as a devout, naive wife whose sacrifice for her paralysed husband turns dark. Raw, handheld and very divisive.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
