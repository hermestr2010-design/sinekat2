Plot

On a tiny Italian island, the shy Mario is hired as a postman to deliver mail to a single person, the exiled Chilean poet Pablo Neruda. The two men become friends, and Neruda teaches Mario about metaphors, which he uses to woo the beautiful Beatrice. Michael Radford's 1994 film, with Massimo Troisi, who died shortly after finishing it, and Philippe Noiret, was a hit and won an Academy Award for its score.
