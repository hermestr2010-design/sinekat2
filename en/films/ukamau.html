Cast

Plot

Made in Aymara, a story of an indigenous man seeking revenge.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jorge Sanjinés made this Bolivian film in Aymara, telling of an indigenous man's revenge after a mestizo kills his wife. It is blunt and powerful, a founding work of political cinema in Latin America.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
