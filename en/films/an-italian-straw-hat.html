Written by: René Clair

Plot

On his wedding day, a groom must replace a woman's straw hat that his horse has eaten, and chases around Paris to find an identical one.

Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's silent farce sends a groom racing around Paris to replace a straw hat his horse has eaten, as his wedding party trails behind. Fast, inventive and perfectly timed, it remains one of the great comedies of the silent era.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
