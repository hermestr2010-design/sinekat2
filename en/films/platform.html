Plot

Members of a state-run Fenyang theatre troupe live through the 1980s as China moves from Maoism to the market, and their company is privatised.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jia Zhangke's epic follows a state theatre troupe in Fenyang through the 1980s as China shifts from Maoism to the market. Long, patient and tender, with Zhao Tao, it is a landmark of Chinese cinema and national memory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
