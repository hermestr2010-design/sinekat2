Plot

A Disney True-Life Adventure documentary portrays the animals of the American south-western desert through a year of seasons. Scorpions dance in a courtship, a wasp battles a tarantula and rattlesnakes, lizards and kangaroo rats survive the heat, while storms bring bursts of life to the sand. James Algar's 1953 film combined remarkable close-up photography with a humorous narration, and it won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature.
