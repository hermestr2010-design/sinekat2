Sinekatalog’s take

Tod Browning's 1931 horror landmark is stagey but atmospheric. Bela Lugosi's hypnotic Count defined the vampire in popular culture, and the early castle scenes remain wonderfully eerie.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
