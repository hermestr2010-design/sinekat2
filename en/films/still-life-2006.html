Written by: Zhangke Jia

Plot

Two people, a coal miner, Han Sanming, and a nurse, Shen Hong, each travel to the town of Fengjie, in order to look for their spouses. The town is being demolished and flooded for the Three Gorges Dam, and they wander through the ruins, a landscape of change. Jia Zhangke's 2006 film, which blends fiction and documentary, won the Golden Lion at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jia Zhangke's drama follows two people searching for spouses in a Yangtze town being flooded for the Three Gorges Dam. Quiet, wry and surreal, it is a striking portrait of a country transforming at human cost.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
