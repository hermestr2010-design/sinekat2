Plot

In 1922, a young Greek woman is one of a group of picture brides who sail to America to marry men they have never met. On the ship she falls in love with a photographer, which brings her into conflict with her promise. Pantelis Voulgaris's 2004 film, produced by Martin Scorsese, is a lyrical, black-and-white drama of emigration, hope and sacrifice.

Sinekatalog’s take

Pantelis Voulgaris's period drama follows a Greek picture bride sailing to America and falling for a photographer. Elegantly shot and moving, with a restrained love story, it is a handsome, bittersweet immigrant tale produced by Scorsese.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
