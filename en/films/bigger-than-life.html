Plot

A schoolteacher given the experimental drug cortisone for a fatal illness becomes addicted and tyrannical to his family.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nicholas Ray's domestic drama has James Mason turning tyrannical on cortisone. A savage, Cinemascope critique of 1950s family life and medicine, it is a bold, bitter and increasingly acclaimed masterpiece.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
