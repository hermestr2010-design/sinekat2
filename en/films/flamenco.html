Plot

A film of performances in the flamenco tradition.

Sinekatalog’s take

Carlos Saura's musical documentary stages flamenco's finest performers in a series of dazzling, minimalist numbers under Vittorio Storaro's light. Sensual, rhythmic and beautiful, it is a joyous celebration of the form.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
