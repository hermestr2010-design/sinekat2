Sinekatalog’s take

Sam Peckinpah's first feature follows a former soldier and a saloon girl burying her son in a dangerous desert trip. Maureen O'Hara and Brian Keith are good, and the film shows hints of Peckinpah's later toughness.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
