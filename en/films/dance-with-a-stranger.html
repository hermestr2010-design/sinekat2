Plot

In 1950s London, a hostess at a club falls obsessively in love with a rich playboy, and ends up as the last woman hanged in Britain.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mike Newell's drama retells the true story of Ruth Ellis, the last woman hanged in Britain, and her fatal passion. Miranda Richardson is superb and Rupert Everett smoulders in a cool, atmospheric 1950s tragedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
