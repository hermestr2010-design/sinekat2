Sinekatalog’s take

Josef von Sternberg's adaptation of Dreiser follows an ambitious young man who drifts toward murdering his pregnant girlfriend for a rich girl. Phillips Holmes and Sylvia Sidney star in a sombre, stylish but compressed telling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
