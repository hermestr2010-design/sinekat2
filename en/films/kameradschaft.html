Plot

After a mine disaster on the French border, German miners cross the frontier to rescue French colleagues.

Sinekatalog’s take

G.W. Pabst's mining drama has German miners crossing the border to rescue French colleagues after a disaster. Tense and humanist, with striking sets, it is a powerful early sound plea for international solidarity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
