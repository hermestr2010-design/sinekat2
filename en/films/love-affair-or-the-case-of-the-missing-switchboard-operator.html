Plot

A young telephone operator in Belgrade has an affair with a rat exterminator, while a sexologist and a criminologist lecture about love and murder.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dušan Makavejev's playful, essayistic film mixes a Belgrade telephone operator's affair with lectures on sex and murder. Provocative, funny and formally daring, it is a landmark of Yugoslav Black Wave cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
