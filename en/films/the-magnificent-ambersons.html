Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's follow-up to Citizen Kane, butchered by its studio, remains a haunting study of a proud family's decline. Even in cut form, its camerawork and sadness are extraordinary.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
