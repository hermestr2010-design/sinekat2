Plot

In a bleak, post-industrial country, a guide called the Stalker leads a Writer and a Professor into the Zone, a forbidden area where, it is said, a room can grant a person's deepest wish. The journey through the waterlogged, overgrown landscape becomes a test of faith and doubt, with the men arguing about what they truly want. Andrei Tarkovsky's 1979 film, loosely based on a novel by the Strugatsky brothers, is a slow, hypnotic spiritual parable.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tarkovsky sends a guide, a writer and a scientist into a forbidden Zone said to grant wishes. Slow, hypnotic and philosophical, it rewards patience with some of cinema's most haunting images.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
