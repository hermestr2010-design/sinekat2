Sinekatalog’s take

Apichatpong Weerasethakul's languid film follows a Burmese migrant and his lover on a day in the Thai forest. Sensuous and dreamy, split into two halves, it is a slow, hypnotic and gently erotic work of tropical drift.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
