Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Andrzej Wajda's sequel to Man of Marble follows a journalist investigating a shipyard strike leader as Solidarity rises. Urgent and political, it won the Palme d'Or and helped define Polish cinema's moral force.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
