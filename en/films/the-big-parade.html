Plot

A rich young American enlists in the First World War, finds love in a French village and loses his leg at the front.

Sinekatalog’s take

King Vidor's silent epic follows a rich American doughboy through love in France and the horrors of the trenches. Moving and visceral, with John Gilbert and Renée Adorée, it was a huge hit and set the war film template.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
