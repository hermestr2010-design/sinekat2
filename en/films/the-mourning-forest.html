Plot

Machiko, a nurse in a rural care home, is grieving the loss of her child. She forms a bond with Shigeki, an elderly resident who is grieving the death of his wife. During a drive, the car breaks down, and they go into the forest, in a journey of mourning. Naomi Kawase's 2007 film, known in Japanese as Mogari no mori, won the Grand Prix at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Naomi Kawase's Cannes prize-winner follows a nurse and an old man grieving their losses who wander into a forest. Gentle, meditative and lush, it is a spiritual, quiet and often beautiful film about grief and nature.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
