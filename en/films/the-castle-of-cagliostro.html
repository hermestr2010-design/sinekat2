Sinekatalog’s take

Hayao Miyazaki's first feature sends the thief Lupin to a small European state to rescue a princess and uncover a counterfeiting ring. Brisk, funny and full of chases, it is a joyful adventure that points toward his later work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
