Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Nagisa Oshima's family saga tells postwar Japan's history through a clan's ceremonies, weddings and funerals, tinged with sinister secrets. Cool, stylised and satirical, it is a rich and caustic portrait of tradition and authority.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
