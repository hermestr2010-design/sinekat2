Sinekatalog’s take

Lars von Trier's hospital miniseries mixes soap opera, ghost story and black comedy in a Copenhagen hospital where strange things happen. Ernst-Hugo Järegård is wonderfully grim, and the whole thing is creepy, funny and addictive.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
