Plot

Amalric and Devos play ex-lovers, with Deneuve as a psychiatrist, in a mix of comedy and tragedy. Amalric won the César for best actor. Known in French as Rois et reine.

Sinekatalog’s take

Arnaud Desplechin's rich, restless film juggles comedy and tragedy around a woman's past lovers and a dying father. Emmanuelle Devos and Mathieu Amalric are superb, with Catherine Deneuve in support, in a daring, sprawling tragicomedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
