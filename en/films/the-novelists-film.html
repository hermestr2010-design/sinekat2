Sinekatalog’s take

Hong Sang-soo's black-and-white film follows a blocked novelist who meets an actress and decides to make a short film with her. Gentle, simple and deeply human, it won a Berlin Silver Bear Grand Jury Prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
