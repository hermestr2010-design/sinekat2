Plot

After the mine where he worked closes, a man heads to Helsinki, is robbed, and ends up in prison and then on the run.

Sinekatalog’s take

Aki Kaurismäki's deadpan crime comedy follows a jobless miner who heads to Helsinki and ends up in trouble. Dry, melancholic and tender, with Turo Pajala sympathetic, it is a classic of Finnish minimalism.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
