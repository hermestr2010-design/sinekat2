Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Alexander Mackendrick's Ealing satire has Alec Guinness inventing indestructible cloth that both bosses and workers fear. Witty and sharp about industry and progress, it is a classic of British comedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
