Structure of Crystals

1969 · Directed by Krzysztof Zanussi
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Andrzej Żarnecki, Jan Myśłowicz, Barbara Wrzesińska

Plot

Two physicists, one who is ambitious and works in the city and another who has chosen a quiet life in a remote weather station, discuss their choices.

Sinekatalog’s take

Krzysztof Zanussi's early film has two physicists debate the lives they chose, one ambitious in the city and one hidden in a remote weather station. It is a quiet, thoughtful meditation on success, and on what we owe to knowledge.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
