Sinekatalog’s take

Arthur Penn's nostalgic drama follows a Yugoslav immigrant's son and his three friends through the 1960s in America. Loose, charming and episodic, with a fine ear for the era, it is a warm if rambling coming-of-age tale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
