Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Mack Sennett's 1913 short has a villain tie Mabel Normand to the tracks while a racing driver chases the train. Fast, silly and parodic, it is a lively early Keystone burlesque of melodrama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
