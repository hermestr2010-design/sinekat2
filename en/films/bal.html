Plot

Yusuf, a six-year-old boy who stutters, lives with his parents in a forest village in northern Turkey, where his father collects honey from beehives hanging high in the trees. When the bees mysteriously disappear, his father goes in search of them, and does not return. Semih Kaplanoğlu's 2010 film, the third part of his Yusuf trilogy, won the Golden Bear in Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take

Semih Kaplanoğlu's Golden Bear winner follows a stuttering boy in a Turkish forest village as bees vanish. Quiet, luminous and patient, with natural sound and light, it is the lyrical close of his Yusuf trilogy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
