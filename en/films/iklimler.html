Plot

A couple's relationship breaks down over the course of a year, shown through the seasons. It won the FIPRESCI prize at Cannes. English title: Climates.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nuri Bilge Ceylan's study of a couple's breakup across the seasons is cool, precise and painfully honest. Shot with long takes and wintry images, it is a bleak, unflinching look at emotional distance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
