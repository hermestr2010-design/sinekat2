Plot

A lonely young woman looks for love on a summer holiday and hopes to see a rare green flash at sunset.

Sinekatalog’s take

Eric Rohmer's improvised summer drama follows a lonely woman hoping for love and a rare green flash. Marie Rivière is wonderfully raw and the film is moving, funny and beautifully attentive to everyday feeling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
