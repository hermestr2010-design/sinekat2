Plot

A Calcutta housewife takes a job to help her family. Known in Bengali as Mahanagar.

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's drama follows a Calcutta housewife who takes a job and discovers independence. Madhabi Mukherjee is wonderful, and the film's warmth and precision make a gentle, perceptive look at gender and change.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
