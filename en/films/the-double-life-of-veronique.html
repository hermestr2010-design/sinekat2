The Double Life of Véronique

Original title: La double vie de Véronique · Turkish title: Veronique'nin İkili Yaşamı

“Each of us is matched somewhere in the world, by our exact double - someone who shares our thoughts and dreams.”

1991 · 1 h 38 min · Directed by Krzysztof Kieślowski
DramaFantasyMusical
Watch trailer

Cast

Irène Jacob, Philippe Volter, Sandrine Dumas

Plot

Two identical young women, one in Poland and one in France, share a mysterious bond without ever meeting.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kieślowski's dreamlike tale of two women who share a mysterious bond is intuitive and haunting. Irène Jacob is luminous, and the film's golden light and Preisner's score are unforgettable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
