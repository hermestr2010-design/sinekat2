Cast

Plot

In 2019 Neo-Tokyo, a motorcycle gang member, Kaneda, sees his friend Tetsuo given strange powers by a military experiment, linked to the legendary Akira. As Tetsuo's abilities spin out of control, and the government, a resistance group and Kaneda's gang battle to stop him, the city is torn apart. Katsuhiro Otomo's 1988 animation, adapted from his own manga, was a landmark in bringing anime to the West.

Sinekatalog’s take

Katsuhiro Otomo's cyberpunk anime about biker gangs and psychic power in a ruined Neo-Tokyo. Its hand-drawn detail and kinetic action opened the West to anime, and it still looks astonishing.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
