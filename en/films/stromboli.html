Plot

A displaced Lithuanian woman marries a fisherman and moves to a volcanic island.

Sinekatalog’s take

Roberto Rossellini's drama strands Ingrid Bergman's displaced woman on a volcanic island. Raw and controversial in its time, with striking tuna-fishing and eruption scenes, it is a stark, spiritual study of isolation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
