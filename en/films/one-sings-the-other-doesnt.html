Sinekatalog’s take

Agnès Varda's feminist musical follows two friends through fifteen years of choices about motherhood, work and independence. Warm, sunny and songful, with Valérie Mairesse and Thérèse Liotard, it is a hopeful, politically alive film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
