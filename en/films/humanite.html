Humanité

1999 · Directed by Bruno Dumont
CrimeDramaMystery
Watch trailer

Cast

Emmanuel Schotté, Séverine Caneele

Plot

In a small town in northern France, Pharaon De Winter, a lonely police detective, investigates the rape and murder of a young girl. He is a quiet, odd man, and his investigation is slow, rather than driven by action. Bruno Dumont's 1999 film won the Grand Prix at Cannes, plus the best actor prize for Emmanuel Schotté and best actress for Séverine Caneele, both non-professionals.
