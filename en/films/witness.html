Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Weir's thriller drops a city cop into Amish country, and the culture clash is quiet and affecting. Harrison Ford is excellent, and the barn-raising and romance give it unusual gentleness.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
