Sinekatalog’s take

Cocteau adapts his own play about a queen who falls for the young anarchist sent to kill her, with Edwige Feuillère and Jean Marais. It is grand, stagey and unabashedly romantic, a showcase for two great performers.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
