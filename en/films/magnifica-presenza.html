Plot

A young man in Rome finds his flat is haunted by actors. English title: Magnificent Presence.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ferzan Özpetek's gentle comedy-fantasy has a young Roman discovering his flat is haunted by a theatre troupe. Warm, witty and tender, with Elio Germano, it is a delicate love letter to acting and chosen family.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
