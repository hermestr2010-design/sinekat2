Sinekatalog’s take

Ryusuke Hamaguchi and Kō Sakai film survivors of the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami as they simply talk about loss. Quiet and patient, it listens more than it shows, and that restraint is its power.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
