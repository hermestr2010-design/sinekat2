Sinekatalog’s take

Vsevolod Pudovkin's first sound film follows a German worker who flees to the Soviet Union after a failed strike. Experimental in its use of sound and montage, it is a bold, ideological but uneven work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
