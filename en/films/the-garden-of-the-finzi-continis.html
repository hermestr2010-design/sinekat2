Sinekatalog’s take

Vittorio De Sica's elegiac drama follows a wealthy Jewish family sealing itself away as fascism closes in. Beautiful and sad, with Dominique Sanda luminous, it won the Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
