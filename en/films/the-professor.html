Plot

His first full-length film, which won the Silver Ribbon for best new director. Known in Italian as Il camorrista.

Sinekatalog’s take

Giuseppe Tornatore's debut follows a Neapolitan criminal's rise to head a camorra clan in the 1970s. Ben Gazzara plays the boss in a stark, gritty and powerfully made crime drama that won the Silver Ribbon for new director.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
