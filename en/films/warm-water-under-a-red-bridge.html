Sinekatalog’s take

Shohei Imamura's late comedy-fantasy follows a jobless man who meets a woman whose body releases torrents of water. Playful, sensual and earthy, with Koji Yakusho, it is a generous, funny and strange film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
