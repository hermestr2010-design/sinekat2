Plot

A couple live on a small island with no water supply. The film has no dialogue. It won the Grand Prize at Moscow in 1961.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kaneto Shindo's dialogue-free film watches a family farming a barren island, hauling water by boat. Beautiful, ritualistic and poignant, it is a pure, powerful study of labour and endurance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
