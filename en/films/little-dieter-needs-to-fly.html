Sinekatalog’s take

Werner Herzog's documentary follows Dieter Dengler, shot down over Laos, back to his survival ordeal. Compelling and eerie, with Dengler a gripping storyteller, it is one of Herzog's finest nonfiction films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
