Plot

Two detectives, the veteran William Somerset, who is about to retire, and the young David Mills, hunt a serial killer who stages murders based on the seven deadly sins. As they follow the clues, the case becomes more twisted, leading to a shocking climax. David Fincher's 1995 thriller, with Morgan Freeman and Brad Pitt, is known for its grimy atmosphere and its ending.

Sinekatalog’s take

A grim detective thriller built around the seven deadly sins. Fincher's rain-soaked, decaying city is as much a character as the killer, and the ending is a gut punch that still gets discussed.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
