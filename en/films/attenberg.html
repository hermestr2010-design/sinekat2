Plot

Marina, a socially awkward young woman in a Greek industrial town, who learns about the world from nature documentaries by David Attenborough, takes care of her dying father, an architect. She experiments with sex and affection through her friend Bella and a visiting engineer, in a series of odd, deadpan scenes. Athina Rachel Tsangari's 2010 film, a key work of the Greek Weird Wave, earned Ariane Labed the Best Actress prize at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take

Athina Rachel Tsangari's Greek weird-wave oddity follows a young woman learning about life from nature documentaries. Deadpan, strange and moving, with Ariane Labed superb, it is a quirky look at death and desire.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
