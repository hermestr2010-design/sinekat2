Plot

In Berlin in 1931, as the Nazis gain power, the English student Brian Roberts rents a room and befriends Sally Bowles, an American singer at the seedy Kit Kat Club. Their relationship develops into a triangle with a wealthy baron, while outside the club, the political climate becomes more sinister. Bob Fosse's 1972 musical, with Liza Minnelli and Joel Grey, won eight Academy Awards.
