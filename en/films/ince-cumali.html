Plot

A 1967 film by Yılmaz Duru, named after its title character İnce Cumali, an outlaw. It won Duru the Golden Orange for best director for a second year in a row, and belongs to the tradition of popular Turkish cinema of the late 1960s.

Sinekatalog’s take

Yılmaz Duru's adventure film follows the outlaw İnce Cumali in a popular late-1960s Turkish western tradition. It won him the Golden Orange for best director for a second year and is a vivid, earthy example of Yeşilçam action.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
