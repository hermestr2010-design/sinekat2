Sinekatalog’s take

Derviş Zaim's film, set in Cyprus, was awarded the UNESCO prize at Venice for its look at division and landscape. Spare and austere, it was shown abroad more than at home and is considered a weaker entry in his filmography.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
