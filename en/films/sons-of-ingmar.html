Sinekatalog’s take

Victor Sjöström adapts Selma Lagerlöf's tale of a family and its faith, with Tore Svennberg and Harriet Bosse. It is stately and landscape-bound, a fine example of the Swedish silent school.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
