Plot

Cody Jarrett, a psychotic gangster with a severe mother fixation, leads a gang in violent robberies. When he is caught, an undercover agent is placed in his cell to learn where the loot is hidden. As the police close in, Cody's reliance on his mother and his headaches lead him towards a fiery end at a chemical plant. Raoul Walsh's 1949 film, with a ferocious performance by James Cagney, includes the famous cry "Made it, Ma! Top of the world!"

Sinekatalog’s take

James Cagney's mother-obsessed gangster Cody Jarrett is volatile and unforgettable. Raoul Walsh's crime film moves fast, and its explosive 'Top of the world, Ma!' finale is iconic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
