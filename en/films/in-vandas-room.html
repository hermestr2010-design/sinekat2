Plot

Vanda, a drug user, spends her days in her bedroom in the Fontainhas, coughing, talking to her sister, while her neighbourhood is demolished.

Sinekatalog’s take

Pedro Costa's long, intimate film watches Vanda in her bedroom in Lisbon's Fontainhas as her neighbourhood is demolished. Shot in dim, painterly light, it is a patient, haunting document of poverty and addiction.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
