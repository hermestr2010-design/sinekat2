Plot

In 1987 Romania, under Ceaușescu, a university student, Otilia, helps her roommate Găbița find an illegal abortion. They meet the abortionist, Mr Bebe, in a hotel room, and the day becomes dangerous as Otilia faces choices. Cristian Mungiu's 2007 film, shot in long takes in a bleak, realistic style, won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.
