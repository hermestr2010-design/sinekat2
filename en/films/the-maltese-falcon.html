Plot

San Francisco private detective Sam Spade takes a case from a woman who calls herself Miss Wonderly, after which his partner is murdered. He becomes caught up in the search by several schemers for a jewelled statue of a falcon, including the suave Joel Cairo, the sinister Kasper Gutman and the lying Brigid O'Shaughnessy. John Huston's 1941 directorial debut, with Humphrey Bogart as Spade, set the template of film noir, and its dialogue and shadows have been imitated ever since.

Sinekatalog’s take

Huston's debut defined film noir: Bogart's Sam Spade, a femme fatale and a hunt for a statuette. The dialogue is tough and fast, and the cynicism has barely aged.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
