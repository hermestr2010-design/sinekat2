Plot

Built on an improvisation workshop for non-professionals. Its four lead actresses shared the best actress award at Locarno.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ryusuke Hamaguchi's five-hour film follows four Kobe women in their thirties as a friend's divorce reveals buried tensions. Its four non-professional leads won Locarno's acting prize in a patient, absorbing and deeply perceptive drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
