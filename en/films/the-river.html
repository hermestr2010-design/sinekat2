Plot

A British family lives on the banks of the Ganges, seen through the eyes of a teenage girl.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Renoir's first colour film watches a British family on the Ganges through a girl's eyes. Gentle, lyrical and beautifully shot on location, it is a luminous, sensuous meditation on growing up and cycles of life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
