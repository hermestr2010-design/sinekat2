Plot

A screwball comedy in San Francisco in which four identical overnight bags are mixed up and a scatterbrained woman upsets a stuffy musicologist.

Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Bogdanovich's screwball comedy is a fast, silly homage to Bringing Up Baby. Barbra Streisand and Ryan O'Neal are fun, and the San Francisco car chase is a joyful highlight.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
