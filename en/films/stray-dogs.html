Plot

A homeless father and his two children wander through Taipei, holding up advertising signs for pay, and take shelter in an abandoned building.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tsai Ming-liang's austere film follows a homeless father and his children living on Taipei's edges. Painfully slow with stunning long takes, it is a demanding, haunting study of poverty and endurance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
