Plot

A biopic of Martin Luther King Jr. and the 1965 Selma to Montgomery marches for voting rights in Alabama. The film shows King's campaign, his negotiations with President Johnson, and the violence of the police and the segregationists. Ava DuVernay's 2014 film, with David Oyelowo as King, was nominated for Best Picture and won an Oscar for the song "Glory".

Sinekatalog’s take

Ava DuVernay's drama of the 1965 voting rights marches is focused and powerful. David Oyelowo gives King humanity and strategic intelligence, and the film makes history feel urgent.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
