Plot

A woman recovering from mental illness spends a summer with her family on a remote island and slips back into delusion. It won the Oscar for Best Foreign Language Film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bergman's chamber drama follows a woman's mental decline on an island. Spare and intense, with four actors, it marks his turn toward silence, doubt and God's absence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
