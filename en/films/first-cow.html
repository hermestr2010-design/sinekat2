Plot

In the 1820s Oregon, a cook and a Chinese immigrant become friends and start a business selling cakes made with milk stolen from the only cow in the area.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kelly Reichardt's gentle frontier story of two friends selling stolen-milk cakes is tender and quietly tense. Slow and observant, it prizes friendship over conquest, with a quietly devastating end.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
