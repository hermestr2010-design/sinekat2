Plot

In rural northern France in the early twentieth century, the young priest Donissan, tormented by self-doubt, mortifies himself and is convinced he is constantly tempted by the devil. He meets a young woman, Mouchette, who has killed a man, and he faces a series of spiritual tests. Maurice Pialat's 1987 film, based on a novel by Georges Bernanos, with Gérard Depardieu, won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Maurice Pialat's Palme d'Or winner follows a tormented rural priest battling doubt and the devil. Gérard Depardieu is intense in a harsh, divisive adaptation of Bernanos that was booed and then widely admired.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
