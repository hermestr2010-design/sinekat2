Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph L. Mankiewicz's romantic fantasy of a widow and a sea captain's ghost is gentle, witty and moving. Gene Tierney and Rex Harrison are wonderful, and Bernard Herrmann's score is superb.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
