Plot

A drama about a deputy mayor and a housing estate scheduled for demolition. Known in English as Les Indésirables.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ladj Ly's drama follows a doctor-turned-deputy mayor's push to demolish a Paris-suburb housing estate and the residents' fight to stay. Urgent and angry, it is a gripping, political follow-up to Les Misérables.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
