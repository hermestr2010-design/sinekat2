Plot

In a Carpathian village a young man of the Hutsul mountain people loses his beloved and is driven to tragedy. It mixes folklore, ritual and a restless camera.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sergei Parajanov's Carpathian love tragedy is a wild folkloric feast of colour, ritual and swirling camera. Hypnotic and poetic, it is a landmark of Soviet Ukrainian cinema and still intoxicating.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
