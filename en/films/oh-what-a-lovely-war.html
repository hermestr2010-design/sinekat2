Plot

A satirical musical of the First World War, staged on Brighton pier, using the songs of the time.

Sinekatalog’s take

Richard Attenborough's directorial debut stages the First World War as a bitter musical revue on Brighton pier. With a huge British cast and period songs, it is a clever, savage and moving anti-war spectacle.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
