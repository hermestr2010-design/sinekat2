Weekend (1930)

1930 · 10 min · Directed by Walter Ruttmann
Documentary
Watch trailer

Cast

Experimental sound montage; no actors

Plot

A work made only of sound, assembled from Berlin street sounds recorded on an optical soundtrack, with no images. Known in German as Wochenende.

Sinekatalog’s take

Walter Ruttmann's experimental piece is a film without images, assembled from Berlin street sounds on an optical soundtrack. Playful and rhythmic, it is a pioneering sound collage and an important early work of radio art.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
