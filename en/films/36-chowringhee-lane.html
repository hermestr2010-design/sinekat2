Plot

Violet Stoneham, an elderly Anglo-Indian schoolteacher in Calcutta, lives alone in a shabby flat with her cat. She is kind to a young couple who borrow her flat for their meetings, and in return she gains a little companionship. Aparna Sen's 1981 debut as writer and director, produced by Shashi Kapoor, with Jennifer Kendal, is a gentle study of loneliness, and won her the National Award for best direction.
