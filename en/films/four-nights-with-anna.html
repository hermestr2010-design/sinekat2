Sinekatalog’s take

Jerzy Skolimowski's quiet drama follows a hospital worker who nightly watches the woman who was assaulted years ago, entering her home in secret. Strange and tender, it is a haunting study of obsession and love.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
