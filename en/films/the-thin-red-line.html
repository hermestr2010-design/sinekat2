Sinekatalog’s take

Terrence Malick's Guadalcanal drama is a meditative, poetic war film. Its whispered voice-overs and nature imagery turn combat into a spiritual question, and it is beautiful, strange and wandering.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
