Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Vigo's short documentary on champion swimmer Jean Taris uses slow motion and underwater shots to turn strokes into poetry. Playful and graceful, it is a brief, lovely early demonstration of Vigo's gift for movement.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
