Plot

A British agent provokes a slave revolt on a Portuguese Caribbean island to open it to British commerce, and returns years later to crush its leader.

Sinekatalog’s take

Gillo Pontecorvo's political drama has Marlon Brando as a British agent stirring then crushing a Caribbean slave revolt. Powerful and angry, with Ennio Morricone's thrilling score, it is a sharp lesson in colonial manipulation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
