Sinekatalog’s take

Matteo Garrone's Oscar-nominated drama follows two Senegalese teens on a perilous migration to Europe. Rooted in humane, almost fable-like imagery, it is urgent and powerful without being sensationalist.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
