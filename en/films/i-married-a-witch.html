Cast

Plot

A witch burned in Salem returns three centuries later and tries to ruin the descendant of her accusers, but falls in love with him.

Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's light supernatural comedy has Veronica Lake as a witch pursuing her ancestors' foe. Charming, witty and sexy, it inspired Bewitched and remains a delightful fantasy-romance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
