Plot

In 1939 a Communist soldier collecting folk songs stays with a peasant family in Shaanxi. Their young daughter dreams of escaping an arranged marriage and joining the Red Army.

Sinekatalog’s take

Chen Kaige's landmark debut follows a Communist soldier collecting folk songs who stays with a peasant family in 1939. Visually stunning, with vast landscapes and silence, it launched China's Fifth Generation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
