Plot

Marty McFly, a teenager in 1985, is accidentally sent back to 1955 in the DeLorean time machine built by the eccentric scientist Doc Brown. He disrupts the first meeting of his parents, and must make sure they fall in love, or he will never be born, while finding a way to get back. Robert Zemeckis's 1985 comedy, with Michael J. Fox, was a huge hit.

Sinekatalog’s take
