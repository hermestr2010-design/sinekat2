Plot

In a Rio de Janeiro slum in the 1960s and 1970s, the growth of organised crime is seen through the eyes of Rocket, a young man who dreams of becoming a photographer. He grows up alongside Li'l Zé, a ruthless gang leader, and his friend Benny. Fernando Meirelles's 2002 film, with its kinetic style and non-professional cast, was nominated for four Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

Rio's favela gangs told with whip-pan energy and a big ensemble. Meirelles mixes documentary grit with flashy editing, and the result is exhilarating and bleak in equal measure.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
