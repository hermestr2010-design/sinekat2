Sinekatalog’s take

Victor Fleming's pre-Code steamer has Clark Gable's rubber planter between Jean Harlow's good-time girl and Mary Astor's lady. Sultry, funny and sharp, with a famous rain-barrel bath, it is a hot, enjoyable romp.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
