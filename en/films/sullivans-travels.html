Plot

John L. Sullivan, a successful Hollywood director of comedies, wants to make a serious film about human suffering, "O Brother, Where Art Thou?". To learn what poverty is like, he sets out dressed as a tramp, but keeps ending up back in comfort. Meeting a girl, played by Veronica Lake, he finally experiences real misfortune. Preston Sturges's 1941 comedy moves between slapstick and sobering drama, and finishes with the argument that laughter is also a gift to the poor.

Sinekatalog’s take
