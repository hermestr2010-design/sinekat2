The Invisible Life of Eurídice Gusmão

2019 · Directed by Karim Aïnouz
DramaRomance
Watch trailer

Cast

Carol Duarte, Julia Stockler, Fernanda Montenegro

Plot

In 1950s Rio, two inseparable sisters, Eurídice and Guida, dream of different futures, a career as a pianist and a romantic escape. When Guida runs away with a Greek sailor, they are separated, and each lives for decades believing the other is somewhere else. Karim Aïnouz's 2019 film, a melodrama, won the Un Certain Regard prize at Cannes.
