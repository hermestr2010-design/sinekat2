Sinekatalog’s take

Maurice Pialat's early comedy-drama follows teenagers in a French provincial town in a loose, naturalistic style. Warm and observant, with non-professional faces, it is a lively, truthful portrait of youth that shows his raw directness.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
