Plot

A drama shown at Locarno and Rotterdam, which won the Golden Orange for best screenplay. English title: The Third Page.

Sinekatalog’s take

Zeki Demirkubuz's bleak drama follows a young man whose obsession with a woman drags both toward ruin. Raw and fatalistic, in the director's mournful style, it won the Golden Orange for best screenplay.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
