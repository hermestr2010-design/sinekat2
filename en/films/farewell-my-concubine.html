Plot

Two boys, Douzi and Shitou, train together at a Peking opera school and become stars, one playing heroic male roles and the other female ones. Over fifty years, from the 1920s through the Japanese occupation, the civil war and the Cultural Revolution, their friendship is tested by a woman, Juxian, and by the political upheavals. Chen Kaige's 1993 film shared the Palme d'Or at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take
