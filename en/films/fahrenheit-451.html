Sinekatalog’s take

François Truffaut's adaptation of Bradbury's dystopia is a quiet, strange, colourful fable of book-burning. Oskar Werner and Julie Christie are fine, and the film's stylised calm is chilling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
