Plot

At a remote Foreign Legion outpost in Djibouti, the former sergeant Galoup remembers how his orderly world was disturbed by the arrival of a handsome new recruit, Sentain, who is admired by the men and the commander. His jealousy grows and leads him to act. Claire Denis's 1999 film, loosely based on Melville's Billy Budd, is a sensual, almost wordless study of masculinity, and ends with a famous dance scene.

Sinekatalog’s take

Claire Denis's Foreign Legion drama is a poetic, sensual meditation on jealousy and masculinity. Denis Lavant's final dance is astonishing, and the film's images and music are hypnotic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
