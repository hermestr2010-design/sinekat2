Robert'in Filmi

1991 · Directed by Canan Gerede
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Patrick Bauchau, Aslı Altan, Menderes Samancılar, Sinan Çetin, Meral Çetinkaya

Plot

Her first fiction feature, selected for Critics' Week at Cannes in 1991, which won the best screenplay prize at the Marseille International Women's Film Festival. English title: Robert's Movie.

Sinekatalog’s take

Canan Gerede's first fiction feature was selected for Critics' Week at Cannes, and follows a film-maker's trip with Patrick Bauchau. An early, thoughtful piece by a rare woman director in Turkish cinema of its time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
