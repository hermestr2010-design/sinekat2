Plot

A mathematician with a failing heart, a grieving mother and an ex-convict are connected by a hit-and-run accident.

Sinekatalog’s take

Iñárritu shuffles the timeline of a tragic accident into a mosaic of grief and guilt. Sean Penn, Naomi Watts and Benicio del Toro give raw performances in a film that is relentlessly heavy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
