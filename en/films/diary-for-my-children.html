Plot

In postwar Hungary, a teenage girl, Juli, returns from the Soviet Union where she spent her childhood, to live with a grandmother in Budapest, as the Stalinist regime tightens. She forms a bond with a Party official, a woman who represents her mother's lost generation. Márta Mészáros's 1984 film, drawn from her own life, won the Grand Prix at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Márta Mészáros's semi-autobiographical drama follows a teenage girl returning to Stalinist Hungary from the USSR. Zsuzsa Czinkóczi is superb in a restrained, moving and politically sharp coming-of-age tale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
