Cast

Plot

In a hospital in Beirut, Nahila, a Palestinian nurse, tells a comatose freedom fighter, Yunes, the story of their life and love, from the 1948 exodus to the refugee camps in Lebanon and the war. Yousry Nasrallah's 2004 two-part epic, based on the novel by Elias Khoury, is a long, tender portrait of Palestinian memory and exile.
