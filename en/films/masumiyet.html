Plot

A drama of obsessive love directed by Zeki Demirkubuz, shown at the Venice Film Festival, which won him the Golden Boll for best director at the Adana festival. The title means "Innocence". The film follows a group of marginal characters whose lives are tied to a prison and a hotel in a provincial town, and is a landmark of Turkish independent cinema.

Sinekatalog’s take

Zeki Demirkubuz's drama of obsessive love among society's margins is raw, dark and powerful. Güven Kıraç and Derya Alabora are strong, and this landmark of Turkish cinema is austere and unforgettable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
