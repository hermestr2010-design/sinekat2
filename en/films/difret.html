Plot

Meaza Ashenafi, a young Ethiopian lawyer, defends a fourteen-year-old girl, Hirut, who killed the man who abducted her to force her into marriage, a traditional practice. The case pits customary law against the state's legal system, and Meaza faces intimidation and opposition. Zeresenay Berhane Mehari's 2014 film, based on a true story, won the Audience Award at Sundance.
