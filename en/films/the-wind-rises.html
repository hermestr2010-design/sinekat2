Sinekatalog’s take

Miyazaki's fictionalised life of aircraft designer Jiro Horikoshi is a wistful look at beauty and its use for war. Elegant, adult and bittersweet, it was announced as his last film at the time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
