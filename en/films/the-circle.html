Plot

The day-to-day struggles of several women in Tehran are linked in a circular story. It won the Golden Lion at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jafar Panahi's Golden Lion winner links several women in Tehran in a circular, cruel day. Tense, angry and formally brilliant, it exposes the daily constraints on Iranian women with devastating clarity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
