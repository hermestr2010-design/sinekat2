Plot

A child murderer terrorises Berlin, and both the police and the city's criminal underworld hunt for him. It was Lang's first sound film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Fritz Lang's early sound thriller follows a child murderer hunted by both police and the city's criminals. Peter Lorre's trembling final speech adds moral complexity to the manhunt. A founding work of the serial-killer film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
