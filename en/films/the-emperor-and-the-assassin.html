Plot

In the third century BC the king of Qin plans to unify China by force. A woman schemes to send an assassin against him, and the story questions the cost of power and conquest.

Sinekatalog’s take

Chen Kaige's epic follows the Qin king's conquest and a plot to assassinate him. Sumptuous and thoughtful, with Gong Li and Zhang Fengyi, it questions tyranny and the price of unification amid grand spectacle.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
