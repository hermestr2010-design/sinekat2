Cast

Plot

In 1960s Ireland, three young women, Margaret, Bernadette and Rose, are sent to the Magdalene laundries, institutions run by the Catholic church, where they are subjected to years of forced labour, humiliation and abuse by the nuns. Peter Mullan's 2002 film, based on real accounts, with a cast including Geraldine McEwan, won the Golden Lion at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take
