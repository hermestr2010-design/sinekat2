Sinekatalog’s take

Samuel Fuller's low-budget Korean War film follows a hardened sergeant and a stranded patrol. Brutal, spare and cynical, it was the first film of the war and is a tough, influential classic of the genre.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
