Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph L. Mankiewicz's Shakespeare adaptation is clear, handsome and strongly acted. Marlon Brando's Mark Antony and James Mason's Brutus impress, and John Gielgud's Cassius is superb in a polished, black-and-white classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
