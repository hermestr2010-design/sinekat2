Sinekatalog’s take

Naomi Kawase's romance pairs a woman who writes audio descriptions for films with a photographer going blind. Tender and sensory, with beautiful light images, it is a delicate if sentimental meditation on seeing and loss.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
