Plot

On the eve of her second wedding, the Philadelphia socialite Tracy Lord is visited by her ex-husband, C.K. Dexter Haven, and by a magazine reporter and photographer sent to cover the event. Among champagne and flirtation, Tracy's cool pride is tested, and she has to decide whom she really loves. George Cukor's 1940 comedy, with Katharine Hepburn, Cary Grant and James Stewart, was a vehicle that revived Hepburn's career, and it won Stewart the Academy Award for Best Actor.
