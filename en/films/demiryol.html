Plot

A Turkish drama directed by Yavuz Özkan, released in 1979. It won the Golden Orange when the awards for 1979 were finally presented in 2011, after the military coup of 1980 had led to the cancellation of the festival that year. The film belongs to the politically engaged cinema of the late 1970s, and is remembered as an important work of that period.
