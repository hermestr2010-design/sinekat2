Plot

In eighteenth-century South America, a Jesuit missionary and a repentant slave trader try to protect a Guaraní community from colonial powers.

Sinekatalog’s take

Roland Joffé's historical drama pairs Jeremy Irons and Robert De Niro in a story of faith and colonial betrayal. Chris Menges's jungle photography and Ennio Morricone's score are stunning, and the film is moving and earnest.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
