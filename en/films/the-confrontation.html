Sinekatalog’s take

Miklós Jancsó's film stages a clash between student groups of different political leanings, told through songs and ritual-like choreography. Formal and rhythmic, it is an energetic, ironic political musical of 1960s Hungary.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
