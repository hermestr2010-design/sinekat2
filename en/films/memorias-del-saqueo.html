Sinekatalog’s take

Fernando Solanas's documentary traces how Argentina's economy collapsed in 2001, naming the banks, politicians and policies he holds responsible. It is angry, forceful and one-sided on purpose, and it works as both a record and a call to action.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
