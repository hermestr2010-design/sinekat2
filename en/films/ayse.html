Plot

A 2024 drama by Necmi Sancak, his first feature, which won him the Golden Orange for best director and the Directors' Guild best debut prize. Built around a woman named Ayşe, it is a quiet, closely observed study of a life under pressure, and was one of the more notable Turkish debut films of its year.

Sinekatalog’s take

Necmi Sancak's quiet, closely observed debut follows a woman named Ayşe through a life under pressure. Binnur Kaya is superb in a restrained drama that won the Golden Orange for best director and the best debut prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
