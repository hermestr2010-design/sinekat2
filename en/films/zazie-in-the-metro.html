Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Louis Malle's anarchic comedy follows a foul-mouthed girl loose in Paris, bent on riding the closed metro. Zany, fast and full of cinematic gags, it is a hyperactive, freewheeling charm.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
