Cast

Jean Gabin, Bernard Blier, Bourvil

Plot

Jean Valjean, an ex-convict imprisoned for stealing bread, is changed by an act of mercy from a bishop and builds a new life as a respected factory owner and mayor. The relentless Inspector Javert, who knows his past, pursues him through years of struggle, and Valjean cares for the orphaned Cosette. Jean-Paul Le Chanois's 1958 French adaptation of Victor Hugo's novel stars Jean Gabin as Valjean and Bourvil as the villainous Thénardier, and is a faithful rendering of the classic.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Paul Le Chanois's lengthy French version of Hugo follows Jean Valjean, redeemed by mercy and pursued by Javert. Jean Gabin and Bernard Blier are superb in a sturdy, humane and affecting retelling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
