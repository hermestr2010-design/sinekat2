Plot

A peasant woman persuades her village to form a farming cooperative, and the film celebrates modern machinery and collective farming.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sergei Eisenstein's silent film follows a peasant woman persuading her village to form a collective and embrace modern machinery. Visually bold and propagandistic, with a famous cream separator sequence, it is an exuberant if uneven Soviet experiment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
