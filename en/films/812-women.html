Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Greenaway's comedy has a Geneva millionaire and his son build a private harem after the mother's death. Visually stylised and sardonic, with Greenaway's usual art-house trappings, it is a cold, divisive and mostly forgotten satire.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
