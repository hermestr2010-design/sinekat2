Sinekatalog’s take

Federico Fellini's mockumentary shows a TV crew filming an orchestra that rebels against its conductor. Funny and allegorical, with Nino Rota's music, it is a short, sharp parable of anarchy and authority.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
