Sinekatalog’s take

Yılmaz Güney's prison drama, made in French exile, follows children in a Turkish jail facing brutality and abuse. Raw, angry and uncompromising, with Tuncel Kurtiz, it is a harrowing indictment of authoritarian violence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
