Plot

A man slowly drives his wife insane by manipulating her surroundings, so as to find the jewels hidden in her house.

Sinekatalog’s take

George Cukor's thriller has Charles Boyer slowly driving his wife, Ingrid Bergman, to doubt her own sanity. The title became a term for psychological manipulation, and Bergman won an Oscar.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
