Plot

Recently dead people spend a week in a way station where they must choose a single memory to take with them into eternity.

Sinekatalog’s take

Hirokazu Kore-eda's gentle fantasy has the newly dead choosing one memory to keep forever. Quiet, simple and deeply moving, with documentary-style interviews, it is a luminous meditation on life's small joys.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
