Plot

Two linked stories, one in a rural hospital and one in a modern city hospital, echo each other. It was inspired by the director's parents.

Sinekatalog’s take

Apichatpong Weerasethakul's two-part film mirrors a rural hospital and a modern city one, inspired by his parents. Gentle, dreamlike and mysterious, with a hypnotic repetition, it is a lyrical meditation on memory and change.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
