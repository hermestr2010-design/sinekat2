Plot

A documentary of everyday Soviet life shot candidly, made as a manifesto for Vertov's idea of the camera eye.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dziga Vertov's silent experiment records everyday Soviet life in candid, quick-cut glimpses as a manifesto for the camera's eye. Playful and radical, with trick effects, it is a landmark of early documentary and film theory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
