Sinekatalog’s take

Alexander Sokurov's graduation film, adapted from Platonov, was never officially released but became a landmark of his early style. Slow, poetic and heavy with feeling, it already shows his gift for turning stillness into drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
