Plot

A businessman of the new African elite is struck impotent on his wedding night to a third wife, and traces the curse to a beggar he has wronged.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ousmane Sembène's satire follows a Senegalese businessman struck impotent on his third wedding night and tracing the curse to a beggar he wronged. Biting and funny, it is a classic of African cinema and a sharp attack on the new elite.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
