Plot

His only English-language film, shot in Ireland, which won the Grand Prix at Cannes. Known in French as Les Années-lumière.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Tanner's only English-language film, shot in Ireland, pairs a young man with an old eccentric who believes he can fly. Trevor Howard is superb in a fable-like, wry and haunting tale that won the Cannes Grand Prix.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
