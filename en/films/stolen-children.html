Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Gianni Amelio's drama follows a young carabiniere escorting two abused children across Italy to an orphanage. Enrico Lo Verso is superb in a quiet, compassionate and unsentimental road film that won Cannes' Grand Jury Prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
