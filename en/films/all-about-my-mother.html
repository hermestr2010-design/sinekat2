Plot

After her teenage son Esteban is killed in a car accident, Manuela, a nurse in Madrid, travels to Barcelona to find the boy's father, who doesn't know of his existence. She joins a circle of women, among them a transgender friend, a pregnant nun and an actress. Pedro Almodóvar's 1999 film won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Almodóvar's melodrama follows a nurse who loses her son and seeks his father in Barcelona. Warm, colourful and tribute-filled, it celebrates women, actors and solidarity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
