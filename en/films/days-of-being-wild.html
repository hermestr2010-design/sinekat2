Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Wong Kar-wai's second film is a moody Hong Kong tale of a restless playboy and the women he hurts. Leslie Cheung is magnetic, and it first showed the lush style of Doyle's camera.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
