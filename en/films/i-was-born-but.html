Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Yasujiro Ozu's silent comedy shows two boys shamed by their father's meek office behaviour. Funny, gentle and quietly sad, with a clear-eyed view of social hierarchy, it is a delightful, moving classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
