Sinekatalog’s take

Pier Paolo Pasolini's stark, ritual retelling of the Greek myth stars Maria Callas in her only film. Raw and hypnotic, with desert landscapes and barbaric detail, it is a strange, striking study of cultural collision.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
