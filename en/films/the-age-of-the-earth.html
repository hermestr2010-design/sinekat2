Sinekatalog’s take

Glauber Rocha's final, chaotic film is a sprawling manifesto of Brazilian myth, politics and religion, with four Christs and a stream of images. Wild, loud and wilfully incoherent, it is a difficult but fascinating last testament.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
