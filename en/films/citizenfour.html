Plot

In 2013, the filmmaker Laura Poitras and the journalists Glenn Greenwald and Ewan MacAskill meet the NSA contractor Edward Snowden in a Hong Kong hotel room, where he reveals the extent of mass surveillance by US intelligence. Poitras's 2014 documentary records the conversations as they happen and the fallout, and won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature.
