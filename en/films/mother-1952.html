Plot

A mother holds her family together in postwar Japan. Known in Japanese as Okaasan.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mikio Naruse's quiet drama follows a postwar Japanese family kept together by a mother who works at a laundry. Kinuyo Tanaka is superb in a modest, tender and honest portrait of everyday sacrifice.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
