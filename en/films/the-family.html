Plot

The life of an Italian family from 1906 to the 1980s, told entirely inside one Roman apartment. It was nominated for an Oscar.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ettore Scola's drama follows an Italian family from 1906 to the 1980s without ever leaving their Rome apartment. Vittorio Gassman anchors a moving, theatrical and quietly clever chronicle of private change.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
