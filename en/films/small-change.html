Sinekatalog’s take

François Truffaut's affectionate portrait of schoolchildren in a small town is full of comic, tender episodes. Warm and wise, with natural child performances and a famous window-fall scene, it celebrates resilience in youth.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
