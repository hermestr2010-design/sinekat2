Plot

An old railway signalman at a remote crossing in Iran lives a life of silent routine, with his wife, going through the same chores every day until he is retired. The long, static shots and sparse dialogue emphasise the repetitiveness of work and the passing of time. Sohrab Shahid-Saless's 1974 film, which won the Silver Bear in Berlin, is a spare, austere masterpiece of the Iranian New Wave.
