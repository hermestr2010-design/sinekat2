Sinekatalog’s take

Federico Fellini's collage of memories and visions of Rome is spectacular, anecdotal and self-indulgent. The ecclesiastical fashion show and Anna Magnani's cameo are highlights of a rambling love letter to the city.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
