Norte, the End of History

Original title: Norte, Hangganan ng Kasaysayan · Turkish title: Norte, Tarihin Sonu

“Unravelling at the speed of life.”

2013 · 4 h 10 min · Directed by Lav Diaz
CrimeDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Sid Lucero, Angeli Bayani, Archie Alemania

Plot

Fabian, a law student who has abandoned his studies, kills a moneylender in the Philippines. Another man, Joaquin, a poor family man, is imprisoned for the crime, and his wife and children struggle to live. The film follows both men in a meditation on guilt and justice, inspired by Dostoevsky's Crime and Punishment. Lav Diaz's 2013 film runs for about four hours.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lav Diaz's long drama, inspired by Crime and Punishment, follows a Filipino law student who kills and an innocent man jailed for it. Patient and powerful, it is a profound, bleak meditation on guilt and injustice.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
