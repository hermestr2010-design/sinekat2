Sinekatalog’s take

Idrissa Ouédraogo's first feature follows a family forced to leave its village by drought and famine in the Sahel. Spare and dignified, with long takes and real faces, it is a powerful, humane debut about survival.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
