Sinekatalog’s take

Veljko Bulajić's Partisan epic follows Yugoslav fighters and civilians in the brutal 1942 Kozara offensive. Big, loud and earnest, with vivid battle scenes, it is a classic of the Yugoslav war film genre, if heavy on heroics.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
