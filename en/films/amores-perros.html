Sinekatalog’s take

Three stories connected by a car crash in Mexico City, with dogs as a running motif. Iñárritu's debut is raw, violent and emotionally intense, and it launched a wave of Mexican cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
