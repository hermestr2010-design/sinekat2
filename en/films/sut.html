Plot

A young Yusuf, devoted to poetry and to his widowed mother, who sells milk, finds his life shaken when she falls in love. English title: Milk.

Sinekatalog’s take

Semih Kaplanoğlu's quiet second chapter of his Yusuf trilogy follows a poet-minded young man whose widowed milk-selling mother finds love. Slow, observant and lovely to look at, it is a tender study of loneliness and growing up.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
