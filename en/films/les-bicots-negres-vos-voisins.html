Sinekatalog’s take

Med Hondo's film follows immigrant workers in France, mixing drama, sketch and agitation to expose racism and exploitation. Angry and inventive on a tiny budget, it is a pioneering work of immigrant cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
