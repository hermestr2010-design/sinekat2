Black Friday

“The shocking truth behind the '93 Bombay blasts”

2004 · 2 h 23 min · Directed by Anurag Kashyap
ActionCrimeDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Kay Kay Menon, Pawan Malhotra, Aditya Srivastava

Plot

Based on Hussain Zaidi's book about the 1993 Bombay bombings. Its release was held up for about two years.

Sinekatalog’s take

Anurag Kashyap's docudrama traces the 1993 Mumbai bombings from the police investigation side. Grainy, fast and unsparing, it is a landmark of Indian realist cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
