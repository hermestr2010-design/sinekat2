Plot

A driver takes the blame for his employer's hit-and-run in return for money, and his family begins to unravel. Ceylan won the Best Director prize at Cannes. English title: Three Monkeys.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nuri Bilge Ceylan's bleak family drama of a driver taking the fall for his boss is atmospheric and slow. Its storm-lit images and quiet dread won Cannes praise, and the film is haunting.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
