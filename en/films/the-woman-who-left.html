Cast

Plot

After thirty years in prison for a crime she did not commit, a teacher is released and sets out to take revenge on the man who framed her.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lav Diaz's long black-and-white drama follows a teacher released after thirty years for a crime she didn't commit and set on revenge. Charo Santos-Concio is superb in a slow, haunting and humane Golden Lion winner.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
