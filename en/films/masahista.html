Cast

Plot

The first of his seven films with the actor Coco Martin. Known in English as The Masseur.

Sinekatalog’s take

Brillante Mendoza's early drama follows a young Manila masseur in a sauna, his work, his father's death and a world of male sex work. Coco Martin is raw in a candid, lowlit and observational film that won Locarno's top prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
