Plot

In a Taipei district struck by a mysterious epidemic, two neighbours are brought together by a hole between their floors. The film is interleaved with musical numbers.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tsai Ming-liang's strange musical drama connects two neighbours through a hole in a Taipei flat as a plague spreads. Wet, bleak and playful, with song numbers, it is a haunting millennial parable of connection.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
