Sinekatalog’s take

Alice Guy-Blaché's 1906 comic short reverses gender roles, with men doing the housework while women take charge. Cheeky and ahead of its time, it is a witty, playful early satire from a pioneering female director.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
