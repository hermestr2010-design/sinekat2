Plot

A student sells his reflection to a sorcerer in exchange for money, and his double begins to destroy his life. Directed by Stellan Rye, with Wegener as star and co-writer.

Sinekatalog’s take

Paul Wegener's 1913 silent fantasy has a student selling his reflection to a sorcerer, whose double then ruins his life. Eerie and groundbreaking, it is a key early work of German horror and doppelgänger tales.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
