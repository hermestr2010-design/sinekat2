Plot

A young man returns to his home village and finds his old friend trapped in alcoholism. It is often called the first film of the New Wave.

Sinekatalog’s take

Claude Chabrol's debut, often called the first New Wave film, follows a man returning to his village to find his friend ruined by drink. Gérard Blain and Jean-Claude Brialy are strong in a raw, sincere provincial drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
