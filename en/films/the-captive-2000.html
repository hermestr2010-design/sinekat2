Sinekatalog’s take

Chantal Akerman's loose adaptation of Proust follows a jealous man who obsessively watches and controls his girlfriend. Cool, fixated and claustrophobic, with Stanislas Merhar and Sylvie Testud, it is a chilly study of possession.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
