Sinekatalog’s take

Bertrand Tavernier's political comedy follows a young speechwriter in the French foreign ministry serving a flamboyant minister. Quick, witty and affectionate, with Thierry Lhermitte and Niels Arestrup, it is a lively satire of power and rhetoric.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
