Plot

In Naples a property developer who is also a councillor profits from a building collapse. The film shows how corruption works within the legal system.

Sinekatalog’s take

Francesco Rosi's political thriller shows a Naples developer-councillor exploiting a building collapse. Rod Steiger is blistering, and the cold, documentary-like exposure of legal corruption makes a tough, vital film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
