Sinekatalog’s take

Pier Paolo Pasolini's second Trilogy of Life film turns Chaucer's bawdy tales into rough, comic sketches. Earthy and uneven, with Ninetto Davoli as a Chaplinesque thief, it is a ribald, lively but patchy romp.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
