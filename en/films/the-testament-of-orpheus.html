Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Cocteau's farewell film wanders through time with the poet as his own subject, with cameos from Picasso and others. Dreamlike, playful and self-referential, it is a charming, eccentric artistic testament.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
