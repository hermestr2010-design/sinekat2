Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jan Troell's sequel follows the Swedish emigrants settling in Minnesota amid hardship, war and the Dakota conflict. Max von Sydow and Liv Ullmann are again superb in a patient, moving continuation of The Emigrants.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
