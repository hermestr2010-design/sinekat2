Plot

Based on a script by Akira Kurosawa, two escaped convicts are trapped on a runaway locomotive in an Alaskan winter.

Sinekatalog’s take

Andrei Konchalovsky's thriller strands two convicts on a brakeless locomotive through the Alaskan snow. Jon Voight and Eric Roberts are intense, and the film is relentless, bleak and thrilling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
