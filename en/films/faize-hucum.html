Sinekatalog’s take

Zeki Ökten's social comedy, a Golden Orange winner for best director, targets loan sharks and the people they trap in debt. Sharp, funny and warm, it is one of the more satisfying Turkish comedies of its time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
