The Chronicle of Anna Magdalena Bach

Original title: Chronik der Anna Magdalena Bach
1968 · 1 h 34 min · Directed by Jean-Marie Straub
BiographyDramaHistory
Watch trailer

Cast

Gustav Leonhardt, Christiane Lang

Plot

The film presents the life of Johann Sebastian Bach, as told by his second wife, Anna Magdalena, through her voice, a few documents, and the performance of Bach's music by the musicians, filmed in period costume and in real locations. Jean-Marie Straub and Danièle Huillet's 1968 first feature is austere and unembellished, and is notable for the music being played live on film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Marie Straub and Danièle Huillet's austere film presents Bach's life through his wife's voice, documents and full performances in period dress. Rigorous and static, it is a demanding, reverent and beautiful film of music.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
