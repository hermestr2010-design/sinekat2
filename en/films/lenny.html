Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Bob Fosse's black-and-white biopic of Lenny Bruce is raw and stylish. Dustin Hoffman gives a driven, fearless performance, and Valerie Perrine is excellent in a bitter portrait of free speech and self-destruction.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
