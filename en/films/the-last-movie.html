Plot

A stuntman stays in a Peruvian village after a western is shot there, and the villagers start to re-enact the film for real.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dennis Hopper's follow-up to Easy Rider has a stuntman staying in a Peruvian village where locals mimic a film shoot. Chaotic, ambitious and fragmented, it is a bold, famously messy art-film experiment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
