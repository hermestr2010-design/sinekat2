Plot

Mona, an African-American fashion model on a photo shoot at a Ghanaian slave fort, is transported back in time to a plantation in the Americas, where she becomes Shola, a slave. She experiences the brutality of slavery and sees the beginnings of resistance. Haile Gerima's 1993 film, a landmark of independent Black cinema, takes its title from a West African word meaning to look back in order to move forward.
