Plot

A drama directed by Tolga Karaçelik, known in English as Ivy, which won him the Golden Orange for best director in 2015. Set on a freighter stranded at sea, a crew faces boredom, tension and a sense of absurdity as they wait for months without pay. The film is a darkly comic, atmospheric portrait of a group of men under strain.
