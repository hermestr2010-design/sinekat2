Mohammed Lakhdar-Hamina follows a mother searching for her son who was arrested by the French army during the Algerian war. Simple, stark and heartbreaking, with Keltoum unforgettable, it won the Cannes prize for best first work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
