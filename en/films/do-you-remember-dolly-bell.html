Plot

A teenage boy in Sarajevo in the early 1960s dreams of being a musician and falls for a young woman working for a local pimp.

Sinekatalog’s take

Emir Kusturica's debut follows a Sarajevo teenager dreaming of music in the early 1960s. Warm, funny and nostalgic, with a rich sense of family and place, it won the Golden Lion for best first work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
