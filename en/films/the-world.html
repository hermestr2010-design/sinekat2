Sinekatalog’s take

Jia Zhangke's drama follows young migrant workers at a Beijing theme park of miniature world landmarks. Zhao Tao is superb in a wry, sad film about globalisation, with the fake skylines symbolising unreachable dreams.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
