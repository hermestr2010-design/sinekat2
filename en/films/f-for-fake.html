Plot

An essay film about forgery and deception, built around the art forger Elmyr de Hory and Clifford Irving, who wrote a fake biography of Howard Hughes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's playful essay film about forgery and truth is a dazzling cut-and-paste magic trick. Witty, mischievous and endlessly inventive, it remains a favourite of film lovers.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
