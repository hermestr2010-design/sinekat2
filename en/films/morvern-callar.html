Sinekatalog’s take

Lynne Ramsay's drifting drama follows a woman who passes off her dead boyfriend's novel as her own. Samantha Morton is mesmerising, and the film's sensory soundtrack and images make it a hypnotic, elliptical study of grief.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
