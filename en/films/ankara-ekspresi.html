Plot

A spy drama adapted from the novel of the same name by Esat Mahmut Karakurt. Ediz Hun plays the Turkish Major Seyfi Bey, who goes on a mission aboard the train, where he confronts the German spy Hilda, played by Filiz Akın. Muzaffer Arslan's 1970 film, known in English as Ankara Express, is a classic of Turkish popular cinema.

Sinekatalog’s take

Muzaffer Arslan's spy adventure, from Esat Mahmut Karakurt's novel, has Ediz Hun's Turkish major confront Filiz Akın's German agent aboard a train. Cheerfully pulpy and dated, it is a popular Yeşilçam thriller with a lively cast.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
