Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Hiroshi Inagaki's first Musashi film turns a wild young fighter into a disciplined swordsman. Toshiro Mifune is magnetic, and the film's colourful action and spiritual themes launched a popular trilogy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
