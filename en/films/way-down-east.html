Way Down East

“A simple story for plain people.”

1920 · 2 h 25 min · Directed by D.W. Griffith
DramaRomance

Cast

Lillian Gish, Richard Barthelmess, Lowell Sherman

Credits

Written by: Lottie Blair Parker

Plot

A young woman tricked into a sham marriage is cast out when her secret is revealed. The climax takes place on a river of breaking ice.

Sinekatalog’s take

D.W. Griffith's silent melodrama sees Lillian Gish cast out after a sham marriage and rescued on a breaking ice floe. Stagey and moralistic, yet gripping, its famous ice sequence still thrills.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
