Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Ousmane Sembène's film shows a West African kingdom torn when outsiders resisting forced conversion to Islam kidnap a princess. Stately and stylised, with striking costumes, it is a sharp and banned-for-years allegory of religion and power.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
