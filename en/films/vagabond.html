Plot

The last weeks of a young drifter in winter in southern France, pieced together from the people she met.

Sinekatalog’s take

Agnès Varda's portrait of a young drifter's last winter is built from the testimony of those who met her. Sandrine Bonnaire is remarkable, and the film is cold, honest and unforgettable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
