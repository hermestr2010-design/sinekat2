Plot

In Poland in 1946, among the ruins of a war that has devastated the country, Emilia, a widow who lives with her mother, meets an American soldier, Norman. Despite not speaking each other's language, they fall in love, but circumstances and the new political order make their relationship difficult. Krzysztof Zanussi's 1984 film is a restrained, spiritual love story that won the Golden Lion in Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take
