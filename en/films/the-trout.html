Plot

A group of executives of a Spanish firm gather at a country lodge to discuss business, and their meeting soon descends into mutual accusations, intrigue and absurdity. The title refers to a fish, a symbol of what they are chasing. José Luis García Sánchez's 1978 satire, which won a prize at the San Sebastián festival, mocks the corruption and pretensions of the Spanish business class in the last years of Franco's rule.
