Sinekatalog’s take

Ettore Scola's tragicomedy follows a love triangle in Rome among a bricklayer, a florist and a pizza maker. Marcello Mastroianni, Monica Vitti and Giancarlo Giannini are brilliant in a sprightly, bittersweet film that slides from farce to heartbreak.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
