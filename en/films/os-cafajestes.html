Sinekatalog’s take

Ruy Guerra's early Cinema Novo film follows two idle men who plan to blackmail a rich man by photographing his mistress in the nude. Cool, sharp and sensual, with famous nudity, it is a stylish, cynical and daring study of class and decadence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
