Plot

A young undertaker's assistant marries the hangman's daughter and is induced to take the hangman's job in order to get an apartment.

Sinekatalog’s take

Luis García Berlanga's black comedy has a young man marrying into the hangman's family to get an apartment. Biting and hilarious, with Nino Manfredi superb, it is a landmark of Spanish satire on capital punishment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
