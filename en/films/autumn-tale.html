Sinekatalog’s take

Eric Rohmer's gentle comedy sees two friends scheming to find a husband for a middle-aged widow. Warm, talky and wise, with Marie Rivière and Béatrice Romand, it is a sunlit, subtle Tale of the Four Seasons finale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
