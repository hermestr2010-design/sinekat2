Plot

A young couple flee across the American South while pursued by figures sent by the woman's mother. It won the Palme d'Or.

Sinekatalog’s take

David Lynch's Palme d'Or winner is a violent, sexy road movie full of Oz references and Elvis swagger. Cage and Dern burn up the screen, though the film's excess makes it a love-or-hate experience.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
