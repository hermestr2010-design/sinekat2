Plot

Mitsuha, a girl in a rural town, and Taki, a boy in Tokyo, find themselves switching bodies in their dreams. As they communicate through notes and phone messages, they begin to fall for each other, until the swaps stop. They try to find each other, and a link to an approaching comet. Makoto Shinkai's 2016 animation became the highest-grossing anime film of all time by 2019.

Sinekatalog’s take

Two teenagers swap bodies across distance and time. Makoto Shinkai's luminous backgrounds and a late twist make it equal parts romance and disaster story; it became one of the biggest anime hits ever.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
