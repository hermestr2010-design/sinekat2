Plot

A beauty queen who is chosen as a bride for a millionaire and a Dutch captain who sails a candy-filled barge are presented in an outrageous satire of capitalism and communism.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dušan Makavejev's outrageous satire links a beauty queen's marriage and a candy-laden barge in a shocking assault on both capitalism and communism. Provocative, grotesque and gleefully transgressive, it remains a divisive cult object.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
