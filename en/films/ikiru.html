Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

A dying bureaucrat searches for meaning and decides to build a small playground. Kurosawa's humane drama, with Takashi Shimura's quiet performance, asks what a life is worth. Moving without being maudlin.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
