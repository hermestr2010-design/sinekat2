Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Konkona Sen Sharma, Rahul Bose

Plot

A drama of a Hindu Brahmin woman and a Muslim photographer thrown together on a bus journey during communal riots. It won the National Award for best direction and screenplay.

Sinekatalog’s take

Aparna Sen's drama throws a Hindu Brahmin woman and a Muslim photographer together on a bus during communal riots. Tender and tense, with Konkona Sen Sharma and Rahul Bose superb, it is a humane plea for tolerance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
