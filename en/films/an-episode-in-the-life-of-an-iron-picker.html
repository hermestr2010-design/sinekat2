Plot

A Roma man in rural Bosnia, who collects scrap metal, tries to get a hospital to treat his wife, who has had a miscarriage, but she lacks health insurance.

Sinekatalog’s take

Danis Tanović's docudrama re-enacts a true event with the real Roma family, as a scrap-metal collector fights to get surgery for his wife. Simple and angry, it is a quiet, humane indictment of systemic indifference.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
