Sinekatalog’s take

Steve McQueen's debut dramatises Bobby Sands's hunger strike with painterly, unflinching images. Michael Fassbender is extraordinary, and the film's long central conversation is a tour de force.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
