Sinekatalog’s take

Aleksandar Petrović's film is a surreal parable of a Serbian village's life, its misfits and wartime backdrop, with a farmer, a girl and the apocalypse in the air. Poetic, comic and bleak, it is a vivid, strange allegory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
