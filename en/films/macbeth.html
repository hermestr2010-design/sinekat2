Cast

Plot

Shakespeare's tragedy of a Scottish lord who, driven by a prophecy and his wife's ambition, murders his king. It was made quickly and on a small budget.

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's low-budget Shakespeare is a stylised, expressionist fever dream shot in cramped sets. Rough yet atmospheric and bold, with Welles's driven Macbeth, it is a striking if flawed adaptation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
