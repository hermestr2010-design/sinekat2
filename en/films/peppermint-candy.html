Plot

A man stands on a railway track, and the story of the twenty years that led him there is told in reverse, back through Korea's modern history.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lee Chang-dong's drama is told in reverse, tracing a broken man back through twenty years of Korean history. Sol Kyung-gu is superb, and the film's structure makes its heartbreak and cruelty hit all the harder.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
