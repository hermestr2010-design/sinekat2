Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Matteo Garrone's fairy-tale anthology is lush, dark and grotesque, with sumptuous locations and strong performances. Its stories feel uneven, but the images are dazzling and strange.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
