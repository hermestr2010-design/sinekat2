Plot

His directing debut, which won the Japan Academy awards for best picture, best director and best screenplay. Known in Japanese as Ososhiki.

Sinekatalog’s take

Juzo Itami's debut comedy follows a family stumbling through the rituals of a patriarch's funeral. Sharp, funny and tender, it won major Japan Academy awards and launched Itami's career of satirical social comedies.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
