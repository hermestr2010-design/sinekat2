Time Regained

1999 · Directed by Raúl Ruiz
DramaHistory
Watch trailer

Cast

Catherine Deneuve, Emmanuelle Béart, Vincent Perez, Pascal Greggory, John Malkovich, Marie-France Pisier

Plot

As he lies in bed in his old age, the writer Marcel recalls his life in the salons and society of late-nineteenth-century and early twentieth-century Paris, from childhood to the First World War. The characters of the aristocracy and the demi-monde come and go as memory shifts. Raúl Ruiz's 1999 film, an adaptation of the last volume of Proust's In Search of Lost Time, with Catherine Deneuve, Emmanuelle Béart and John Malkovich, is a sumptuous essay in memory.
