Cast

Ahmet Özel, Hakan Emre Ünal, Mücahit Koçak, Ozan Dağara, Elif Aydın

Plot

A dam project forces the evacuation of a village. Ahmet, a recluse, refuses to leave his home, and gradually becomes isolated as the water rises, in a story about attachment to place and memory. Ali Özel's 2019 film, whose title means "Steppe", is a quiet, visually striking drama of loss and endurance.
