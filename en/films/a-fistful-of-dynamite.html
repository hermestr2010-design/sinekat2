Cast

Rod Steiger, James Coburn, Romolo Valli

Plot

During the Mexican Revolution a Mexican bandit and an Irish explosives expert join forces and are drawn into the fighting. It is also known as Duck, You Sucker!

Sinekatalog’s take

Sergio Leone's Mexican Revolution western pairs a bandit, Rod Steiger, with an Irish explosives expert, James Coburn, as they stumble into rebellion. Epic, funny and elegiac, with Morricone's score, it is a grand, underrated Leone work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
