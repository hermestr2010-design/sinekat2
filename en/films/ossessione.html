Plot

A drifter and a married woman plot to kill her husband. It is an unofficial adaptation of The Postman Always Rings Twice and is often called the first neorealist film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Luchino Visconti's debut, an unofficial Postman Always Rings Twice, is a gritty, sensual precursor to neorealism. Banned by fascist authorities, it shows Italian life with harsh honesty and passion.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
