Sinekatalog’s take

Ousmane Sembène's drama shows Diola villagers in 1940s Senegal resisting French demands for rice and recruits, led by the women. Austere and fierce, it is a powerful, anticolonial work of African cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
