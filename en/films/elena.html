Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Andrey Zvyagintsev's cold drama about a nurse-turned-wife torn between her husband and her son is moral and tense. Nadezhda Markina is superb, with Philip Glass's music adding dread.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
