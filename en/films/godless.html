Godless

2016 · Directed by Ralitza Petrova
DramaCrime
Watch trailer

Cast

Irena Ivanova, Ivan Savov, Ivan Barnev

Plot

Gana, a care nurse in a Bulgarian town, sells the identity papers of the elderly people she looks after to a gang of criminals, to fund her morphine addiction. When an old man dies and she meets a young medical student, her life begins to unravel. Ralitza Petrova's 2016 film, with Irena Ivanova, is a bleak portrait of moral desolation, and won the Golden Leopard at Locarno.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ralitza Petrova's drama follows a Bulgarian care nurse who sells the identity papers of her elderly patients to fund her addiction, until a death and a young student change things.
