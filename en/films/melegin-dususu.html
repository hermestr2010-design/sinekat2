Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Semih Kaplanoğlu's early drama follows a woman and the city in a stark, minimal and often bleak style. Sparse and slow, it lacks the later warmth of his Yusuf trilogy and was received with mixed reactions.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
