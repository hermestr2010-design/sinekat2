Sinekatalog’s take

Bernardo Bertolucci's experimental film, after Dostoevsky's The Double, follows a young man haunted by an alter ego. Pierre Clémenti is feverish in a Godard-tinged, restless and stylised exercise that remains more curiosity than success.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
