Plot

A nine-year-old boy in Belfast in 1969 grows up as sectarian violence breaks out in his working-class street. Shot in black and white.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kenneth Branagh's black-and-white memoir of a boy in 1969 Belfast is warm, funny and nostalgic. It sees the Troubles through a child's eyes, and Ciarán Hinds and Judi Dench are delightful.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
