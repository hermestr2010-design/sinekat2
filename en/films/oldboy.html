Plot

Oh Dae-su, a drunken businessman, is kidnapped and imprisoned in a small room for fifteen years without explanation. When he is suddenly released, he is given five days to find out who imprisoned him and why, and takes revenge. Park Chan-wook's 2003 film, with Choi Min-sik, is known for its ferocity, its hallway fight scene and its shocking twist, and won the Grand Prix at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Park Chan-wook's revenge thriller imprisons a man for fifteen years and then releases him with a puzzle. The corridor fight is famous, but the twist and operatic melodrama are what endure.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
