Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

George Cukor's classic adaptation brings the March sisters to life with Katharine Hepburn as a spirited Jo. Warm, sentimental and beautifully acted, it remains a cosy, much-loved version of Alcott's novel.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
