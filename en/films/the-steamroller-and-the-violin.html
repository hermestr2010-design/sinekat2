Sinekatalog’s take

Andrei Tarkovsky's graduation short follows a small violinist and a friendly road worker. Tender and visually inventive, with Soviet colour and hints of the master to come, it is a charming early gem.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
