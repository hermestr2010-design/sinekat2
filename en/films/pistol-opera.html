Sinekatalog’s take

Seijun Suzuki's colourful late film follows a female assassin who wants to be number one in a guild of killers. Dazzling, theatrical and willfully odd, it is a surreal, stylised and playful riff on his Branded to Kill.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
