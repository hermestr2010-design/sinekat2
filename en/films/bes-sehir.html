Plot

A 2010 feature directed and written by Onur Ünlü, whose title means "Five Cities". The film mixes dark humour with a story of lost people on the road and in the cities of Turkey, in the quirky style that Ünlü is known for.

Sinekatalog’s take

Onur Ünlü's quirky road comedy-drama strings together lost people in Turkish towns with dark humour and offbeat characters. Typical of his dry, absurd storytelling, it is a bittersweet, oddball portrait of drifting lives.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
