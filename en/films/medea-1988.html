Sinekatalog’s take

Lars von Trier's TV adaptation of Euripides, from a Dreyer script, plays out in misty, painterly images. Stylised and sombre, with Kirsten Olesen as Medea, it is a haunting, formally striking and intense tragedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
