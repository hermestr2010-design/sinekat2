Plot

Travis Bickle, a lonely Vietnam veteran, takes a job driving a taxi at night in New York and is repelled by the filth and vice he sees. Insomniac and isolated, he becomes obsessed with a campaign worker, Betsy, and then with rescuing a young prostitute, Iris, and his anger builds towards violence. Martin Scorsese's 1976 film, written by Paul Schrader, with Robert De Niro, is a landmark of New Hollywood.

Sinekatalog’s take

A lonely, unstable Vietnam veteran drives through a grimy New York. Scorsese and Paul Schrader's portrait of alienation is hypnotic, and Robert De Niro's Travis Bickle is one of cinema's most disturbing loners.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
