Sinekatalog’s take

Pier Paolo Pasolini's debut follows a pimp in Rome's slums with the dignity of Bach and neorealist rawness. Harsh and tender, it elevates its outcast hero into a tragic figure.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
