Plot

Coral, an overweight nurse, and Nicolás, a gigolo, meet through a lonely hearts club, and become lovers. He conducts a series of swindles on women, and she joins him, and the couple leave a trail of murders as they travel across Mexico. Arturo Ripstein's 1996 film, based on the real case of the Honeymoon Killers, is a dark and tragic love story.

Sinekatalog’s take

Arturo Ripstein's bleak crime drama, based on the Lonely Hearts killers, follows a nurse and a gigolo who swindle and murder across Mexico. Regina Orozco and Daniel Giménez Cacho are chilling in a sad, nasty romance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
