Plot

A world-weary private detective is hired to find an ex-convict's girlfriend and is drawn into a web of murder and blackmail. After Raymond Chandler.

Sinekatalog’s take

Edward Dmytryk's Chandler adaptation made Dick Powell a tough Philip Marlowe. Moody, witty and tangled, with a hallucinatory drug sequence, it is a defining film noir.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
