Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Moufida Tlatli's drama follows two women on Djerba whose husbands work abroad most of the year, living by tradition and waiting. Gentle and lyrical, with fine performances, it is a sensitive study of female solitude and desire.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
