Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Guru Dutt's tragic classic follows a famous director whose love for an actress brings his fall. Flopping on release, it is now a revered, melancholic masterpiece with sublime black-and-white photography and songs.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
