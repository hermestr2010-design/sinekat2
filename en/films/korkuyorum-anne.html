Plot

A drama that won the Golden Orange and the Golden Boll for best screenplay. English title: Mommy, I'm Scared.

Sinekatalog’s take

Reha Erdem's drama follows a troubled boy in a Turkish town, in a poetic, stark style. Golden Orange and screenplay winner, it is a strange, haunting and visually distinct work of Turkish arthouse.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
