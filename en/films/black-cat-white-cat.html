Plot

Roma small-time crooks on the Danube are caught up in a series of scams and a forced wedding, in a farce.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kusturica's Romani farce is a whirlwind of gangsters, weddings and bad luck. Exuberant and chaotic, it is among his most purely joyful films, with a brass-band soundtrack.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
