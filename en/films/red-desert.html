Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Michelangelo Antonioni's first colour film follows an anxious woman in an industrial wasteland. Monica Vitti is magnetic, and the film's painterly use of colour makes alienation feel visceral and beautiful.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
