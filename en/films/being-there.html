Plot

Chance, a simple-minded gardener who has spent his life in a wealthy man's house and knows the world only from television, is forced out when his employer dies. By chance he meets the wife of a powerful financier and her dying husband, who is close to the president. His simple remarks about gardening are taken as profound wisdom. Hal Ashby's 1979 film, with Peter Sellers, is a gentle, deadpan satire of media and politics.

Sinekatalog’s take

Hal Ashby's satire has a simple gardener mistaken for a sage by Washington's elite. Peter Sellers's deadpan performance makes a gentle, sharp comedy about how we project wisdom onto blankness.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
