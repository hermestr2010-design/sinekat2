Sinekatalog’s take

Jerzy Skolimowski's survival thriller follows Vincent Gallo's escaped prisoner alone in a snowy forest. Nearly wordless and physical, it is a stark, tense and politically pointed study of endurance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
