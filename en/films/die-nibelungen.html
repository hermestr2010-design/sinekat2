Plot

A two-part epic of the German legend of the hero Siegfried and his wife Kriemhild's revenge.

Sinekatalog’s take

Fritz Lang's two-part silent epic, Siegfried and Kriemhild's Revenge, retells the German saga of the dragon-slaying hero and his widow's vengeance. Monumental and stylised, with mighty sets and stark compositions, it is a landmark of fantasy cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
