Sinekatalog’s take

Jerzy Skolimowski's debut, in which he plays a student about to leave for the army, drifts through a day of aimless conversations and breakups. Raw, jazzy and improvised, it is a vivid early statement of the Polish New Wave generation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
