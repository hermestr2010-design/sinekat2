Plot

An adaptation of the stories of Bruno Schulz, set in a sanatorium where time behaves strangely. Known in Polish as Sanatorium pod klepsydrą.

Sinekatalog’s take

Wojciech Has's surreal adaptation of Bruno Schulz sends a man into a sanatorium where time slips. Lush, baroque and dreamlike, it is a dense, sumptuous and unforgettable vision of memory and decay.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
