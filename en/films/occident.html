Sinekatalog’s take

Cristian Mungiu's early comedy braids three stories of Romanians dreaming of the West and colliding with each other in Bucharest. Dry, quick and sharp, it is a witty, loose forerunner of the Romanian New Wave.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
