Plot

An ageing horror star decides to retire just as a young sniper begins shooting at random. Their stories meet at a drive-in.

Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Bogdanovich's debut pairs Boris Karloff as an ageing horror star with a random sniper. Tense, original and sharp about real versus movie violence, it is a smart, chilling little thriller.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
