Plot

In 1910 in the Brazilian backlands, a young man is chosen by family custom to kill a member of a rival family, setting off a blood feud.

Sinekatalog’s take

Walter Salles's Brazilian drama follows a boy forced by tradition to kill in a blood feud. Spare, sun-baked and beautifully shot, with Rodrigo Santoro, it is a quiet, tragic tale of honour's cycle.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
