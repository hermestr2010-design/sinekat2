Bay of Blood

Original title: Ecologia del delitto / A Bay of Blood · Turkish title: Kanlı körfez

“They came to play, they stayed to die.”

1971 · 1 h 24 min · Directed by Mario Bava
HorrorMysteryThriller
Watch trailer

Cast

Claudine Auger, Luigi Pistilli, Claudio Volonté

Plot

After the murder of an old countess, a series of killings takes place around a bay as heirs fight for her estate.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mario Bava's gory thriller about greedy heirs killing one another is often called the first slasher. Inventive, bloody and darkly comic, it influenced Friday the 13th and is a landmark of horror.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
