Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Kenji Mizoguchi's early sound drama follows a telephone operator who sacrifices herself for her family and is cast out. Isuzu Yamada is superb in an angry, unsentimental portrait of women's exploitation in modern Japan.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
