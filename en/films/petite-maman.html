Plot

After her grandmother's death, an eight-year-old girl meets another girl her age in the woods near the house who is her mother as a child.

Sinekatalog’s take

Céline Sciamma's tiny, tender fantasy has a girl meeting her mother as a child in the woods. At barely 72 minutes it is quiet, magical and profoundly moving about grief and childhood.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
