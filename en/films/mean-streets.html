Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Scorsese's breakthrough is a raw, kinetic portrait of Little Italy hoods. Keitel and De Niro crackle with nervy energy, and the film's pop-song soundtrack changed how cinema used music.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
