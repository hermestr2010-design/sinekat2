Plot

A Sicilian worker who loses his job for voting against the mafia's candidate moves north and starts an affair, while his wife has her own.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lina Wertmüller's comedy follows a Sicilian worker fired for his vote, who strays north and demands honour from his wife. Giancarlo Giannini is hilarious in a sharp, ribald satire of machismo and politics.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
