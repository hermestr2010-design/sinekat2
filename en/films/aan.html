Sinekatalog’s take

Mehboob Khan's swashbuckling musical, India's first Technicolor film, pits a peasant hero against a tyrant prince, with Dilip Kumar and Nimmi. Colourful and brisk, it is a rousing, spectacular adventure that was a major international hit.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
