Plot

Janez Burger's first feature, a Slovenian film also known as Idle Running, premiered in competition at Karlovy Vary in 1999 and won the Grand Prize and the Fipresci Prize. The film is a lively portrait of young Slovenes in the years after independence, and helped to revive the Slovenian film industry.
