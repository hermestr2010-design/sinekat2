Sinekatalog’s take

D.W. Griffith's 1914 biblical epic, his first feature, follows a widow who seduces and kills an Assyrian general to save her city. Blanche Sweet is strong, and the large battle scenes show his growing ambition and scale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
