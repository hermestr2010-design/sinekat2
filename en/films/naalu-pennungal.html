Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Adoor Gopalakrishnan's anthology adapts four short stories by Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai about women across social classes in Kerala. Padmapriya and Nandita Das are sensitive in a gentle, quiet and humane film that won him the National Award.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
