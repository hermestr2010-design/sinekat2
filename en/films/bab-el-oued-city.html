Plot

In the Algiers district of Bab El-Oued, a young baker throws away the loudspeaker that Islamists use and has to hide from them.

Sinekatalog’s take

Merzak Allouache's drama follows an Algiers baker who throws away an Islamist loudspeaker and must hide from the militants. Tense, sharp and brave, shot as the civil war loomed, it is a vivid and prophetic portrait of a neighbourhood under threat.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
