Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Dino Risi's original comedy-drama sends a blind, bitter ex-captain on an Italian road trip with a young soldier. Vittorio Gassman is brilliant, and the film's blend of cynicism and tenderness is deft and moving.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
