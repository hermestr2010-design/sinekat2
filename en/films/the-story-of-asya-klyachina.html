Sinekatalog’s take

Andrei Konchalovsky filmed life on a collective farm with real villagers and a story of an unmarried mother, and Soviet authorities suppressed it for two decades. Warm, humane and unglamorous, it is often called his best film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
