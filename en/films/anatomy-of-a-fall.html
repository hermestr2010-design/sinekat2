Plot

When a man is found dead in the snow outside his chalet in the French Alps, his wife, the German novelist Sandra, is suspected and put on trial. The couple's blind son is the only witness, and the trial exposes the intimacies and tensions of their marriage. Justine Triet's 2023 film, with Sandra Hüller, won the Palme d'Or and the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.

Sinekatalog’s take

Justine Triet's courtroom drama turns a death in the snow into a puzzle about marriage and truth. Sandra Hüller is extraordinary, and the film keeps you guessing without handing you easy answers.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
