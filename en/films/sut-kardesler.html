Sinekatalog’s take

Ertem Eğilmez's beloved Yeşilçam comedy is a warm, rowdy tale of two families bound by milk and class jokes. A TV staple in Turkey, it is silly, affectionate and full of classic comic performances.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
