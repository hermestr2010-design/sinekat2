Sinekatalog’s take

Fritz Lang's wartime thriller has Walter Pidgeon's English hunter pursued after sighting Hitler in his rifle sights. Taut, shadowy and exciting, with George Sanders chilling, it is a lean chase and pointed propaganda.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
