Plot

A drama of rural life, Bereketli Topraklar Üzerinde, which won him the Golden Orange for best director. English title: On Fertile Lands.

Sinekatalog’s take

Erden Kıral's drama of rural Anatolia follows migrant cotton workers and the toll of exploitation. Winner of the Golden Orange for direction, it is a stark, humane and significant film of Turkish social realism.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
