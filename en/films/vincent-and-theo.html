Sinekatalog’s take

Robert Altman's biopic traces the bond between Vincent van Gogh and his brother Theo, cutting through myth. Tim Roth and Paul Rhys are superb, and the film's plain, unromantic approach makes a moving study of art and sacrifice.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
