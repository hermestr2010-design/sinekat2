Plot

Adapted from Michel Marc Bouchard's play. It premiered at Venice. Known in French as Tom à la ferme.

Sinekatalog’s take

Xavier Dolan's rural psychological thriller has a grieving man trapped by his dead lover's family. Tense, claustrophobic and Hitchcockian in style, it shows Dolan's range beyond melodrama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
