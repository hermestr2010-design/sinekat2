Plot

In Tito's Yugoslavia after the break with Stalin in 1948, a boy sees his father taken away after an indiscreet remark.

Sinekatalog’s take

Emir Kusturica's Palme d'Or winner sees Tito-era Yugoslavia through a boy's eyes after his father is taken away. Warm, funny and sad, it mixes political satire and childhood wonder beautifully.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
