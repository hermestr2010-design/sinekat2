Sinekatalog’s take

Paolo Sorrentino's debut comedy-drama follows two Neapolitans with the same name, a footballer and a singer, whose paths cross. Toni Servillo is excellent in a sly, stylish first film already full of his visual flair.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
