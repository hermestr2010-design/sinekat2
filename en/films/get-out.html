Plot

Chris, a young Black photographer, goes to meet the parents of his white girlfriend, Rose, at their wealthy family estate. The visit begins with awkward politeness and a strange behaviour among the Black staff, and Chris slowly discovers the terrifying secret behind it. Jordan Peele's 2017 directorial debut mixes horror, satire and social comment, and won the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jordan Peele's horror-comedy sends a Black photographer to meet his white girlfriend's family. A sharp satire of liberal racism with real scares, it turned a small budget into a cultural event.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
