Plot

Dom Cobb, a thief who steals secrets by entering people's dreams, is offered a chance to clear his criminal record if he can do the opposite: plant an idea in the mind of a powerful businessman's heir. He assembles a team and goes down through layers of dreams within dreams. Christopher Nolan's 2010 thriller, with Leonardo DiCaprio, won four Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

A heist film set inside dreams, with a rule-heavy premise that Nolan explains without losing momentum. The rotating-corridor fight and the layered finale are the showpieces. More puzzle than drama, but a thrilling one.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
