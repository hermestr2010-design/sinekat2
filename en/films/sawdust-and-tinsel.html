Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Ingmar Bergman's early circus drama traces a ringmaster's shame and a savage humiliation scene. Harsh and expressive, with Harriet Andersson vivid, it is a stark, theatrical study of degradation and desire.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
