Plot

French prisoners of war in a German camp in the First World War plan their escape.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Renoir's WWI prison-camp drama is humanist at heart, treating class ties as more binding than national ones. Elegant, humane and quietly anti-war, it is a classic of French cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
