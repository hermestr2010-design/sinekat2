Le joli mai

Original title: The Lovely Month of May

“So, how are you feeling?”

1963 · 2 h 45 min · Directed by Chris Marker
Documentary
Watch trailer

Cast

Yves Montand (narrator), Parisians interviewed

Credits

Written by: Chris Marker

Plot

A portrait of Paris in the spring of 1962, just after the end of the Algerian War, told through interviews with ordinary people.

Sinekatalog’s take

Chris Marker and Pierre Lhomme's documentary takes to the streets of Paris in spring 1962, interviewing ordinary people just after the Algerian War. Probing, witty and humane, it is a landmark of cinéma vérité and a fine social portrait.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
