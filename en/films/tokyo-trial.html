Sinekatalog’s take

Masaki Kobayashi's four-hour documentary reconstructs the postwar Tokyo war crimes trials from archive footage. Calm and rigorous, it is a monumental, thoughtful examination of justice, responsibility and memory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
