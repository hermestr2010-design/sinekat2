Plot

Adam, a former swimming champion who works as a hotel pool attendant in N'Djamena, is replaced by his son Abdel during the hotel's change of ownership while the civil war in Chad rages around them. Pressed by a rebel threat, he makes a decision that betrays his son. Mahamat-Saleh Haroun's 2010 film won the Jury Prize at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mahamat-Saleh Haroun's drama follows a former swimming champion replaced at his hotel pool job by his son during the Chadian civil war. Youssouf Djaoro is moving in a quiet, spare story of pride, betrayal and guilt.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
