Plot

A young man sent to the countryside during the Cultural Revolution is made a village schoolteacher. He drops the rote teaching of slogans and tries to make the children think for themselves.

Sinekatalog’s take

Chen Kaige's drama sends a young man from the Cultural Revolution to teach in a village school, where he drops rote slogans to help children think. Quiet and layered, with fine landscapes, it is a subtle, moving fable on education.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
