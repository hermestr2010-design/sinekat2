Plot

A war orphan wakes up one morning with green hair, and becomes an outcast in his town until he turns it into a symbol of peace.

Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph Losey's fable follows a war orphan whose hair turns green and who is shunned by his town until it becomes a symbol of peace. Dean Stockwell is lovely in a sincere, colourful parable against prejudice.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
