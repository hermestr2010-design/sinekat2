Plot

In the winter of 1942, in occupied Belarus, two Soviet partisans go in search of food, and are captured by the Nazis. Under interrogation and torture, one of them, Sotnikov, remains defiant while the other, Rybak, is tempted to betray. Larisa Shepitko's 1977 film, set in a harsh snowy landscape, treats the story with Christian symbolism and moral intensity, and won the Golden Bear in Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take
