Sinekatalog’s take

Yasujiro Ozu's first colour film follows a father who disapproves of his daughter's choice of husband while advising others to be modern. Gentle, witty and handsome, it is a rich, warm comedy of generations.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
