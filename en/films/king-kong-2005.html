Plot

In 1933 a film crew travels to a remote island where they find a giant ape, who falls in love with the actress and is brought to New York.

Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Jackson's three-hour love letter to the 1933 original is overlong but often thrilling. Skull Island is a rollercoaster, and the ape's tender bond with Naomi Watts gives the spectacle a surprising heart.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
