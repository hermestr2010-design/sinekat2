Sinekatalog’s take

Robert Bresson's colour drama follows a pawnbroker piecing together why his young wife died. Dominique Sanda is quietly arresting in a restrained, bleak study of marriage, control and silence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
