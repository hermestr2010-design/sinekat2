Sinekatalog’s take

Dario Argento's second giallo teams a blind puzzle-maker and a reporter in a murder hunt. Less polished than his debut, it is still stylish, with inventive camerawork and a catchy Morricone score.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
