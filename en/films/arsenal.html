Plot

Set at the end of the First World War, the film follows the uprising of workers at a Kyiv arsenal in an impressionistic series of scenes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alexander Dovzhenko's silent film dramatises a 1918 workers' uprising at a Kyiv arsenal in poetic, impressionistic images. Fierce, strange and visually powerful, it is a landmark of Ukrainian and Soviet silent cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
