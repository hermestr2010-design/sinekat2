Plot

A stoker rescues a suicidal woman from the harbour and marries her on a night's impulse. A silent film of fog and lamplight.

Sinekatalog’s take

Josef von Sternberg's silent masterpiece follows a stoker saving a suicidal woman and marrying her on impulse. Fog, lamplight and George Bancroft's gruff tenderness make it a gorgeous, sad and poetic harbour romance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
