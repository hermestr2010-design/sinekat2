Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Abderrahmane Sissako's film stages a trial of the World Bank and IMF in a Malian courtyard, as family life continues around it. Inventive and humane, mixing satire and drama, it is a politically sharp and warm-hearted work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
