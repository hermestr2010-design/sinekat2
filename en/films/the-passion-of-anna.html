Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Ingmar Bergman's stark island drama explores violence, isolation and failed communication among four people. Intense and austere, with Liv Ullmann and Max von Sydow superb, it is a bleak and uncompromising work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
