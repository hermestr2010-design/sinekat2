Sinekatalog’s take

Dušan Makavejev's comedy follows a Soviet officer left behind in Berlin after the wall's fall, drifting among the new capitalists. Quirky and surreal, with Makavejev's collage style, it is an amusing but uneven farewell to the old order.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
