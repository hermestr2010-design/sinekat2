Sinekatalog’s take

Robert Bresson's austere film draws its dialogue straight from the trial records. Short, severe and intense, with Florence Delay as Joan, it is a stark and haunting study of faith, persecution and dignity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
