Plot

Her feature debut, adapted from an Alice Munro story. It won the Genie for best direction and an Oscar nomination for best adapted screenplay.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sarah Polley's debut is a delicate, heartbreaking study of a marriage tested by Alzheimer's. Julie Christie is luminous, and the film's restraint and compassion make it deeply moving.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
