Sinekatalog’s take

Ernst Lubitsch's silent farce follows a spoiled American millionaire's daughter determined to marry a prince. Fast, silly and full of visual gags, with Ossi Oswalda, it is a sparkling early example of his comic spark.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
