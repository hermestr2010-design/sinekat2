Sinekatalog’s take

Eric Rohmer's seaside comedy watches teenage Pauline observe her cousin's love tangles. Sunny, witty and talky, with Rohmer's gift for self-deception, it is a delightful summer entertainment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
