Plot

The Little Tramp joins the Klondike gold rush and finds himself in a storm-battered cabin with a fugitive and a giant of a prospector. Starving, he boils and eats his own boot, and the hungry prospector sees him as a chicken. In a frontier town he falls for the dance hall girl Georgia. Famous scenes include the dance of the bread rolls and the cabin teetering over a cliff. Charlie Chaplin's 1925 comedy balances slapstick with pathos, and it is the film he said he most wanted to be remembered for.
