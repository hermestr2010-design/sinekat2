Sinekatalog’s take

Alice Rohrwacher's Cannes Grand Prix winner follows a beekeeping family in rural Italy and a TV talent contest. Lyrical, earthy and magical, with fine child performances, it is a bittersweet fable about tradition and change.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
