Plot

Her first 35mm feature, which won the Caméra d'Or at Cannes, making her its youngest winner.

Sinekatalog’s take

Naomi Kawase's debut follows a family in a dying Nara village after a railway project is cancelled, their lives fraying slowly. It won the Caméra d'Or, with soft images, silence and rural beauty in a quiet, personal first work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
