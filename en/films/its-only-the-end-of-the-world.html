Plot

Adapted from Jean-Luc Lagarce's play. It won the Grand Prix at Cannes. Known in French as Juste la fin du monde.

Sinekatalog’s take

Xavier Dolan's family drama, based on Lagarce's play, is a hothouse of shouting and unspoken pain. The cast is superb, but the claustrophobic closeups and constant noise exhaust as much as they move.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
