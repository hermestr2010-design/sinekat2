Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Resnais's comedy has Parisians lip-syncing to old French pop songs in the middle of their romantic tangles. Playful, bright and witty, with Sabine Azéma and Agnès Jaoui, it is a stylish, charming musical farce.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
