Plot

A documentary on the Italian Resistance, made with other directors.

Sinekatalog’s take

Giuseppe De Santis and others' documentary assembles newsreel and reconstructed footage of the Italian Resistance and the liberation. Raw, immediate and heartfelt, it is a vital record of the war's end and an early cornerstone of postwar Italian cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
