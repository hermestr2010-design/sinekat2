Sinekatalog’s take

Julien Duvivier's episodic drama follows a widow tracking down the men on her old dance card and seeing how time changed them. Marie Bell, Harry Baur and Louis Jouvet shine in a wistful, polished and elegant portrait of regret.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
