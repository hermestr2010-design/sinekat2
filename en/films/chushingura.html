Sinekatalog’s take

Hiroshi Inagaki's colourful epic retells the forty-seven ronin avenging their master, with Toshiro Mifune in a supporting role. Stately and lavish, it is a long, dignified take on Japan's national legend.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
