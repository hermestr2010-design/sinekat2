Plot

An American oil company sends a young executive, Mac, to buy up a Scottish fishing village, which they want for a refinery. He finds the villagers surprisingly willing to sell, but is slowly charmed by the beauty of the place and its eccentric inhabitants. Bill Forsyth's 1983 film, with Burt Lancaster, Peter Riegert and a score by Mark Knopfler, is a gentle, warm comedy that is loved by many.
