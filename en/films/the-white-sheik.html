Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Federico Fellini's first solo feature sends a honeymooning bride chasing her photo-novel idol across Rome. Light, tender and farcical, with Alberto Sordi superb, it is a charming comic debut full of Fellini's later themes.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
