Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Tanner's drifting film follows a Swiss sailor who jumps ship in Lisbon and falls into the city's quiet light and a love affair. Bruno Ganz is lovely in a lyrical, melancholy and finely observed travel-poem.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
