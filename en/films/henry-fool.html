Plot

Henry Fool, a drifter who claims to be writing a great confession, moves into the home of a shy, near-silent garbageman, Simon Grim. Henry inspires him to write, and Simon's poem becomes a sensation. Meanwhile Henry's life gradually unravels. Hal Hartley's 1997 film, a deadpan comedy about literature, fame and failure, won the prize for best screenplay at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Hal Hartley's deadpan comedy has a drifter inspiring a garbageman to write a scandalous poem. Dry, witty and strange, with James Urbaniak and Parker Posey, it is a cleverly absurd tale of art and fraud.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
