Sinekatalog’s take

Sam Peckinpah's elegiac western, scored by Bob Dylan, is a mournful meditation on the end of the Old West. Butchered by its studio, it still moves, and the Dylan songs are unforgettable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
