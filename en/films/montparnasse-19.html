Sinekatalog’s take

Jacques Becker's biopic follows Modigliani's last days in poverty and illness in Paris. Gérard Philipe is superb and Anouk Aimée lovely in a tragic, lyrical portrait of an artist unrecognised in his time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
