Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Bruno Dumont's Grand Prix winner moves from a Flemish farm to a nameless war, where young men become brutal and ruined. Harsh, spare and unsparing, it is a stark meditation on violence and the animal in us.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
