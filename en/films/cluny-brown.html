Sinekatalog’s take

Ernst Lubitsch's gentle comedy sends Jennifer Jones's free-spirited plumber's niece into service in an English manor. Whimsical, satirical and charming, with Charles Boyer, it is a light, wry romp at class pretension.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
