Plot

A man barricades himself in a hotel room after a murder, and spends the night recalling the events that led to it.

Sinekatalog’s take

Marcel Carné's poetic realist classic follows a killer barricaded in a hotel room, recalling his fate. Jean Gabin is magnetic, and the film's fatalism and flashbacks influenced noir for years.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
