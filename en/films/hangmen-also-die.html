Sinekatalog’s take

Fritz Lang's wartime thriller dramatises the aftermath of Heydrich's assassination and Czech resistance. Tense and angry, with a Brecht story and Hanns Eisler's score, it is a gripping, morally charged propaganda drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
