Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Johnnie To's cool gangster film follows five hired guns guarding a triad boss through long hours of waiting. Minimalist, stylish and sharply choreographed, with a memorable shopping-mall shootout, it is a Hong Kong classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
