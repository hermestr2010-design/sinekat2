Plot

Félicité, a proud bar singer in Kinshasa, tries to raise money for an operation for her teenage son, who has been injured in a motorcycle accident and whose leg is at risk. She moves through the city, begging, borrowing and confronting a hard-drinking mechanic, Tabu, who becomes her unexpected companion. Alain Gomis's 2017 film, with musical scenes by the Kasai Allstars, won the Grand Jury Prize in Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Gomis's drama follows a proud Kinshasa bar singer racing to raise money for her injured son's operation. Véro Tshanda Beya is magnificent in a vivid, musical and rough-textured portrait of will and city life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
