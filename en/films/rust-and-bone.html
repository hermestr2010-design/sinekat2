Plot

A killer-whale trainer who loses her legs in an accident and a drifter working as a bouncer become involved.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jacques Audiard's drama of a trainer and a drifter is raw, physical and emotional. Marion Cotillard and Matthias Schoenaerts give strong, unsentimental performances, avoiding melodrama in a story that invites it.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
