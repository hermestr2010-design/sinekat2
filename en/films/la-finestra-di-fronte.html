La finestra di fronte

Original title: Facing Windows · Turkish title: Karşı pencere

“Desire knows no bounds.”

2003 · 1 h 42 min · Directed by Ferzan Özpetek
DramaRomance
Watch trailer

Cast

Giovanna Mezzogiorno, Raoul Bova, Filippo Nigro, Massimo Girotti

Plot

Giovanna, a married woman in Rome who feels trapped in her life, develops a fascination with a handsome man in the building opposite. At the same time, she and her husband find an old man who has lost his memory, and they try to find out who he is. Ferzan Özpetek's 2003 drama won the David di Donatello for best film, and was the last film role of Massimo Girotti.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ferzan Özpetek's drama blends a wife's temptation with a mystery about an amnesiac old man. Giovanna Mezzogiorno is superb in a warm, emotional film about memory, love and secrets.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
