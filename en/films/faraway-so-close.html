Sinekatalog’s take

Wim Wenders's sequel to Wings of Desire sends an angel into the human world of a reunified Berlin. Ambitious and star-filled with Peter Falk and Willem Dafoe, it is poetic but overlong and less magical than the original.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
