Sinekatalog’s take

Charlie Chaplin's drama, in which he barely appears, follows a country girl who becomes a Paris kept woman. Edna Purviance is fine in a sophisticated, subtle silent melodrama that was influential for its restraint and was a commercial disappointment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
