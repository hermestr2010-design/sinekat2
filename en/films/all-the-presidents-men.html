Plot

In 1972, the Washington Post reporters Bob Woodward and Carl Bernstein investigate a break-in at the Democratic National Committee headquarters in the Watergate building. With the help of an anonymous source, "Deep Throat", they follow a trail that leads to the highest levels of the Nixon administration. Alan J. Pakula's 1976 film, with Robert Redford and Dustin Hoffman, won four Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

Two Washington Post reporters unravel Watergate. Pakula's procedural is tense without chases or shootouts, built on phone calls, notes and the shadows of a parking garage.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
