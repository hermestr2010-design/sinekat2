Cast

Plot

A mix of documentary and narrative that stages an annual passion play performed by villagers in northern Portugal. Known in Portuguese as Acto da Primavera.

Sinekatalog’s take

Manoel de Oliveira's film blends documentary and narrative to stage a village Passion play in northern Portugal. Austere and reverent, with a fascinating mix of reality and performance, it is an early work in his singular career.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
