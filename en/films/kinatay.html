Plot

Peping, a young criminology student in Manila, takes a job as a driver for a gang of criminals, in order to pay for his wife and baby. During one long night, he witnesses a brutal crime, and is drawn into it. Brillante Mendoza's 2009 film, known in English as Butchered, won him the award for best director at Cannes, and was controversial for its violence.
