Sinekatalog’s take

Nicolas Philibert's documentary spends time at La Borde, a psychiatric clinic, as patients and staff prepare a summer festival. Open-hearted and tender, with no judgement, it is a humane, quietly joyous portrait of mental illness and community.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
