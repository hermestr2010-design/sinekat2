Sinekatalog’s take

Hou Hsiao-hsien's drifting crime film follows small-time Taiwanese gangsters chasing deals, cars and dreams. Languid and elliptical, with a strong sense of place and music, it is a cool, melancholy portrait of aimless modern lives.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
