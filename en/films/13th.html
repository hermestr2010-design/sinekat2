Plot

A Netflix documentary on race, the American criminal justice system and mass incarceration, named for the Thirteenth Amendment. It was nominated for the Oscar for best documentary.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ava DuVernay's documentary connects the Thirteenth Amendment's loophole to mass incarceration in the US. Direct and persuasive, it is an argumentative film with a clear point of view.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
