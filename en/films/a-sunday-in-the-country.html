Plot

In 1912, an elderly painter receives his son's family at his country house, and his unconventional daughter arrives.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bertrand Tavernier's tender period piece follows an aging painter receiving his family at his country house in 1912. Painterly, warm and wistful, with Sabine Azéma excellent as the unconventional daughter, it is a masterpiece of quiet feeling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
