Camp de Thiaroye

Original title: The Camp at Thiaroye
1988 · 2 h 37 min · Directed by Ousmane Sembène
DramaWar
Watch trailer

Cast

Ibrahima Sane, Sijiri Bakaba, Ami Diakhate

Plot

In 1944, African soldiers back from the war in Europe are held at a camp in Senegal, and their demands for pay are crushed by the French army. Co-directed with Thierno Faty Sow.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ousmane Sembène and Thierno Faty Sow's drama retells the 1944 massacre of African soldiers demanding pay at a Senegalese camp. Angry, dignified and moving, it is a powerful indictment of colonial betrayal and a landmark of African cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
