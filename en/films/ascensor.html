Plot

Four strangers become trapped together in an elevator. Tomás Muñoz's 1978 Spanish film uses a single claustrophobic setting to build a drama of character, as the passengers react to the confinement in their different ways.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tomás Muñoz's Spanish thriller traps four strangers in a lift, where claustrophobia and character collide. Spare and tense, using a single setting, it is a modest but effective drama of confinement and reaction.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
