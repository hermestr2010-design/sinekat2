Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jacques Rivette's debut follows a student drawn into a web of conspiracy theories around a dead musician in Paris. Moody, dreamlike and rambling, it is an early New Wave work of paranoia and unfinished plays.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
