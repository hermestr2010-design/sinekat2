Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Patricio Guzmán's documentary traces Chile's history through water, from the Pacific to the lost indigenous peoples and the dictatorship's victims. Poetic and haunting, it is a luminous essay on memory and the sea.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
