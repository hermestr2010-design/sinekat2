Plot

Wyatt and Billy, two bikers, sell cocaine to a dealer and ride across the American Southwest from Los Angeles to New Orleans for Mardi Gras. On the way they visit a commune, are jailed in a small town and befriend an alcoholic lawyer, George Hanson, played by Jack Nicholson. Dennis Hopper's 1969 film, with a rock soundtrack, became a landmark of counterculture cinema.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dennis Hopper's counterculture road movie defined an era of American independent film. Its rock soundtrack and open-road imagery still work, even if the story is loose and has aged unevenly.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
