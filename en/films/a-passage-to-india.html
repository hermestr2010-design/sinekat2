Written by: E.M. Forster

Plot

A young Englishwoman visiting colonial India accuses a local doctor of assault, and the trial divides the community.

Sinekatalog’s take

David Lean's last film adapts E.M. Forster with grand scenery and careful ambiguity. Peggy Ashcroft won an Oscar, and the film is handsome and thoughtful, though paced with epic deliberation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
