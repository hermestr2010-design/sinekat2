Plot

A suicidal rock musician rescues a woman who has lost her memory in a motorbike accident, and pretends to be her boyfriend.

Sinekatalog’s take

Julio Medem's romantic comedy-thriller has a suicidal rocker pretending to be the boyfriend of an amnesiac woman. Playful, twisty and visually inventive, it is a charming early Medem puzzle of identity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
