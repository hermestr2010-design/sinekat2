Plot

Workers and a saboteur in a factory. Co-directed with Sergei Yutkevich.

Sinekatalog’s take

Fridrikh Ermler and Sergei Yutkevich's early sound drama follows workers uncovering a saboteur in a Leningrad factory. Brisk and propagandist, but skilfully made, it was a big Soviet hit and is a good example of the five-year-plan film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
