Plot

Set in Istanbul during a nationwide power cut, it follows four characters in a neighbourhood that is being gentrified, as they face the transformation of the city and their own lives. Azra Deniz Okyay's 2020 film, whose title means "Ghosts", is an energetic portrait of a younger generation in a city under pressure.

Sinekatalog’s take
