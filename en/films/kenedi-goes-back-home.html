Sinekatalog’s take

Želimir Žilnik's documentary follows Kenedi, a Roma man deported from Germany, returning to Serbia and trying to rebuild. Funny, humane and lightly staged, it is a warm, sharp look at belonging and borders.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
