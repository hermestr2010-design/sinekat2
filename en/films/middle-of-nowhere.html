Plot

The wife of a man serving a ten-year sentence gives up medical school to support him. It won her the directing award at Sundance.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ava DuVernay's quiet drama follows a woman who leaves medical school to support her husband in prison, and finds her own path. Emayatzy Corinealdi is excellent in a tender, intimate portrait that won DuVernay Sundance's directing prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
