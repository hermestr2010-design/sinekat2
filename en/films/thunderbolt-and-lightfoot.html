Sinekatalog’s take

Michael Cimino's debut mixes buddy comedy and heist thriller, with Clint Eastwood and a Oscar-nominated Jeff Bridges. Loose, funny and suddenly bleak, it is an underrated 1970s gem.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
