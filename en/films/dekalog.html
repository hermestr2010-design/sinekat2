Plot

A series of ten one-hour films, each loosely inspired by one of the Ten Commandments, set among residents of a Warsaw housing estate.

Sinekatalog’s take

Krzysztof Kieślowski's ten one-hour films, each loosely inspired by a commandment, observe residents of a Warsaw housing estate facing moral dilemmas. Subtle, humane and unforgettable, it is one of the great achievements of television and cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
