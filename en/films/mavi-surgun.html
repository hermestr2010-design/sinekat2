Plot

A film (Mavi Sürgün) which won him the Golden Orange for best director. English title: The Blue Exile.

Sinekatalog’s take

Erden Kıral's biographical drama follows the exile of the writer Cevat Şakir (the Fisherman of Halicarnassus) to Bodrum, a Golden Orange winner for best director. Handsome and thoughtful, with lovely Aegean views, it is a sympathetic, literary portrait.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
