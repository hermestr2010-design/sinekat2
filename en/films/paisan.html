Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Roberto Rossellini's neorealist anthology follows six episodes of the Allied advance through Italy. Rough, humane and mostly non-professional, with a devastating final story, it is a landmark of postwar cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
