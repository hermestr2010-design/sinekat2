Plot

Otto and Ana meet as children in Madrid, when his father and her mother start a relationship, and are separated. Their lives are connected by chance across the years in a series of coincidences and circular patterns, until they meet again in Finland, under the midnight sun. Julio Medem's 1998 film is a romantic puzzle that plays with fate and repetition.
