Plot

A man stranded at Charles de Gaulle airport after a missed flight finds himself living among the other passengers waiting there.

Sinekatalog’s take

Philippe Lioret's comedy strands a man at Charles de Gaulle airport and has him settle among other stuck passengers. Jean Rochefort and Marisa Paredes are charming in a gentle, funny and humane film about an airport as a village.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
