Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Nolan's space epic pairs big physics ideas with a father-daughter story. The visuals and Hans Zimmer's organ score are overwhelming, and the emotional core mostly holds, even when the plot reaches for the sublime.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
