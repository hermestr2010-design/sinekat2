Plot

In 1694 an artist is hired to make twelve drawings of a country estate and becomes entangled in a murder plot.

Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Greenaway's baroque murder puzzle sees an artist drawing a country estate and a conspiracy. Witty, mannered and visually dazzling, with Michael Nyman's score, it launched his reputation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
