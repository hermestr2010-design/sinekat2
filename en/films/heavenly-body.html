Sinekatalog’s take

Alice Rohrwacher's debut follows a thirteen-year-old girl preparing for confirmation in a Calabrian town, caught between the church and her family. Subtle and sharp, shown in the Directors' Fortnight, it is a promising, observant coming-of-age film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
