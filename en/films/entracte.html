Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's Dadaist short, made for a ballet interlude, offers a surreal funeral pulled by a camel. Playful, chaotic and visually daring, with Satie's music and cameos by Duchamp and Man Ray, it is a landmark of avant-garde film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
