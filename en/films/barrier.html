Plot

A medical student leaves school and wanders through Warsaw with a nameless girl. A film of surreal satire on Polish society.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jerzy Skolimowski's surreal satire sends a medical student wandering Warsaw with a nameless tram-conductor girl and a series of absurd encounters. Playful, bold and visually inventive, it is a witty, restless portrait of Polish society in the 1960s.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
