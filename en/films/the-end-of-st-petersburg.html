Plot

A young peasant arrives in St. Petersburg and awakens politically through the years of the war and the 1917 revolution.

Sinekatalog’s take

Vsevolod Pudovkin's silent epic follows a peasant arriving in the capital and awakening politically through war and revolution. Rousing and powerfully edited, it is a classic of Soviet montage and revolutionary storytelling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
