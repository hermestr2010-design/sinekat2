Plot

A reformed counterfeiter tries to go straight, while his younger brother becomes a policeman, and the gang boss he once worked for returns.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Woo's Hong Kong crime classic made Chow Yun-fat a star and defined heroic bloodshed. Its operatic gunfights and themes of loyalty and brotherhood still thrill, even if the melodrama is thick.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
