Sinekatalog’s take

Ingmar Bergman's chamber drama follows a fashion agency manager and her model on a trip to Gothenburg, both drawn into doomed affairs. Eva Dahlbeck and Harriet Andersson shine in a bittersweet, perceptive study of women and illusion.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
