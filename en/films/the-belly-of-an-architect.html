Sinekatalog’s take

Peter Greenaway's Roman drama follows an American architect obsessed with an exhibition and his own failing body. Cool, composed and darkly witty, with Brian Dennehy superb, it is a stately, bleakly funny study of mortality.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
