Padre Padrone

Turkish title: Babam ve ustam
1977 · 1 h 54 min · Directed by The Taviani Brothers
BiographyDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Omero Antonutti, Saverio Marconi, Marcella Michelangeli

Plot

Gavino Ledda, a Sardinian boy, is taken out of school by his domineering father and made to work as a shepherd in the mountains, in isolation and ignorance. He eventually joins the army, where he learns to read, and goes on to study and become a linguist. The Taviani Brothers' 1977 film, based on Ledda's autobiography, is a harsh and moving drama about the power of education, and won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.
