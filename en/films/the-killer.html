The Killer

Original title: Dip huet seung hung · Turkish title: Acımasız Katil

“One vicious hitman. One fierce cop. Ten thousand bullets.”

1989 · 1 h 51 min · Directed by John Woo
ActionCrimeDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Chow Yun-fat, Danny Lee, Sally Yeh

Plot

Ah Jong, a hired killer, accidentally blinds a singer, Jennie, during a shoot-out, and takes one last job to pay for her operation. A tough cop, Li Ying, pursues him, but the two develop a mutual respect, and they unite against the triad bosses who betray the killer. John Woo's 1989 film, with Chow Yun-fat and Danny Lee, is a landmark of the Hong Kong action film.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Woo's Hong Kong crime film has a hitman, Chow Yun-fat, trying to do one last job to help a singer he blinded. Operatic and emotional, with slow-motion gunplay, it influenced Tarantino and many others.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
