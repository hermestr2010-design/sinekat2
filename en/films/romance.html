Plot

Marie, a schoolteacher in a disappointing relationship with a man who refuses to have sex with her, has a one-night stand with a stranger. Then she begins a sado-masochistic affair with her headmaster, as she explores desire and her own body. Catherine Breillat's 1999 film, with Caroline Ducey and the Italian porn star Rocco Siffredi, was controversial for its explicit scenes.

Sinekatalog’s take
