Sinekatalog’s take

G.W. Pabst's drama recounts Hitler's final days in the bunker, with Albin Skoda as the Führer. Serious and tense, with sober realism, it is a powerful, claustrophobic precursor to later Downfall films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
