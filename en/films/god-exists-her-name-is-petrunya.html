God Exists, Her Name Is Petrunya

2019 · Directed by Teona Strugar Mitevska
ComedyDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Zorica Nusheva, Labina Mitevska, Violeta Sapkovska

Plot

In a small Macedonian town, an unemployed woman, Petrunya, who has recently been turned down for a job interview, impulsively jumps into a river to grab a cross thrown by a priest in an Orthodox Epiphany ritual, which only men are allowed to do. Her victory causes outrage among the men, and a confrontation with the police and church. Teona Strugar Mitevska's 2019 film is a tragicomedy of patriarchy.

Sinekatalog’s take

Teona Strugar Mitevska's satire has an unemployed woman jump into a river to grab a cross in an Orthodox Epiphany ritual reserved for men.
