Plot

A small-time smuggler is hired by a mysterious billionaire to investigate the past that the billionaire claims to have forgotten. It is also known as Confidential Report.

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's tangled thriller sends a smuggler to investigate a billionaire's forgotten past. Flawed and fragmentary, with baroque camerawork and a cast of oddballs, it is a fascinating, uneven noir.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
