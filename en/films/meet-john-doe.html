Plot

A newspaper invents a man who plans to jump from a building, then hires someone to play him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Frank Capra's populist drama has Gary Cooper as a hobo pressed into being a fake grassroots hero. Barbara Stanwyck is superb, and despite a muddled ending the film bites with real anxiety about demagoguery.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
