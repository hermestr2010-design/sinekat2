Plot

During the Second World War a ship carrying whisky runs aground off a Scottish island whose people have run dry. They set out to salvage the cargo despite the English commander of the Home Guard.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alexander Mackendrick's Ealing comedy has Scottish islanders salvaging a wrecked ship's whisky despite officialdom. Gentle, witty and delightfully eccentric, it is a classic of British comedy about small-town cunning.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
