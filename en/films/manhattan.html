Plot

A twice-divorced television writer in New York has an affair with a seventeen-year-old girl, and falls for his best friend's mistress. Shot in black and white to Gershwin.

Sinekatalog’s take

Woody Allen's black-and-white love letter to New York, with Gershwin on the soundtrack, follows a neurotic writer and his relationships. Gordon Willis's photography is gorgeous, though the film's central romance has aged badly.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
