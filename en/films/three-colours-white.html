Plot

A Polish hairdresser, humiliated by his French wife, returns to Poland and plans his revenge.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kieślowski's middle film in the colours trilogy is a dark romantic comedy about equality and revenge. Zbigniew Zamachowski is wonderfully sad-sack, and its wry tone sets it apart from its serious siblings.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
