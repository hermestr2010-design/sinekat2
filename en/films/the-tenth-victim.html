Sinekatalog’s take

Elio Petri imagines a future where killing is a televised sport, with Marcello Mastroianni and Ursula Andress assigned to hunt each other. Stylish, pop-coloured and wickedly funny, it is a sharp sixties satire of media and violence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
