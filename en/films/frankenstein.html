Plot

A scientist assembles a creature from parts of corpses and brings it to life with electricity. The creature, abandoned and misunderstood, escapes.

Sinekatalog’s take

James Whale's horror classic gives Boris Karloff the Monster, with flat head and bolts. More pitiful than frightening, it set the visual look for generations of monster movies.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
