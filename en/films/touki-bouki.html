Plot

Mory, a cowherd who rides a motorbike with a pair of horns on its handlebars, and Anta, a student, are young lovers in Dakar who dream of escaping to Paris. They scheme to raise money for the passage, but when the time comes, one of them hesitates. Djibril Diop Mambéty's 1973 film, with its fragmented structure and bold imagery, is a landmark of African cinema.
