Hitler: A Film from Germany

Original title: Hitler, ein Film aus Deutschland
1977 · 7 h 22 min · Directed by Hans-Jürgen Syberberg
BiographyDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Heinz Schubert, Peter Kern, Harry Baer

Plot

In a seven-hour spectacle shot entirely in a studio, using puppets, projections, and a small group of actors, Hans-Jürgen Syberberg reflects on Hitler as a figure of German history, myth and culture. Different actors play Hitler and his associates, while the narration discusses the way he has been absorbed into popular memory. The 1977 film is a challenging epic about how a nation deals with its past.
