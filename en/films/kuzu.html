Plot

A feature that won best film and best director at the Antalya Golden Orange and premiered at the Berlinale. English title: The Lamb.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kutluğ Ataman's tragicomedy follows a young Turkish man whose circumcision ceremony and military service collide with his fears and desires. Winning best film and director at the Golden Orange, it is a sharp, funny and tender look at masculinity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
