Plot

His return to commercial filmmaking, following three people whose lives overlap. Known in French as Sauve qui peut (la vie).

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Luc Godard's return to commercial cinema weaves three lives with slow-motion breaks and bleak wit. Cool and fragmented, with Isabelle Huppert and Nathalie Baye, it is a harsh, sensual and formally playful Godard.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
