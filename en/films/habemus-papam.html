Plot

A newly elected pope is overcome by panic and refuses to appear on the balcony, so a psychoanalyst is brought into the Vatican.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nanni Moretti's comedy about a newly elected pope panicking before the crowd is gentle and witty. Michel Piccoli is touching, and the film's humane look at doubt and responsibility is delightful.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
