Plot

On the eve of Nigerian independence in 1960, Danladi Waziri, a Northern police inspector, is sent to a remote village in the Yoruba region to investigate the murder of young women. As he closes in on the killer, the film shows the tensions of colonial rule, tribes and religion, and the secret behind the crimes. Kunle Afolayan's 2014 thriller is among the most successful Nigerian films.
