Plot

A young girl in Tehran tries to buy a goldfish for the New Year with her money. It won the Caméra d'Or at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jafar Panahi's debut follows a little girl in Tehran trying to buy a goldfish. Told almost in real time with charming simplicity, it is a gentle, observant film that won the Caméra d'Or.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
