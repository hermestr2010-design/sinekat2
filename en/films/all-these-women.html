All These Women

Original title: För att inte tala om alla dessa kvinnor

Comedy

Plot

A comedy about a critic who tries to write about a famous cellist and is surrounded by the women in his life. It was Bergman's first colour film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ingmar Bergman's broad comedy, his first in colour, has a critic hounded by the women around a famous cellist. Bibi Andersson and Harriet Andersson appear, but the mannered farce is widely considered one of his weakest.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
