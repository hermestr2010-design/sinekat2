Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Nicolas Philibert's Golden Bear winner spends time on a floating Paris day-centre for psychiatric patients, filming workshops and conversation. Gentle, respectful and moving, it is a warm portrait of care and creativity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
