Sinekatalog’s take

Richard Lester's zany swinging-London comedy follows a shy teacher learning how to attract women. Quick-cut, silly and stylish, with Rita Tushingham, it won the Palme d'Or and captures the mod energy of its moment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
