Plot

A blind boy returns home for the summer from his school in Tehran, and his father, who wants to remarry, is embarrassed by him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Majid Majidi's Iranian drama of a blind boy and his embarrassed father is lyrical and heartbreaking. Rich in colour and nature, it is a tender, spiritual film with a quietly shattering ending.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
