Sinekatalog’s take

Ernst Lubitsch's early musical has Maurice Chevalier as a roguish count marrying Queen Jeanette MacDonald and chafing as prince consort. Witty, sophisticated and risqué, it is a sparkling sound-era landmark of the Lubitsch touch.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
