Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

George A. Romero's quiet, sad vampire film asks whether a troubled young man is truly a monster. Gritty and humane, with John Amplas affecting, it is one of Romero's finest, most original works.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
