Plot

During the Second World War, Miloš Hrma, a shy young trainee at a small railway station in occupied Czechoslovakia, struggles with his inability to make love to his girlfriend. While the older station staff go about their daily routines, Miloš tries to solve his problem, and becomes involved in a resistance plan to blow up a German ammunition train. Jiří Menzel's 1966 debut, based on a novel by Bohumil Hrabal, combines gentle comedy with tragedy and won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
