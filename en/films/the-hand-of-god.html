Sinekatalog’s take

Paolo Sorrentino's semi-autobiographical film mixes Naples, football and family tragedy into a warm, funny, sad coming-of-age story. Gorgeously shot and tender, it is his most personal work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
