Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jasmila Žbanić's docudrama follows an Australian woman who stays at the Višegrad hotel where Muslim women were abused during the Bosnian war. Quiet and restrained, it is a sombre, careful and haunting confrontation with memory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
