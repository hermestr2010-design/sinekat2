Plot

Three friends, Kabir, Imran and Arjun, take a bachelor trip through Spain before Kabir's wedding, each to try an adventure the others choose. Along the way, they confront their fears and their regrets, and one of them meets a woman, Laila. Zoya Akhtar's 2011 coming-of-age comedy-drama, with Hrithik Roshan, Farhan Akhtar and Abhay Deol, won her the Filmfare award for best director.
