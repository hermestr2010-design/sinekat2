Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Mikio Naruse's drama follows a Ginza bar hostess clinging to her dignity. Hideko Takamine is stunning, and the film's sober, bruised view of love and money makes a mature, moving masterpiece.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
