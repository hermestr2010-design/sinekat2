Plot

Roger, a film producer in Hong Kong, takes care of Ah Tao, the elderly servant who raised him and his family, after she suffers a stroke and moves into a nursing home. The film follows their reversal of roles and the quiet tenderness between them. Ann Hui's 2011 film, with Andy Lau and Deannie Yip, is a restrained and moving drama that won the Golden Horse and Hong Kong Film Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take
