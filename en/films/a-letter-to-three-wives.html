Plot

Three women on a day trip receive a letter from a friend who says she has run off with one of their husbands, and each reviews her marriage.

Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph L. Mankiewicz's witty comedy-drama has three women wondering which husband has run off. Sharply written and elegantly structured, it won two Oscars and is a sparkling portrait of postwar marriage.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
