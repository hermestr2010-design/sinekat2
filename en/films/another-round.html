Plot

A film dedicated to his daughter Ida that won the Oscar for best international feature and the BAFTA for best film not in English. Known in Danish as Druk.

Sinekatalog’s take

Thomas Vinterberg follows four teachers who test the theory that a constant light drunkenness improves life. Funny, then sobering, with Mads Mikkelsen's wonderful final dance, it's a bittersweet look at midlife.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
