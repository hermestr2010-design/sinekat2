Plot

Muraki, a yakuza just released from prison after killing a rival, finds the world of the gangs has changed. In the gambling dens he meets a mysterious young woman, Saeko, who gambles at high stakes for the thrill, and they are drawn together by a shared boredom and the search for a more intense experience. Masahiro Shinoda's 1964 film is a stylish, cool crime film of the Japanese New Wave.

Sinekatalog’s take

Masahiro Shinoda's yakuza noir follows a world-weary gangster drawn to a reckless gambler woman. Cool, stylish and moody, with striking black-and-white photography, it is a classic of the Japanese New Wave.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
