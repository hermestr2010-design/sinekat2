Diary of a Lost Girl

Original title: Tagebuch einer Verlorenen
1929 · 1 h 19 min · Directed by G.W. Pabst
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Louise Brooks, Fritz Rasp, Josef Rovenský

Plot

A pharmacist's daughter is seduced, bears a child and is sent to a reformatory, then goes to a brothel. After a novel by Margarete Böhme.

Sinekatalog’s take

G.W. Pabst's silent drama follows Louise Brooks's pharmacist's daughter through seduction, reformatory and brothel. Brooks is luminous, and the film's frank social critique makes a daring, tragic Weimar classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
