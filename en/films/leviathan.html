Plot

Kolya, a man in a northern Russian coastal town, fights the corrupt mayor who wants to seize his land and house. He calls on an old army friend, a lawyer from Moscow, but his marriage and friendships break under the pressure of the struggle. Andrey Zvyagintsev's 2014 film, a bleak parable of power and the Church, won the Best Screenplay prize at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Andrey Zvyagintsev's Russian tragedy pits a man against a corrupt mayor in a harsh seaside town. Bleak, powerful and politically sharp, it is also visually stunning, with the whale skeleton as a haunting image.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
