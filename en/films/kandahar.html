Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Mohsen Makhmalbaf's drama follows an Afghan-Canadian journalist crossing Taliban Afghanistan to save her sister. Poetic and haunting, with striking images of burqas and prosthetics, it drew new attention to the country's plight.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
