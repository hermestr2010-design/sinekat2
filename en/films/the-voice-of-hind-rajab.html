Plot

Red Crescent volunteers in Gaza take a phone call from a six-year-old girl trapped in a car under fire, and try to send an ambulance. Built around real recordings.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kaouther Ben Hania's drama, built around a real emergency call recording, is harrowing and almost unbearable. Tight and devastating, it puts the audience inside a call centre waiting for help that does not arrive.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
