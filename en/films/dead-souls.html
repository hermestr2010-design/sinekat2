Sinekatalog’s take

Wang Bing's nine-hour documentary records the testimonies of survivors of the Gobi desert labour camps of the anti-rightist campaign. Patient, devastating and essential, it is a monumental act of memory against official silence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
