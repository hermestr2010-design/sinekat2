Sinekatalog’s take

Tengiz Abuladze's tragicomedy of a Georgian mountain village follows a boy, his grandmother and two eccentric neighbours. Warm, funny and gently sad, it is a loved classic of Georgian cinema and early Abuladze.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
