Plot

In the 1970s, five teenage sisters in a Michigan suburb die by suicide, as remembered by the neighbourhood boys who watched them.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sofia Coppola's debut is a dreamy, sorrowful portrait of teenage girls seen from a distance. Soft-focus and nostalgic, it makes mystery out of grief, and its soundtrack is memorable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
