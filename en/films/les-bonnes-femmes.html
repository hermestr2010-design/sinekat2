Sinekatalog’s take

Claude Chabrol's cool, bleak film follows four Paris shop girls whose small hopes lead to dark ends. Bernadette Lafont and Stéphane Audran shine in a bitter, ironic portrait that was a flop and is now admired.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
