Sinekatalog’s take

Shohei Imamura's documentary-fiction film begins as a search for a missing salesman and turns into a probing examination of truth, performance and the filmmaking itself. Provocative and cunning, it is a landmark of blurring genres.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
