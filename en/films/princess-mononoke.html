Plot

A young prince cursed by a boar god gets caught between the forest gods and an ironworking town.

Sinekatalog’s take

Miyazaki's environmental epic refuses easy villains: iron-makers and forest gods both have a case. Beautiful, violent and weighty, it is among his most serious films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
