The Immortal Story

1968 · 58 min · Directed by Orson Welles
Drama

Plot

A wealthy old merchant in Macao decides to make a sailor's story come true. It is based on a tale by Isak Dinesen and was made for French television.

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's short, hushed film has a rich old merchant in Macao deciding to make a sailor's story come true. Elegant and strange, with Jeanne Moreau, made for French TV, it is a delicate, melancholy late Welles.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
