Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Sinan Çetin's drama follows Turkish migrants in Germany and the tensions of identity, work and prejudice. Earnest and topical, it is a modest, occasionally melodramatic film that reflects early 1990s migration debates.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
