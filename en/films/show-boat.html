Plot

Across forty years on the Mississippi, the daughter of a showboat captain falls in love with a gambler. Based on the musical by Kern and Hammerstein.

Sinekatalog’s take

James Whale's lavish adaptation of the Kern-Hammerstein musical spans decades on the Mississippi. Irene Dunne and Paul Robeson, singing Ol' Man River, give the film its heart in a rich, moving and ambitious classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
