Plot

The life of Puyi, the last emperor of China, from boyhood to a political prisoner and ordinary citizen. It won nine Oscars.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bertolucci's epic traces Puyi's life from boy emperor to gardener, filmed in the Forbidden City. Visually opulent and sad, it won nine Oscars and conveys a life shaped by history.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
