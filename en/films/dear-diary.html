Plot

In three episodes, Moretti rides his Vespa through Rome, travels to the Aeolian islands and describes his cancer treatment.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nanni Moretti's three-part film mixes a Vespa ride through Rome, island-hopping and a cancer story. Witty, personal and charming, it is a delightful, offbeat self-portrait that won him Best Director at Cannes.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
