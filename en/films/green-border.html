Agnieszka Holland's black-and-white drama follows refugees, activists and guards on the Belarus-Poland border. Harrowing, angry and humane, it caused uproar in Poland and offers a powerful, unflinching indictment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
