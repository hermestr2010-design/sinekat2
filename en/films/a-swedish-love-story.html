Sinekatalog’s take

Roy Andersson's debut follows two teenagers who fall in love over a Swedish summer while the adult world around them is cold and unhappy. Tender and observant, it is a delicate, funny and beautifully shot early gem.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
