Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Satoshi Kon's anime lets a therapist enter patients' dreams, and a stolen device spills nightmares into reality. Dazzling, surreal and fast, it prefigured Inception and is a showcase of imagination.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
