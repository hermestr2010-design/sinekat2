What Time Is It There?

Original title: Ni na bian ji dian
2001 · 1 h 56 min · Directed by Tsai Ming-liang
DramaRomance
Watch trailer

Cast

Lee Kang-sheng, Chen Shiang-chyi, Lu Yi-ching

Plot

A street watch seller's customer leaves for Paris, and he begins to set all the clocks in his life to Paris time.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tsai Ming-liang's deadpan drama follows a watch seller and a woman flying to Paris, both linked by loss. Quiet, funny and melancholy, with Jean-Pierre Léaud's cameo, it is a lovely study of loneliness across time zones.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
