Plot

In 1988, as Chile's dictator Augusto Pinochet faces a referendum on his rule, a young advertising executive, René Saavedra, is asked to lead the campaign for the "No" vote. Using the methods of commercial advertising, with jingles and rainbows, he sells optimism, while the regime tries to intimidate him. Pablo Larraín's 2012 film, with Gael García Bernal, was nominated for an Academy Award.

Sinekatalog’s take
