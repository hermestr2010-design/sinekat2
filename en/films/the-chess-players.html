Plot

Two Lucknow noblemen play chess while the British annex their kingdom. His only Hindi-language feature of this period.

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's historical satire has two Lucknow nobles obsessed with chess while the British annex their kingdom. Witty, elegant and sharp, with Sanjeev Kumar and Saeed Jaffrey, it is a sly comedy of decadence and conquest.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
