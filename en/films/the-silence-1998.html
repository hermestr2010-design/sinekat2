Mohsen Makhmalbaf's lyrical film follows a blind Tajik boy with a gift for sound, pressed by his mother and landlord. Delicate and colourful, with music weaving through everyday noise, it is a gentle, sensory fable.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
