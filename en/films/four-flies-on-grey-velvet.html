Sinekatalog’s take

Dario Argento's third giallo is a stylish but erratic murder mystery with a blackmailed drummer. It has fun camerawork and a Morricone score, though its plot is weaker than his earlier hits.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
