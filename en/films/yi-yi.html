Plot

A Taipei family goes through births, deaths and marriages over a year. The father, NJ, meets an old love in Japan, his teenage daughter experiences her first romance, and his eight-year-old son, Yang-Yang, takes photographs of the backs of people's heads. Edward Yang's 2000 film is a warm, wise portrait of modern life, and won him best director at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Edward Yang's Taiwanese family drama follows three generations over a few months. Quiet, observant and wise, it finds big feelings in small moments and ends with a child's gentle insight.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
