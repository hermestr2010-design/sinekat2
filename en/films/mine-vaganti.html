Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Ferzan Özpetek's family comedy-drama in Lecce centres on a son's coming out at a pasta-factory dinner. Colourful, warm and funny, it is a lively, crowd-pleasing ensemble piece with Özpetek's usual affection.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
