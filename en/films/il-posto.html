Plot

A teenager from a poor family in Milan applies for a job in a big company and gets used to the monotony of office life.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ermanno Olmi's gentle drama follows a teenage boy entering the monotony of a corporate office job. Delicate and observational, with non-professional actors, it is a tender, comic and quietly sad portrait of modern work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
