Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Resnais's theatrical adaptation of Bernstein's play follows a love triangle between a violinist, his friend and his friend's wife. Sabine Azéma and André Dussollier shine in a stylised, intense and tragic chamber drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
