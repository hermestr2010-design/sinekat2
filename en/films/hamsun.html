Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jan Troell's biopic follows Norwegian Nobel laureate Knut Hamsun's support for the Nazi occupation and his postwar trial. Max von Sydow is magnificent in a thoughtful, complex portrait of pride, error and old age.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
