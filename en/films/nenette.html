Sinekatalog’s take

Nicolas Philibert's documentary watches Nénette, an aging orang-utan in a Paris zoo, as staff and visitors speak about her. Slow and thoughtful, with fine close-ups, it is a gentle, moving meditation on captivity, ageing and the human gaze.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
