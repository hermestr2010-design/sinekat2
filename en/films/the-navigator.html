Plot

A spoiled rich man and the girl he wants to marry end up drifting alone on a huge empty ocean liner. Donald Crisp co-directed.

Sinekatalog’s take

Buster Keaton's silent comedy strands two spoiled lovers on a deserted ocean liner. Inventive gags, an underwater sequence and cannibal-island chaos make it one of Keaton's most beloved and successful films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
