Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Dariush Mehrjui's drama updates Büchner's Woyzeck to Tehran, following a postman who steals people's letters and falls into obsession. Ali Nassirian is superb in a dark, tense and psychological film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
