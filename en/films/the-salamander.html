Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Tanner and John Berger's comedy follows two friends trying to write a TV script about a young woman accused of shooting her uncle. Bulle Ogier is magnetic in a witty, free-wheeling and left-wing essay on truth and storytelling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
