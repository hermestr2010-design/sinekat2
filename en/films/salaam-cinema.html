Sinekatalog’s take

Mohsen Makhmalbaf's comic docudrama stages open auditions for a film, as hundreds of hopeful Iranians swamp him and blur reality and performance. Funny, tense and affectionate, it is a delightful love letter to cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
