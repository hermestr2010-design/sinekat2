Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Hitchcock's wartime experiment confines survivors to one small boat for the whole film. Tallulah Bankhead is sharp, and the tight moral drama about trust and a German captain is clever and tense.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
