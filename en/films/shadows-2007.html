Plot

A doctor in Skopje is hit by a car and begins to see ghosts, then goes in search of the truth about a mysterious woman.

Sinekatalog’s take

Milčo Mančevski's ghost story has a Skopje doctor who survives a car crash and starts seeing spirits tied to an old mystery. Atmospheric and moody, with striking images, it is an eerie if uneven supernatural thriller.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
