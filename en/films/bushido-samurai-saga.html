Bushido, Samurai Saga

Original title: Bushidô zankoku monogatari / Bushido
1963 · 2 h 2 min · Directed by Tadashi Imai
ActionDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Kinnosuke Nakamura, Eiji Funakoshi, Isao Kimura

Plot

Seven episodes spread across four hundred years follow the descendants of a samurai family, each facing a conflict between loyalty to their lord and their own humanity. Beginning in the Muromachi period and ending in modern times, the stories show how the code of the warrior damages those who obey it. Tadashi Imai's 1963 film, with Kinnosuke Nakamura in all the lead roles, is a critique of feudal values and won the Golden Bear in Berlin.
