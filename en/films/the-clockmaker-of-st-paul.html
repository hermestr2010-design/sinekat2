The Clockmaker of St. Paul

1974 · Directed by Bertrand Tavernier
CrimeDrama
Watch trailer

Cast

Philippe Noiret, Jean Rochefort, Jacques Denis

Plot

Michel Descombes, a quiet clockmaker in Lyon, discovers that his son Bernard is wanted for the murder of a factory owner. As he tries to understand the son he never knew, a police inspector watches his struggle, and the father's love is tested. Bertrand Tavernier's 1974 debut, with Philippe Noiret and Jean Rochefort, is a humane and subtle story, and won the Silver Bear in Berlin.
