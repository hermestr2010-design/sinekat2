Plot

Three women in different eras, Virginia Woolf in 1923, a housewife in 1951 and a New Yorker in 2001, are linked by the novel Mrs Dalloway.

Sinekatalog’s take

Stephen Daldry's three-era drama is sombre, elegant and carried by remarkable performances. Nicole Kidman, Julianne Moore and Meryl Streep are all excellent, and Philip Glass's score gives it flow.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
