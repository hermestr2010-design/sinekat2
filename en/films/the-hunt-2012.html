Plot

A film in competition for the Palme d'Or, and nominated for the Oscar for best foreign language film. Known in Danish as Jagten.

Sinekatalog’s take

A kindergarten teacher is falsely accused and a village turns on him. Mads Mikkelsen is quietly devastating in Vinterberg's study of rumour and mob certainty. Tense and humane.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
