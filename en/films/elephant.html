Plot

A day at a high school in Oregon, shown through long tracking shots following different students, ends in a shooting. Inspired by Columbine.

Sinekatalog’s take

Gus Van Sant's school-shooting drama follows students through long, drifting tracking shots. It is quiet, cold and deliberately unexplained, and the lack of easy answers makes it all the more unsettling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
