Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Resnais's playful film has actors watching a recording of Anouilh's Eurydice and drifting into performing it. Mathieu Amalric and Anne Consigny lead a witty, theatrical and elegiac late meditation on art and mortality.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
