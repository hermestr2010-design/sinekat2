Peter Greenaway's dense reimagining of The Tempest unfolds through Prospero's books, with John Gielgud as the sorcerer. Lavish, nude-filled and digital, it is a visually overwhelming, intellectual and bizarre experiment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
