Plot

An industrial designer comes home to find his wife in bed and his dinner cold, and finds a gun that belonged to the gangster Dillinger.

Sinekatalog’s take

Marco Ferreri's strange drama follows a bored industrial designer who finds a gun and slides into a private, surreal night. Michel Piccoli is brilliant in a cool, dreamlike satire of consumer society.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
