Cast

Clint Eastwood, Gian Maria Volonté, Marianne Koch

Plot

A stranger arrives in a town controlled by two rival families and plays them against each other. The story is borrowed from Kurosawa's Yojimbo.

Sinekatalog’s take

Leone's breakthrough western, a close remake of Kurosawa's Yojimbo, made Clint Eastwood a star. Sparse, cynical and stylish, with Morricone's music, it set the spaghetti-western template.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
