Plot

A recreation of the events of 30 January 1972 in Derry, when British paratroopers shot dead thirteen unarmed civil rights marchers.

Sinekatalog’s take

Paul Greengrass's docudrama recreates the Derry killings of 1972 in a gripping, handheld style. Urgent and angry, it avoids easy villains and lets the tragedy unfold with devastating immediacy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
