Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Reha Erdem's drama follows a group of troubled young people in Istanbul, shot with a bold, poetic eye. Strange, sad and visually bold, it earned him the Yeşilçam director award and a strong place in Turkish arthouse.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
