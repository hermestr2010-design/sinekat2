Plot

His first feature, a murder investigation told from several viewpoints. Known in Italian as La commare secca.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bernardo Bertolucci's debut, from a Pasolini story, retells a Rome prostitute's murder through the shifting accounts of suspects. Rough and neorealist with a loose structure, it is a promising, rashomon-like first film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
