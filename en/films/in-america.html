Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jim Sheridan's semi-autobiographical drama follows an Irish family finding hope in a Manhattan tenement. It is warm and moving, with wonderful child performances, though it leans toward sentiment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
