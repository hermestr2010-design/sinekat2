Plot

Vijay, a poet in Calcutta, cannot find a publisher for his poems and is mocked by his family and a society that values only money. He is rescued by Gulab, a prostitute who admires his work. When he is believed dead, his poems become suddenly famous. Guru Dutt's 1957 film, with songs by S. D. Burman and Sahir Ludhianvi, is a bitter and lyrical attack on materialism and is listed among Time's best films.

Sinekatalog’s take

Guru Dutt's classic follows a poet scorned by a materialist world, until he is thought dead. Melancholy and beautifully shot, with unforgettable songs, it is a landmark of Indian cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
