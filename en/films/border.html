Plot

Tina, a customs officer at a Swedish port, has an unusual sense of smell that lets her detect people's emotions, shame and guilt. When a strange man, Vore, arrives, she is drawn to him and discovers the truth about her own nature. Ali Abbasi's 2018 film, adapted from a story by John Ajvide Lindqvist, a mix of fantasy and social drama, won the Un Certain Regard prize at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ali Abbasi's Swedish fantasy-drama about a customs officer with a strange gift is odd, tender and unsettling. It mixes folklore, romance and social allegory in a way that feels truly original.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
