Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

George Lucas's student-film-turned-feature is a stark, chilly vision of a drug-numbed future. Visually austere and very early Lucas, it is more interesting as concept and design than as drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
