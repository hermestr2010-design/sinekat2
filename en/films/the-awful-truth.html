Plot

Jerry and Lucy Warriner, a married couple, suspect each other of infidelity and file for divorce. During the sixty days before the divorce is final they each try to sabotage the other's new romance, with the help of a dog who is the subject of a custody dispute, and in the process realise that they still love one another. Leo McCarey's 1937 screwball comedy, with Cary Grant and Irene Dunne, relies on improvisation and sparkling timing, and won McCarey the Academy Award for Best Director.
