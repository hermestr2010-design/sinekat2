Plot

A Slovenian film, Vaje v objemu, directed by Metod Pevec. It won him the Vesna Award for best director at the Festival of Slovenian Film. The story follows a group of people who find new possibilities in life through the practice of tango, and has a warm, humane tone.

Sinekatalog’s take

Metod Pevec's Slovenian drama follows people who find new possibilities in life through tango, with warmth and gentle humour. Winner of the Vesna Award for best director, it is a human, crowd-pleasing film about connection.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
