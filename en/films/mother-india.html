Plot

Radha, a poor Indian peasant woman, struggles to bring up her sons after her husband is maimed in an accident. Preyed on by a moneylender and facing hunger, floods and the temptations of her rebellious son Birju, she holds to her moral principles at great cost. Mehboob Khan's 1957 epic, with Nargis, a remake of his own Aurat, was nominated for an Academy Award and remains a defining image of Indian cinema.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mehboob Khan's epic of a poor mother's struggle is a landmark of Indian cinema. Nargis gives a towering performance, and the film's grand melodrama and moral force made it an enduring national classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
