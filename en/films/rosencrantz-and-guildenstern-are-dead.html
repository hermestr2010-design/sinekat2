Plot

Two minor characters from Hamlet, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern, wander through the edges of the play, puzzled about what they are doing and where they are going. They bet on coin tosses, philosophise and are swept into events they do not understand, towards a fate they cannot change. Tom Stoppard's 1990 film of his own play, with Gary Oldman and Tim Roth, won the Golden Lion at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take
