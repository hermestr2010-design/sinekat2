Sinekatalog’s take

Agnès Varda's Technicolor drama of a happy family man taking a lover is sunny and unsettling. Its sweet surfaces and pastel beauty hide a cool, ironic question about happiness and what it permits.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
