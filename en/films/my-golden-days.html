Plot

He directed and co-wrote this film, which won the César and the Lumière award for best director. Known in French as Trois souvenirs de ma jeunesse.

Sinekatalog’s take

Arnaud Desplechin's romance looks back on a man's youth and first love in three memories. Warm, literary and tender, with fresh young leads, it is a rich, bittersweet coming-of-age film, a prequel to My Sex Life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
