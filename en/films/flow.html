Plot

A cat, whose home is destroyed in a great flood, survives by clinging to a boat with a capybara, a lemur, a bird and a dog. They must work together to navigate a drowned world full of mysterious dangers. Gints Zilbalodis's 2024 Latvian animation, without dialogue and made with free software, won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

Sinekatalog’s take

Gints Zilbalodis's dialogue-free animated adventure follows a cat and other animals on a boat in a flooded world. Simple and beautiful, it depends on movement and mood, not words.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
