Plot

Canada's selected Oscar entry, later disqualified because its dialogue did not meet the language requirement. It won best director and adapted screenplay at the Canadian Screen Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

Deepa Mehta's drama follows a young Sri Lankan Tamil boy discovering his sexuality amid family and ethnic conflict. Colourful and sensitive, it was Canada's Oscar entry before being disqualified, and is a warm and moving coming-of-age film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
