Sinekatalog’s take

Eric Rohmer's comedy of four young people in a new town shuffles friendships and crushes with his usual light touch. Talky, sunny and perceptive, it shows how people argue themselves into and out of love.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
