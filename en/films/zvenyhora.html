Sinekatalog’s take

Alexander Dovzhenko's poetic silent film joins Ukrainian legend, one family's history and the revolution in a dreamlike, uneven whole. Dense, strange and visionary, it is the beginning of the style that grew into Earth.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
