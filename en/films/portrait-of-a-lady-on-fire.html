Plot

In 1770 Brittany, the painter Marianne is commissioned to paint the wedding portrait of Héloïse, a young woman who has just left a convent and refuses to pose. Marianne must observe her in secret by day and paint at night, and as they get to know each other, they fall in love. Céline Sciamma's 2019 film is a luminous, restrained romance, and won the Best Screenplay prize at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take
