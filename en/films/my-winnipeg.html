Plot

A 'docufantasia' about Guy Maddin's home town, in which the filmmaker tries to leave Winnipeg on a train, but cannot. He recounts a mixture of real and invented local history, including sleepwalking, the hockey arena and his family, with actors recreating his childhood home. The 2008 film was named best Canadian film at the Toronto festival.

Sinekatalog’s take

Guy Maddin's docufantasia mixes real and invented history of his hometown as he tries to escape it. Playful, hypnotic and funny, with sleepwalking and a hockey arena, it is a dreamlike, personal and inventive love letter.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
