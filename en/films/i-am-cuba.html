I Am Cuba

Original title: Soy Cuba · Turkish title: Ben Küba

“For you, I am the casino, the bar, the hotels. But the hands of these children and old people, are also me.”

1964 · 1 h 48 min · Directed by Mikhail Kalatozov
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Sergio Corrieri, Luz María Collazo, José Gallardo

Plot

Four stories show Cuba before the revolution, with long, acrobatic tracking shots through streets and over rooftops.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mikhail Kalatozov's Soviet-Cuban epic is a visual marvel of acrobatic long takes through streets and rooftops. Propagandistic but dizzyingly beautiful, it astonished a new generation when rediscovered.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
