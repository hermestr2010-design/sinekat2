Plot

A drama among friends. Ambra Angiolini won a David di Donatello for best supporting actress in her acting debut. English title: Saturn in Opposition.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ferzan Özpetek's ensemble drama follows a circle of friends facing a sudden death and the fragility of their bonds. Warm and mournful, with a fine cast, it is a sensitive study of friendship, loss and chosen family.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
