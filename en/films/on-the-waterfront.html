Plot

Terry Malloy, a former boxer turned dockworker, who once threw a fight for the benefit of the corrupt union boss Johnny Friendly, is pulled into a murder investigation. Encouraged by the dead man's sister, Edie, and a crusading priest, he must decide whether to testify against the mob and lose his friends. Elia Kazan's 1954 film, with a landmark performance by Marlon Brando, including the "I coulda been a contender" scene, won eight Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

A dockworker finds his conscience against a corrupt union. Marlon Brando's 'I coulda been a contender' scene is famous, and Kazan's film is gritty, though its stance on informing is shaped by his own HUAC history.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
