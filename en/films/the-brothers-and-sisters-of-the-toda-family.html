Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Yasujiro Ozu's drama follows a widow and her daughter rejected by relatives after the father dies. Gentle, bitter and quietly angry, with Ozu's early mastery of home and family, it is a sharp study of selfishness and dignity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
