Plot

The long march of the Cheyenne from Oklahoma to their homeland.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Ford's last western follows the Cheyenne's long march home and treats them with sympathy. Epic and elegiac, though uneven in tone, with Monument Valley stunning, it is a sincere farewell and apology to Native Americans.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
