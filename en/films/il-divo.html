Plot

The career of the seven-time Italian prime minister Giulio Andreotti, his links to the mafia, and the trials that followed.

Sinekatalog’s take

Paolo Sorrentino's stylised portrait of Giulio Andreotti is a baroque political biopic. Toni Servillo is brilliant as the hunched, mysterious statesman, and the film is slick, witty and darkly hypnotic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
