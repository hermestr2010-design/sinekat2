Sinekatalog’s take

Charlie Chaplin's late, tender drama of an ageing comedian and a young dancer is sentimental and deeply felt. It features the only screen pairing of Chaplin and Buster Keaton, and its reflection on fading fame is touching.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
