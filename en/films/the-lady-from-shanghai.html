Plot

An Irish sailor is hired by a crippled lawyer and becomes entangled in a murder plot with the lawyer's beautiful wife. It ends in a famous hall of mirrors.

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's baroque noir ends in a famous hall of mirrors. Rita Hayworth is stunning, and the plot is convoluted, but the film's inventive visuals and strange mood make it a cult classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
