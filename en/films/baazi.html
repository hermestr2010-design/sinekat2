Sinekatalog’s take

Guru Dutt's directorial debut is a Bombay noir with Dev Anand as a gambler dragged into crime, in shadowy light and moral ambiguity. Stylish and tuneful, it set the template for the Indian crime-musical.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
