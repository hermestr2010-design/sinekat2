Plot

Based on a novel by Romain Gary, it follows a Black animal trainer who tries to deprogram a dog trained to attack Black people. Paramount shelved it in the United States.

Sinekatalog’s take

Samuel Fuller's provocative drama of a dog trained to attack Black people is intense and uncomfortable. Long shelved in the US, it tackles racism with blunt, pulpy force and a startling, grim ending.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
