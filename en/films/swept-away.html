Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Lina Wertmüller's farce strands a snobbish heiress with a communist deckhand on an island, flipping their power. Sharp, sexy and provocative, with Giancarlo Giannini and Mariangela Melato, it is a bold, divisive class comedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
