Plot

In the Australian bush of the 1890s, Sybylla Melvyn, a headstrong and talented young woman, dreams of becoming a writer. She is sent to live with her wealthy grandmother and receives a marriage proposal from the handsome Harry Beecham, but she refuses him to pursue independence. Gillian Armstrong's 1979 film, with Judy Davis, an adaptation of Miles Franklin's novel, won her the AFI award for best director.

Sinekatalog’s take

Gillian Armstrong's period drama follows a headstrong young Australian woman who wants to be a writer, not a wife. Judy Davis is dazzling, and the film's gorgeous photography and independent spirit made it a landmark.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
