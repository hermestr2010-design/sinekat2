Sinekatalog’s take

Ryusuke Hamaguchi's long, experimental film follows a theatre group and its audience, with rehearsals, conversations and love tangles. Patient and loose, with dialogue-led scenes, it is an early sign of the director's fine ear for talk.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
