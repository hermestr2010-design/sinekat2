Plot

A pianist loses his hands in a crash and receives a murderer's hands in a transplant, and fears they will take control of him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Robert Wiene's silent horror stars Conrad Veidt as a pianist whose transplanted hands might belong to a murderer. Eerie, expressionistic and tense, with Veidt's anguished performance, it is a haunting early chiller.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
