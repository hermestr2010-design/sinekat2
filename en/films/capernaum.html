Plot

Zain, a twelve-year-old boy from the slums of Beirut, sues his parents for having brought him into the world. Through flashbacks, we see how he grew up neglected, ran away, and took care of the baby of an undocumented Ethiopian woman. Nadine Labaki's 2018 film, with non-professional actors, won the Jury Prize at Cannes and was nominated for an Academy Award.

Sinekatalog’s take
