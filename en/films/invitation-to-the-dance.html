Sinekatalog’s take

Gene Kelly's three-part dance film tells its stories through ballet and animation without any dialogue. Ambitious and elegant, with Kelly and Igor Youskevitch, it is a sincere, uneven experiment that was ahead of its time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
