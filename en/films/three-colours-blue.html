Plot

After a car accident kills her husband, a composer, and their young daughter, Julie tries to free herself from the past by cutting off her ties and starting a new life in Paris. But she cannot escape the music, and her husband's unfinished work. Krzysztof Kieślowski's 1993 film, with Juliette Binoche, the first of his trilogy, is a subtle meditation on grief and liberty.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kieślowski's first of the trilogy follows a woman in freedom after losing her husband and daughter. Juliette Binoche anchors a meditative film of grief, music and colour.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
