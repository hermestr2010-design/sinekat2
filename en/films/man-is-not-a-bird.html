Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Dušan Makavejev's early comedy follows an engineer falling for a hairdresser in a mining town as a hypnotist performs at the local factory. Fresh, witty and unorthodox, it is an assured, playful debut of Yugoslav modernism.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
