Plot

Saga returns to his village in Burkina Faso after a long absence, only to find that his betrothed, Nogma, has been married to his father, which breaks the village's laws. Saga and Nogma fall in love, and their transgression leads to a tragedy of honour and tradition. Idrissa Ouédraogo's 1990 film won the Grand Prix of the Jury at Cannes, and is a spare, powerful drama set in the Mossi lands.

Sinekatalog’s take

Idrissa Ouédraogo's tragedy follows a man who returns to his Burkina Faso village to find his betrothed married to his father, and the law-breaking love that follows.
