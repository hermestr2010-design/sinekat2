Plot

Ani, a young sex worker from Brooklyn, meets Vanya, the immature son of a Russian oligarch, and marries him on impulse in Las Vegas. When his family hears, they send henchmen to have the marriage annulled, and a chaotic night follows. Sean Baker's 2024 film, with Mikey Madison, mixes screwball comedy with social realism, and won the Palme d'Or and the Academy Award for Best Picture.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sean Baker's Brooklyn-to-Vegas comedy begins as a Cinderella fantasy and slides into chaos, then heartbreak. Mikey Madison is fantastic, and the film is funny, frantic and surprisingly moving.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
