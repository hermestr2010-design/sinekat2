Plot

A film shown in the Directors' Fortnight at Cannes, which received a special mention for the Caméra d'Or. Known in French as La Vie de Jésus.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bruno Dumont's debut follows aimless young men in a northern French town, racism and a fragile love. Raw and austere with non-professional actors, it is a harsh, striking portrait of provincial life and violence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
