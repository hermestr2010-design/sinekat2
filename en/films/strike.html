Strike

Original title: Stachka · Turkish title: Grev

“Down with autocracy! Long live the democratic republic!”

1925 · 1 h 22 min · Directed by Sergei Eisenstein
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Aleksandr Antonov, Grigori Aleksandrov, Maxim Strauch

Credits

Written by: Grigoriy Aleksandrov

Plot

Workers at a Russian factory in 1912 go on strike after a colleague is accused of theft. The strike is crushed with brutal force, shown in a famous cut against a slaughterhouse.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sergei Eisenstein's first feature depicts a factory strike in Tsarist Russia with bold montage. Its rhythmic editing and intercut slaughterhouse climax made it a landmark of revolutionary filmmaking.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
