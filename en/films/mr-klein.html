Plot

In occupied Paris in 1942, a wealthy art dealer who buys the property of fleeing Jews discovers a namesake who may be taken for him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph Losey's chilling thriller has Alain Delon as a Paris art dealer profiting from persecuted Jews, then mistaken for a namesake. Kafkaesque and stylish, it builds to a haunting, devastating finish.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
