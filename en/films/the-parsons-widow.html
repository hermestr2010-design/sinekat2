Sinekatalog’s take

Carl Theodor Dreyer's silent comedy-drama has a young pastor who must marry the old widow of his predecessor to get his post. Warm, wry and humane, with Hildur Carlberg, it is a charming tale of tolerance.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
