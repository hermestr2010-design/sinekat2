Plot

In a strict Stockholm school, a student, Jan-Erik, is bullied by his Latin teacher, nicknamed Caligula, who is a sadist. Jan-Erik falls in love with Bertha, a shop girl, and the teacher, who is also obsessed with her, torments them both. Alf Sjöberg's 1944 film, with a script by the young Ingmar Bergman, who also served as assistant director, won the Grand Prize at Cannes and is considered an early influence on Bergman's own work, particularly on his portraits of authority.
