Plot

Set in 1978, it imagines the days of a young woman among the Red Brigades guarding the kidnapped politician Aldo Moro. She grows doubtful about what her comrades are doing.

Sinekatalog’s take

Marco Bellocchio's drama imagines the Red Brigade members guarding kidnapped Aldo Moro in 1978. Claustrophobic and quiet, with Maya Sansa's doubting young woman at its centre, it is a thoughtful, humane view of violence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
