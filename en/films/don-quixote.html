Sinekatalog’s take

G.W. Pabst's sound adaptation of Cervantes stars the great bass Feodor Chaliapin as the knight, in a lavish and well-meaning telling. Chaliapin's presence is memorable, though the film is episodic and uneven.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
