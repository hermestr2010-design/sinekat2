Sinekatalog’s take

Zeki Demirkubuz's bleak Cannes drama studies obsession and shame in a love triangle. Stark, claustrophobic and unflinching, it is part of his Mental Minefields trilogy and a defining work of the new Turkish cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
