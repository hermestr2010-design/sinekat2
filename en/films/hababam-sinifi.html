Hababam Sınıfı

1975 · Directed by Ertem Eğilmez
Comedy
Watch trailer

Cast

Kemal Sunal, Tarık Akan, Münir Özkul

Plot

The pupils of a boarding school class, who have failed repeatedly, outwit their teachers and the headmaster in a series of pranks. The class's leaders, with the help of the cheerful, tolerant teacher Mahmut, turn school life into a comic battlefield. Ertem Eğilmez's 1975 film, the first of a very popular series, starring Kemal Sunal and Tarık Akan, is a beloved comedy in Turkey.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ertem Eğilmez's comedy follows the repeat-failure boarding-school class who outwit their teachers and headmaster, with Kemal Sunal, Tarık Akan and Münir Özkul. Anarchic, warm and endlessly quotable, it is the most beloved comedy in Turkish film history.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
