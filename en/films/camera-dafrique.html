Sinekatalog’s take

Férid Boughedir's documentary surveys twenty years of African cinema, with interviews and clips from pioneers across the continent. Informative and warm, it is an essential introduction to the history and ambitions of African filmmaking.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
