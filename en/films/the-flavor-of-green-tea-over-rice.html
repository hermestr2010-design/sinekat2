Sinekatalog’s take

Yasujiro Ozu's marital comedy-drama follows a bored couple finding warmth after a quarrel. Gentle, witty and humane, with a lovely final kitchen scene, it is a delicate portrait of long marriage and modesty.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
