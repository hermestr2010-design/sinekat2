Plot

A film star reflects on his career during a train journey. Known in Bengali as Nayak.

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's chamber drama puts a film star on a train, forced to reflect on fame and doubt. Uttam Kumar and Sharmila Tagore shine in a sharp, intimate and psychologically rich study.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
