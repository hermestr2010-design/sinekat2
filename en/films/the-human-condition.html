Plot

A pacifist is sent to Manchuria as a labour camp supervisor and then conscripted, in a trilogy of war and conscience. It is nearly ten hours long in total.

Sinekatalog’s take

Masaki Kobayashi's nearly ten-hour trilogy follows a pacifist sent to Manchuria as a labour-camp supervisor and then conscripted. Tatsuya Nakadai is magnificent as a man trying to keep his conscience through the worst of war, in a monumental, searing epic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
