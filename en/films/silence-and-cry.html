Plot

A young revolutionary hides on a farm after the 1919 revolution, and the farmer's wife becomes a moral dilemma for him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Miklós Jancsó's drama follows a young revolutionary who hides on a farm after 1919's failed Hungarian uprising and becomes entangled with the farmer's wife. Stark, formal and tense, with sweeping long takes, it is a rigorous, cold study of power.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
