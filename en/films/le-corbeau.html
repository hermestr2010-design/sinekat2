Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Henri-Georges Clouzot's tale of poison-pen letters terrorising a small town is sharp, paranoid and morally murky. Made during the Occupation, it caused controversy but remains a tense, brilliant thriller.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
