Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Lütfi Ömer Akad's drama, adapted from Halide Edib's novel, is set during the War of Independence and follows a teacher caught between nationalists and reactionaries. Earnest and rousing, it is an important period film of early Turkish cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
