Plot

Butch Cassidy, a charming talker, and the Sundance Kid, a gunslinger, lead the Hole in the Wall Gang in a series of train and bank robberies in the Old West. When a relentless posse chases them, they flee with Etta Place to Bolivia, where they try to continue their trade. George Roy Hill's 1969 Western, with Paul Newman and Robert Redford and the song "Raindrops Keep Fallin' on My Head", was a hit.

Sinekatalog’s take

Two charming outlaws flee to Bolivia. Paul Newman and Robert Redford's chemistry, a Burt Bacharach score and the freeze-frame ending made this western a warm, funny favourite.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
