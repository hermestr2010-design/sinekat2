Plot

A stripper returns to her village with a friend to see her family, and shocks the community. The first Japanese colour feature film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Keisuke Kinoshita's comedy, Japan's first colour feature, has a stripper returning to her mountain village and scandalising the locals. Hideko Takamine is hilarious in a bright, satirical and affectionate tale about modernity versus tradition.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
