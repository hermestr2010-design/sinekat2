Plot

Before the First World War, a boy in France grows up to be a fascist leader, in a story loosely after Sartre.

Sinekatalog’s take

Brady Corbet's debut follows a spoiled boy in post-war France becoming a future dictator. Grim, stylish and ominous, with Scott Walker's score, it is a chilling if austere study of the roots of tyranny.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
