Plot

Residents of a Siberian island village are told they must leave before it is flooded by a new dam. Begun by Shepitko and completed by Klimov.

Sinekatalog’s take

Elem Klimov's drama follows villagers of a Siberian island told to leave before a dam floods it, a film begun by Larisa Shepitko and completed after her death. Haunting, lyrical and elegiac, it is a profound requiem for a way of life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
