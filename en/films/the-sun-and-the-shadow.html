Plot

A poetic Bulgarian drama directed by Rangel Vulchanov in 1962, which helped to bring Bulgarian film to international festivals. Its lyrical, contemplative style marked a break from the more conventional cinema of the period, and it is regarded as an early work of the country's own new wave.
