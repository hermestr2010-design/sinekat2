Sinekatalog’s take

Lars von Trier's long, chaptered confession has Charlotte Gainsbourg telling her sexual life to Stellan Skarsgård, who answers with fly-fishing and music. It is provocative, funny and uneven, and more interested in ideas than in arousal.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
