Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Hitchcock's avian horror is a slow-burn thriller that explodes into terrifying attack scenes. The lack of a neat explanation makes it more disturbing, and its effects still impress.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
