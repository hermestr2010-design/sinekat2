Shanghai Express

“Many Men Had Loved Her -- but only one had been loved in return!”

1932 · 1 h 22 min · Directed by Josef von Sternberg
AdventureDramaFilm-Noir
Watch trailer

Cast

Credits

Written by: Jules Furthman

Plot

On a train through civil-war China, a notorious courtesan meets her former lover, a British army doctor, as the train is seized by a warlord.

Sinekatalog’s take

Josef von Sternberg's train drama gives Marlene Dietrich her iconic 'It took more than one man to change my name to Shanghai Lily.' Glamorous, stylised and exotic, it is a visual treat of 1930s Hollywood.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
