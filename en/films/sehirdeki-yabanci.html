Plot

An early film, Şehirdeki Yabancı, which was entered at the Moscow International Film Festival in 1963. English title: Stranger in the City.

Sinekatalog’s take

Halit Refiğ's early drama, entered at the Moscow festival in 1963, follows a newcomer's alienation in the city. It marks his turn toward a socially aware, national cinema and still reads as an honest portrait of migration.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
