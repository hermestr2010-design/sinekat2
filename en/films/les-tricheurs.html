Sinekatalog’s take

Marcel Carné's drama follows Parisian youths living on jazz and impulse in Saint-Germain, in an early look at the post-war generation. Jacques Charrier and Pascale Petit star in a stylish, moralistic and somewhat dated portrait.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
