Sinekatalog’s take

Fritz Lang's offbeat revenge western has Marlene Dietrich ruling an outlaw hideout and a cowboy hunting his fiancée's killer. Stylised and moody, with its ballad narration, it is a stagey, unusual curio.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
