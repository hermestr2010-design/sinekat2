Cast

Plot

Cinderella, a kind young woman mistreated by her stepmother and stepsisters, dreams of a better life. With the help of her mice friends, her fairy godmother and a magical pumpkin coach, she attends the prince's ball, but must flee at midnight, leaving behind a glass slipper. Wilfred Jackson, Hamilton Luske and Clyde Geronimi's 1950 Disney film revived the studio's fortunes after the war, and features the songs "A Dream Is a Wish Your Heart Makes" and "Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo".

Sinekatalog’s take

Disney's classic retelling is charming, tuneful and handsomely animated. The mice and the transformation scene are the highlights, and it helped revive the studio after the war years.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
