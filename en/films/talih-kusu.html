Sinekatalog’s take

Atıf Yılmaz's comedy-drama follows Turkish characters chasing luck and love in a lively, warm-hearted mix. Light, human and wry, it is a pleasant example of the director's late 1980s work with its sharp eye for urban life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
