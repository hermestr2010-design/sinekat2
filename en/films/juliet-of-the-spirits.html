Cast

Plot

A middle-aged wife, suspecting her husband, is haunted by visions.

Sinekatalog’s take

Federico Fellini's first colour feature is a candy-bright fantasia about a wife haunted by visions. Giulietta Masina is lovely and the images are wild, though the film is overstuffed and self-indulgent.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
