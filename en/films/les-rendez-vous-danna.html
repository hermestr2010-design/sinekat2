Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Chantal Akerman's quiet film follows a young director travelling by train across Europe and meeting strangers in sad encounters. Aurore Clément is superb in a cool, observant study of loneliness and displacement.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
