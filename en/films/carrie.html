Plot

A shy teenage girl with telekinetic powers is bullied at school and dominated by her religious mother, until a cruel prank at the prom.

Sinekatalog’s take

Brian De Palma's adaptation of Stephen King mixes teen cruelty with operatic horror. Sissy Spacek is heartbreaking, and the prom sequence, with its split screens and dread, remains a classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
