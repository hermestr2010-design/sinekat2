Sinekatalog’s take

Ken Loach's quietly angry drama of a pub landlord and Syrian refugees in a Durham village is humane and moving. Possibly his last film, it makes a simple plea for solidarity with warmth and grit.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
