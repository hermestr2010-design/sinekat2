Plot

A wisecracking air force DJ is sent to Saigon in 1965, where his irreverent broadcasts delight the troops and anger his superiors.

Sinekatalog’s take

Barry Levinson's war comedy is mostly a showcase for Robin Williams's manic radio improv. The jokes fly fast, and the film turns serious enough to give his energy a human edge.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
