Plot

A shy and overweight Belgrade boy in the 1950s, Zoran, adores Marshal Tito, and writes an essay that wins him a school trip retracing Tito's route as a young man. He experiences the political and personal realities of the time, in a comedy about hero worship. Goran Marković's 1992 film is a humorous and affectionate look at the cult of personality in socialist Yugoslavia.
