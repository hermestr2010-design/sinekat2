Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Powell and Pressburger's wartime fantasy has an RAF pilot argue for his life before a heavenly court. Colourful on earth and monochrome in heaven, it is witty, romantic and visually inventive.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
