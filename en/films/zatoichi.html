Plot

A blind itinerant masseur, who is also a master swordsman, helps a village terrorised by a gang. A new version of an old series.

Sinekatalog’s take

Takeshi Kitano's blind-swordsman reboot is bloody, funny and stylish. Its rhythmic sound design, sudden bursts of action and joyful tap-dance finale make it a distinctive, entertaining samurai film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
