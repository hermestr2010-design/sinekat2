Plot

A lazy nobleman. Known in Russian as A Few Days from the Life of I. I. Oblomov.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nikita Mikhalkov adapts Goncharov's novel of a nobleman who cannot get out of bed, with Oleg Tabakov wonderfully dozy in the lead. A sunny, melancholy comedy about inertia, and about what a lazy man may be right about.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
