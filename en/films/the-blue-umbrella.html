Sinekatalog’s take

Vishal Bhardwaj's gentle children's film, from Ruskin Bond, follows a hill girl whose beloved blue umbrella is coveted by a shopkeeper. Pankaj Kapur is wonderful in a warm, small-scale tale of envy and kindness.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
