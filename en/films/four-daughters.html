Plot

A Tunisian mother and her two younger daughters re-enact, with actors, the story of the two older sisters who left to join ISIS.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kaouther Ben Hania's hybrid documentary has actors fill in for two sisters who joined ISIS, as their mother and younger sisters reconstruct events. Intimate, inventive and searching, it is a bold and haunting family portrait.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
