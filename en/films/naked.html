Plot

A verbally violent drifter from Manchester arrives in London and drifts among the people he meets, in a bleak portrait of Thatcher-era Britain.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mike Leigh's savage drama follows a verbally abusive drifter through a bleak London night. David Thewlis gives a tremendous, tornado-like performance, and the film is angry, funny and very raw.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
