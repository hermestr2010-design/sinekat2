Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Claude Chabrol's cool triangle story has a rich woman and a young street artist drawn to the same architect in Saint-Tropez. Stylish and chilling, with Stéphane Audran superb, it is a sleek study of desire and control.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
