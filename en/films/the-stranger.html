Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Orson Welles's postwar thriller tracks a hidden Nazi in a quiet American town. Edward G. Robinson is sharp, Welles is menacing, and the tight film is one of his most conventional yet effective works.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
