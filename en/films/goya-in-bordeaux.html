Sinekatalog’s take

Carlos Saura's elegant biopic has the aging painter Goya recalling his life, loves and nightmares in exile. Francisco Rabal is superb, and Vittorio Storaro's photography turns Goya's paintings into living images.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
