Sinekatalog’s take

Christian Petzold's noir updates The Postman Always Rings Twice to the German northeast, with a veteran, a fruit-stand owner and his wife. Nina Hoss is superb in a spare, tense story of money and desire.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
