Plot

A ray puts Paris to sleep, except for a few people.

Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's early silent comedy has a ray freezing Paris while a few survivors enjoy the empty city. Whimsical and inventive, with trick photography, it is a playful, dreamlike early sci-fi fantasy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
