Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Luc Godard's second feature follows a deserter ordered to kill during the Algerian war. Banned for years, it is a cool, tense, politically knotted thriller with Anna Karina's debut.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
