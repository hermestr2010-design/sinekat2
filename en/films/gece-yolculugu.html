Plot

A film (Gece Yolculuğu) which was shown in Un Certain Regard at Cannes in 1988 and won the Golden Orange for best director. English title: Night Journey.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ömer Kavur's Golden Orange-winning drama follows a filmmaker's journey that blurs memory and reality, shown in Un Certain Regard at Cannes. Slow, atmospheric and thoughtful, it is a key film of Turkish art cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
