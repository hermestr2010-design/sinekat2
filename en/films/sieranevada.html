Sinekatalog’s take

Cristi Puiu's long, talky comedy-drama traps a family in a cramped Bucharest flat for a memorial meal. Sharp and funny, with fluid long takes, it is a rich, claustrophobic portrait of post-Ceaușescu Romania.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
