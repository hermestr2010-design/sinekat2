Plot

During the First World War, a German farmer, who has been sent to the front, is in love with a young woman. When he gets leave and returns home, he finds that she has fallen in love with a Russian prisoner of war who works on the farm. Rainer Simon's 1985 film, from a story by Leonhard Frank, is a quiet drama about love and the pressure of war, and won the Golden Bear in Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take

Rainer Simon's drama has a German soldier return on leave in World War I to find his sweetheart in love with a Russian prisoner. Spare and humane, it won the Golden Bear at Berlin as a quiet, anti-war chamber piece.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
