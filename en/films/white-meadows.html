Sinekatalog’s take

Mohammad Rasoulof's allegorical film follows a boatman who travels among salt-lake islands collecting people's tears. Surreal and visually striking, it is a bleak, poetic fable of oppression and ritual.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
