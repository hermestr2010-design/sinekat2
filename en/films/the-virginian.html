Plot

A cowboy foreman on a Wyoming ranch must hang his best friend for cattle rustling. An early sound western.

Sinekatalog’s take

Victor Fleming's early sound western stars Gary Cooper as a ranch foreman forced to hang his friend for rustling. Cooper's laconic charm and Walter Huston's villainy lift a stagey but influential classic with the famous 'smile when you say that' line.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
