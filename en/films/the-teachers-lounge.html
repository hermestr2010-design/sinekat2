Plot

Carla Nowak, an idealistic young teacher at a German secondary school, becomes upset by a series of thefts and decides to set a trap by leaving a camera in the staff room. When it appears to show a colleague stealing, her action leads to a crisis in the school, with students, parents and staff turning against her. İlker Çatak's 2023 thriller was nominated for an Academy Award.
