Sinekatalog’s take

Ted Kotcheff's thriller pits a traumatised Vietnam veteran, Sylvester Stallone's Rambo, against a hostile small-town sheriff. More sombre than its sequels, it is a story about alienation and a country's neglect of its soldiers.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
