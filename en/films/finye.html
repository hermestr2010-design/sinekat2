Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Souleymane Cissé's drama follows a student who rebels against Mali's military regime and falls for the governor's daughter. Bold, stylised and political, it won the Cannes jury prize and is a landmark of West African cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
