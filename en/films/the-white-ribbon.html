Plot

A series of mysterious incidents unsettles a northern German village on the eve of the First World War. It won the Palme d'Or.

Sinekatalog’s take

Michael Haneke's black-and-white drama is set in a German village before WWI, where strange punishments occur. Cold and ambiguous, it suggests how rigid, cruel upbringing can breed later brutality.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
