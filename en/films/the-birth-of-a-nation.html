Written by: Thomas Dixon Jr.

Plot

Two families are followed through the American Civil War and Reconstruction. It was innovative in technique but is a racist work that portrays the Ku Klux Klan as heroes.

Sinekatalog’s take

D.W. Griffith's 1915 epic was a technical landmark that shaped film grammar, but it is a racist work that glorifies the Ku Klux Klan. It must be watched critically, as an important and deeply troubling historical document.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
