Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Paul Leni's late silent thriller has a director restaging a Broadway theatre play years after a murder to trap the killer. Atmospheric and gothic, with trick shots, it is a creaky but lively old-dark-house-style mystery.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
