Sinekatalog’s take

Agnès Varda's documentary portrays the shopkeepers of her own Rue Daguerre in Paris, lingering on their habits and dreams. Warm, curious and affectionate, it is a delightful small-scale portrait of a neighbourhood.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
