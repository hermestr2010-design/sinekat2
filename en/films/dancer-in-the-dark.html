Plot

A nearly blind immigrant factory worker hides her condition to save money for her son's operation and escapes into musical fantasies. It won the Palme d'Or.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lars von Trier's melodrama has Björk as a near-blind factory worker who dreams in musical numbers. Raw and cruel, it is very divisive, but Björk's performance and music are extraordinary.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
