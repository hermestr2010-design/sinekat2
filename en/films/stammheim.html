Plot

A reconstruction of the 1975 trial of the Red Army Faction leaders at Stuttgart-Stammheim, based on court records.

Sinekatalog’s take

Reinhard Hauff reconstructs the 1975 trial of the Red Army Faction leaders from court records, in a single claustrophobic courtroom. It is tense and talky, and it was controversial for how it treated the defendants and the state.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
