Sinekatalog’s take

Michael Winterbottom's docudrama follows two Afghan refugees travelling overland from Pakistan to London, played by real refugees. Rough, urgent and humane, it won the Golden Bear for its vivid look at migration.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
