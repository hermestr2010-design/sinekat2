Plot

In 1963 France, when abortion is illegal, Anne, a bright young student, discovers that she is pregnant and has twelve weeks to find a way to end the pregnancy before it destroys her future. Friends turn away, and she faces dangerous options alone. Audrey Diwan's 2021 film, based on Annie Ernaux's memoir, is a tense and intimate drama, and won the Golden Lion at Venice.

Sinekatalog’s take
