Sinekatalog’s take

Mario Bava's sci-fi horror strands astronauts on a fog-bound planet of possessing spirits. Vivid with coloured light and eerie sets, on a small budget, it influenced Alien and is a stylish cult classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
