Cast

Plot

A popular comedy of Yeşilçam starring Kemal Sunal.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kemal Sunal at his funniest, as a village simpleton who is mistaken for a pasha's lookalike. The farce is broad but the class comedy underneath is sharp, and the film remains one of Turkish cinema's most quoted and rewatched.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
