Sinekatalog’s take

Wes Craven's debut is a notorious, brutal revenge horror, loosely based on Bergman's The Virgin Spring. It is crude and hard to watch, but its raw impact and moral shock made it a landmark of the genre.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
