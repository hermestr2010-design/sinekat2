Sinekatalog’s take

Hiroshi Inagaki's fantasy retells the Japanese creation myth, as Toshiro Mifune's storm god Susanoo battles an eight-headed serpent. Colourful and spectacular, with Eiji Tsuburaya's effects, it is an exuberant, childlike epic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
