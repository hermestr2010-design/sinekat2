Plot

In a village in Burkina Faso, a woman gives shelter to girls fleeing female circumcision, and defies the elders with the protective spell of moolaadé.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ousmane Sembène's last film follows a village woman sheltering girls from female circumcision and defying elders. Warm, brave and colourful, it is a powerful, humane protest wrapped in village life.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
