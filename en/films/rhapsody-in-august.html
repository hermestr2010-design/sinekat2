Plot

A grandmother in Nagasaki remembers the atomic bomb.

Sinekatalog’s take

Akira Kurosawa's late, gentle drama has a Nagasaki grandmother recalling the atomic bomb as her grandchildren meet a Japanese-American relative. Quiet and sincere, with Richard Gere in a small role, it is a modest, humane elegy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
