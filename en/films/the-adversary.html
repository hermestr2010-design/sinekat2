Plot

A young unemployed man in Calcutta. Known in Bengali as Pratidwandi.

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's drama follows an unemployed Calcutta graduate frustrated by a humiliating job hunt and a changing city. Dhritiman Chatterjee is superb in a sharp, inventive and angry film that opens his Calcutta trilogy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
