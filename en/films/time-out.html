Sinekatalog’s take

Laurent Cantet's drama follows a man who loses his job and hides it from his family, pretending to work abroad. Quiet, tense and humane, it is a chilling, psychologically acute study of shame and identity.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
