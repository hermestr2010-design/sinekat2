Plot

A 1973 drama by Nejat Saydam, whose title means "the ache that does not cease". The film won him the Golden Orange for best director, and is typical of the emotional melodramas that were popular in Turkish cinema of the 1970s.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nejat Saydam's 1973 melodrama, a Golden Orange winner for best director, wrings tears from loss and unending sorrow in a typical Yeşilçam mould. Emotional and direct, it is representative of the popular 1970s weepies.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
