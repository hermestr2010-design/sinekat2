Plot

A circus donkey wanders across Poland and Italy, seeing the cruelty and kindness of the people he meets.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jerzy Skolimowski's wandering donkey odyssey is a bold, visually inventive riff on Bresson. Its flashes of red, drone shots and animal's-eye view make a haunting, unusual film about human cruelty.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
