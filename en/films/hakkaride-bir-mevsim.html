Plot

A teacher, Ali, is sent to a remote Kurdish village in the Hakkari mountains, and finds a harsh world of poverty, cold and fear. He struggles to communicate with the villagers and to teach their children, while the long winter isolates them all. Erden Kıral's 1983 film, known as A Season in Hakkari, won the Silver Bear Special Jury Prize at Berlin.
