Plot

A young Guinea-Bissauan woman, who has the voice of a singer but a family curse forbidding her to sing, goes to Paris and falls in love, then returns to her village.

Sinekatalog’s take

Flora Gomes's musical comedy follows a young woman from Guinea-Bissau with a singer's voice and a family curse, who goes to Paris and then returns home. Colourful, warm and spirited, it is a charming, lightly satirical African musical.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
