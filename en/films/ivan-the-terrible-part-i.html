Plot

Ivan IV is crowned the first tsar of Russia and fights the nobles who oppose him and the enemies on his borders.

Sinekatalog’s take

Sergei Eisenstein's stylised epic of Russia's first tsar is opulent and theatrical. Nikolai Cherkasov is imposing, and the film's expressionist images and Prokofiev score make a grand, if state-flattering, spectacle.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
