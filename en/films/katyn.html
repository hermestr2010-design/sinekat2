Plot

The families of Polish officers wait for news after the 1940 Katyn massacre by Soviet forces.

Sinekatalog’s take

Andrzej Wajda's deeply personal film about the Katyn massacre, which killed his own father, is sober and devastating. Its final sequence is harrowing, in a work of grief and national memory.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
