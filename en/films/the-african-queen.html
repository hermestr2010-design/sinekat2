Plot

A rough boat captain and a missionary take a river steamer through East Africa in the First World War to attack a German ship.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Huston's adventure sends prim Katharine Hepburn and gin-sodden Humphrey Bogart down an African river on a rickety boat. Their chemistry carried it, and Bogart won his only Oscar.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
