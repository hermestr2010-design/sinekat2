Plot

His first feature and part one of his Ghosts trilogy. Known in German as Die innere Sicherheit.

Sinekatalog’s take

Christian Petzold's first feature follows a pair of former radicals hiding with their teenage daughter on the run. Tense and quiet, with Julia Hummer fine as the girl, it is a cool, controlled study of ideals and family.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
