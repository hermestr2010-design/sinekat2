Plot

A pompous, sexist and clueless French secret agent is sent to Cairo in 1955 to investigate a colleague's death.

Sinekatalog’s take

Michel Hazanavicius's Bond spoof sends Jean Dujardin's clueless agent to 1955 Cairo. Sharp period detail and Dujardin's perfectly dim-witted charm make it a brilliantly silly comedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
