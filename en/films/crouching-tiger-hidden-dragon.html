Plot

In Qing dynasty China, the warrior Li Mu Bai gives up his sword, the Green Destiny, to be placed in the care of a friend in Beijing, but it is stolen by a masked thief. The investigation leads to a headstrong young noblewoman, Jen, with extraordinary fighting skill, and to a past love between Li Mu Bai and Yu Shu Lien. Ang Lee's 2000 film won four Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ang Lee's wuxia romance blends sword duels with a story of repressed love. Wire-fu fights, bamboo-top chases and Zhang Ziyi's fierce young fighter make it graceful and moving.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
