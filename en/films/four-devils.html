Sinekatalog’s take

F.W. Murnau's lost circus melodrama, with Janet Gaynor, followed four trapeze performers and a love affair, and is remembered through stills and descriptions. Praised for its visual poetry, its absence is one of silent cinema's major losses.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
