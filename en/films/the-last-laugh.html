The Last Laugh

Original title: Der letzte Mann
1924 · 1 h 30 min · Directed by F.W. Murnau
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Emil Jannings, Maly Delschaft, Max Hiller

Credits

Written by: Carl Mayer

Plot

A proud hotel doorman is demoted to washroom attendant and loses his dignity. The camera moves freely and the film uses almost no intertitles.

Sinekatalog’s take

F.W. Murnau's silent masterpiece follows a proud doorman demoted to washroom attendant. Emil Jannings is devastating, and the unchained camera and near-absence of intertitles made it a landmark of visual storytelling.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
