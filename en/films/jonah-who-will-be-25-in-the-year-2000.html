Plot

Eight friends, all in their thirties, who are survivors of the 1968 political upheaval, seek ways of living with their ideals in Geneva. They include a teacher, a market gardener, a cashier and a proofreader, and in the course of the film they interact and try to find new ways of living. Alain Tanner's 1976 film, written with John Berger, is a gentle, witty comedy of post-1968 disillusion.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Tanner's ensemble film follows eight Geneva friends, survivors of 1968, living out their ideals in everyday life. Witty, warm and full of ideas, it is a charming, humane political comedy for a disillusioned generation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
