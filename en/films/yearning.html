Sinekatalog’s take

Mikio Naruse's late drama has a widow who kept her husband's shop alive pursued by her younger brother-in-law. Hideko Takamine is superb in a restrained, devastating melodrama of longing that builds to a heartbreaking finale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
