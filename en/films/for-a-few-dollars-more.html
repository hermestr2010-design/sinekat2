Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Two bounty hunters, played by Clint Eastwood and Lee Van Cleef, circle the same outlaw. Leone's second Dollars film deepens his style, with a musical-watch motif and a tense, drawn-out finale.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
