Sinekatalog’s take

Gaston Kaboré's sequel to Wend Kuuni follows the grown boy on a quest across West Africa to find a cure for his sister, discovering his past. Visual and slow, it is a lyrical, fable-like continuation that won the Pan-African Film Festival's top prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
