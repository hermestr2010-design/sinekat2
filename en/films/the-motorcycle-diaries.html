Plot

In 1952 the young medical student Ernesto Guevara and his friend ride a motorbike across South America, and he is changed by the poverty he sees.

Sinekatalog’s take

Walter Salles follows young Che Guevara and his friend on a road trip across South America. A warm travelogue that shows an awakening conscience rather than a legend, with beautiful landscapes.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
