Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Vincente Minnelli's Belle Époque musical won nine Oscars with its lush colour and Lerner-Loewe songs. Leslie Caron is charming and Maurice Chevalier delightful, though the story's premise has dated.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
