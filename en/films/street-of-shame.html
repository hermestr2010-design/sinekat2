Plot

Women in a Tokyo brothel, in his last film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kenji Mizoguchi's final film follows women in a Tokyo brothel district facing poverty and hope. Sharp, sombre and compassionate, with Machiko Kyo vivid, it helped lead to the end of Japan's legal prostitution.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
