Plot

American paratroopers parachute into the Burmese jungle to destroy a Japanese radar station, and must fight their way out.

Sinekatalog’s take

Raoul Walsh's wartime adventure drops Errol Flynn and his paratroopers into the Burmese jungle to wreck a radar station. Rousing and tough, with a gruelling retreat, it was a hit, though its Flynn-wins-the-war bravado caused a stir in Britain.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
