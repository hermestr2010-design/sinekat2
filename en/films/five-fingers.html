Sinekatalog’s take

Joseph L. Mankiewicz's spy thriller, based on a true case, follows a valet in wartime Ankara who sells British secrets to the Germans. James Mason is superb in a sleek, witty and tense film that makes a suave villain of its hero.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
