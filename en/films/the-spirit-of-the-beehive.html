Plot

In 1940, in a village on the Castilian plateau, the six-year-old Ana sees the film "Frankenstein" at a travelling cinema and asks her older sister about the monster. Convinced that the creature is a spirit living nearby, she goes out to look for it, and finds a wounded fugitive soldier. Víctor Erice's 1973 film, made under Franco's censorship, is a subtle allegory of silence and fear.

Sinekatalog’s take

Víctor Erice's quiet, poetic film follows a little girl obsessed with Frankenstein in Franco-era Spain. Ana Torrent's wide eyes carry a haunting, ambiguous story of childhood and silence.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
