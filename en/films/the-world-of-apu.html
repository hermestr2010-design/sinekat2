Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's conclusion to the Apu Trilogy is a deeply humane story of love, loss and fatherhood. Soumitra Chatterjee and Sharmila Tagore are wonderful in a tender, luminous masterpiece of Indian cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
