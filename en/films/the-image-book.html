Plot

A collage of film clips and texts about violence and the Arab world, made late in his career.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Luc Godard's late essay-collage cuts film clips, paintings and texts into an angry, fragmented reflection on violence and the Arab world. Dense, abrasive and visually daring, it is a challenging final statement.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
