Plot

Two Roman boys who shine shoes are sent to a juvenile prison after a petty crime and their friendship is destroyed.

Sinekatalog’s take

Vittorio De Sica's neorealist drama of two shoeshine boys destroyed by prison is heartbreaking. Played by street children, its simple story and bleak ending made it a powerful indictment of postwar Italy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
