Sinekatalog’s take

Abbas Kiarostami's experimental film is five long takes of the Caspian shore: driftwood, walkers, ducks, dogs and a night storm. Meditative and hypnotic, it is a minimalist poem about time, nature and looking.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
