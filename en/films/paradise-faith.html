Cast

Plot

A woman goes from house to house with a statue of the Virgin Mary. It won the Special Jury Prize at Venice. Known in German as Paradies: Glaube.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ulrich Seidl's uneasy drama follows a devout woman missionary with a statue of the Virgin Mary, whose faith turns obsessive. Unsparing and disturbing, with Maria Hofstätter fearless, it won the Venice Special Jury Prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
