Sinekatalog’s take

Victor Sjöström's silent drama follows a fugitive and a rich widow who flee to the mountains and live as outlaws. Spectacular in its landscape and bleak in its tragedy, it is a landmark of Swedish silent cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
