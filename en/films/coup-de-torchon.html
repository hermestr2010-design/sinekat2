Plot

A weak and mocked police chief in French colonial Africa in 1938 starts to kill the people who have humiliated him. After Jim Thompson.

Sinekatalog’s take

Bertrand Tavernier's black comedy has Philippe Noiret as a mocked police chief turning killer in colonial Africa. Dark and funny, with Isabelle Huppert, it is a shrewd, unsettling look at humiliation and power.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
