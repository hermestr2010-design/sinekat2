Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

D.W. Griffith's wartime drama follows lovers in a French village during the First World War, with Lillian and Dorothy Gish. Propagandist and melodramatic, but vivid, it is an early example of cinema as a war-effort tool.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
