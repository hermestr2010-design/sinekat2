Plot

Episodes from the life of the fifteenth-century Russian icon painter in a time of war and cruelty.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tarkovsky's long, episodic portrait of a medieval icon painter in turbulent Russia. Demanding and majestic, with a final colour sequence of Rublev's icons that feels like a revelation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
