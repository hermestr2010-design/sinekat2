Plot

In a rainy, neon-lit Los Angeles in 2019, Rick Deckard, a former police officer, is forced to come out of retirement to hunt down four replicants, artificial humans who have escaped from an off-world colony. As he meets Rachael, a replicant who believes she is human, he begins to question what makes one human. Ridley Scott's 1982 film, with Harrison Ford and Rutger Hauer, has been hugely influential in science-fiction design.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ridley Scott's rainy, neon-lit future noir asks what makes a person human. The visuals and Vangelis's score are legendary; the pace is slow, and several versions exist, with the Final Cut usually preferred.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
