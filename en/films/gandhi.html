Plot

The film follows the life of Mohandas Gandhi, from his experience of racial discrimination as a lawyer in South Africa, through his campaigns of non-violent resistance against British rule in India, the Salt March and Partition, to his assassination in 1948. Richard Attenborough's 1982 epic, with Ben Kingsley in the title role, was a labour of love for the director and won eight Academy Awards.
