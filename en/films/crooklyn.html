Sinekatalog’s take

Spike Lee's warm, funny memoir of a 1970s Brooklyn family is full of music, colour and detail. Told partly through a girl's eyes, with Alfre Woodard superb, it is among his most affectionate films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
