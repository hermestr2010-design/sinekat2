Sinekatalog’s take

Abbas Kiarostami's last film brings twenty-four still images, paintings and photographs to life for about four minutes each. Quiet, poetic and meditative, it is a gentle, strange farewell about seeing and time.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
