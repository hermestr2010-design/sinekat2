Plot

A gang of criminals led by the sinister Professor Marcus rent rooms from the sweet, elderly Mrs Wilberforce and pretend to be a string quintet, using their rehearsals as cover for planning a robbery. After the robbery, the sweet old lady finds out and they decide that she must be killed, but she turns out to be harder to remove than expected. Alexander Mackendrick's 1955 Ealing comedy, with Alec Guinness and Katie Johnson, is a black comedy that grows more outrageous scene by scene.

Sinekatalog’s take
