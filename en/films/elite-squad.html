Plot

In Rio de Janeiro, Captain Nascimento of the elite police unit BOPE, tired of the violence, looks for a successor while fighting drug gangs in the favelas, before the Pope's visit. Two idealistic young cadets are trained as they are tested by corruption. José Padilha's 2007 film, with Wagner Moura, won the Golden Bear in Berlin and was controversial for its portrayal of police brutality.

Sinekatalog’s take

José Padilha's tense Rio drama follows a special-police captain in the city's favelas. Gritty and fast, the film is controversial for its brutality and for being seen to glorify violent policing.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
