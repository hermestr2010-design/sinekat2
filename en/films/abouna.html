Sinekatalog’s take

Mahamat-Saleh Haroun's drama follows two Chadian brothers who search for their missing father and end up in a Koranic school. Quiet, simple and moving, with fine child performances, it is a touching portrait of loss and brotherhood.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
