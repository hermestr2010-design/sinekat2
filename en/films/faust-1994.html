Plot

A modern man in Prague finds a mysterious invitation, goes to a puppet theatre and sells his soul. Mixes puppetry and live action.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jan Švankmajer's surreal take on Faust blends puppetry, clay animation and live action in a Prague puppet theatre. Dark, comic and nightmarish, it is a baroque, inventive nightmare of temptation and fate.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
