Sinekatalog’s take

Tobe Hooper's swamp-hotel horror follows a madman feeding guests to his crocodile. Lurid and stylised, with an artificial red-lit look, it is sleazy and rough but has a certain B-movie nightmare charm.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
