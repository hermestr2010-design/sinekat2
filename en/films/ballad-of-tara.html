Sinekatalog’s take

Bahram Beyzaie's poetic film blends myth and the present as a woman and an outsider share a village in a haunting, ritual-like fable. Susan Taslimi is striking in a stylised, beautifully shot work of the Iranian New Wave.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
