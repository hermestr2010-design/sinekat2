Plot

In Ireland during the First World War, a young woman from the landowning Ascendancy class, Connie, lives in a large house with her mother and brother. Little by little she learns about the Irish independence movement and becomes involved, which puts her at odds with her family and class. Edward Bennett's 1983 film, with Julie Covington and Ian Charleson, won the Silver Lion at Venice.
