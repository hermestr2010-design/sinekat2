Plot

In 1930s Korea a young pickpocket is hired as a maid to help a con man swindle a wealthy heiress, with unexpected twists.

Sinekatalog’s take

Park Chan-wook's lush erotic thriller relocates Sarah Waters's Fingersmith to colonial Korea. Three-part storytelling, twists and gorgeous design make it intricate, sensual and darkly funny.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
