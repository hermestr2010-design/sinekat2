Plot

Graham, a drifter, arrives in town to stay with an old friend, John, a lawyer whose marriage to Ann is already in trouble. Graham, who is impotent and records women talking about their sexual lives on video, upsets the couple's lives, particularly when John's affair with Ann's sister is revealed. Steven Soderbergh's 1989 debut won the Palme d'Or at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Steven Soderbergh's debut helped launch the 1990s indie boom. Talky, intimate and sharply acted, it examines intimacy and secrets with calm, and its cool modern feel still works.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
