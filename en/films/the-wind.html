Plot

A young woman from Virginia moves to the harsh Texas prairie, where the relentless wind drives her toward madness.

Sinekatalog’s take

Victor Sjöström's silent masterpiece sends Lillian Gish to the Texas plains where the wind slowly drives her mad. Gish is astonishing, and the film's storm imagery makes a powerful study of isolation and dread.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
