Plot

In a drought-stricken village in northern Senegal, Banel, a young woman, is passionately in love with her husband, Adama, and is determined to live apart with him, in a house buried in the sand. Adama, who is the village's heir, is expected to lead, and their escape from the community's obligations draws them into tragedy. Ramata-Toulaye Sy's 2023 debut competed at Cannes.
