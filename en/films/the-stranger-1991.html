Plot

A long-lost uncle arrives at his niece's home. Known in Bengali as Agantuk, his last film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Satyajit Ray's last film has a long-lost uncle visiting his niece's home and challenging its comfort. Calm, witty and humane, with Utpal Dutt superb, it is a graceful, thoughtful farewell about civilisation.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
