Plot

A modern retelling of Tristan and Isolde, in which a young man escorts his uncle's fiancée and they fall under a love potion's spell. Written by Jean Cocteau.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Delannoy's retelling of Tristan and Isolde, scripted by Cocteau, sets the legend in modern France with Jean Marais and Madeleine Sologne. Dreamy, romantic and visually elegant, it was a huge hit in wartime France.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
