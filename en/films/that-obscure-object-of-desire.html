Plot

An older man pursues a young woman who is played by two actresses. His last film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Buñuel's final film has two actresses play the same woman who endlessly frustrates a rich older man. Sly, absurd and politically sharp, it turns desire into a surreal running gag.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
