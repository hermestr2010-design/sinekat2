Plot

A taxi driver in a Bihar town loves his battered old Chevrolet, which he treats as a living friend. The film is called a pioneering story of a man and his machine.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ritwik Ghatak's drama follows a taxi driver in Bihar who treats his beaten-up Chevrolet as a living friend. Kali Banerjee is wonderful in a funny, tender and deeply original fable about man and machine.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
