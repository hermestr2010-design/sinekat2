Plot

The matriarch of the Vuillard family, Junon, is diagnosed with a rare disease and needs a bone-marrow donation. The family gathers for Christmas at their home in Roubaix, including her troubled son Henri, who was banished years before. Old resentments surface with affection and humour. Arnaud Desplechin's 2008 film, with Catherine Deneuve and Mathieu Amalric, was in competition at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take
