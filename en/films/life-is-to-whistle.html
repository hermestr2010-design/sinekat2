Sinekatalog’s take

Fernando Pérez's Cuban comedy-drama follows three Havanese, a choreographer, a young woman and a man who have lost faith, searching for something to believe in. Playful and humane, with Afro-Cuban rhythms, it is a lively portrait of a city in crisis.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
