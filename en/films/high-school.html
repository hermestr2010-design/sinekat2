Plot

Frederick Wiseman's 1968 documentary observes a high school in Philadelphia, Northeast High, over several weeks. There is no narration or music, only classes, meetings, corridors and discipline sessions, in which teachers and students are shown in their daily routines. The film is a cornerstone of observational documentary, and a revealing study of authority and conformity in American education.
