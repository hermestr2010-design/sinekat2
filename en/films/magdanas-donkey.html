Plot

A short film co-directed with Rezo Chkheidze that won best short fiction at Cannes in 1956.

Sinekatalog’s take

Tengiz Abuladze and Rezo Chkheidze's short film follows a poor Georgian woman whose donkey is her only treasure, with a gentle satire of bureaucracy. Humane and poetic, it won Cannes' best short fiction in 1956.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
