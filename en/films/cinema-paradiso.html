Plot

A successful director, Salvatore, returns to his Sicilian home town for the funeral of Alfredo, the projectionist who taught him about film as a boy. Through memories, we see the cinema where he watched films with the villagers, and the censorship by the priest, as well as his first love. Giuseppe Tornatore's 1988 film, with Ennio Morricone's score, won the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
