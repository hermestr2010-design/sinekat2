Plot

A street singer in a Paris suburb, a young Romanian woman and his rival become entangled in love and crime. One of the first French sound films.

Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's early sound film follows a street singer, a Romanian girl and a rival in a Paris neighbourhood of love and petty crime. Light, musical and visually playful, it is a charming, influential poetic-realist treat.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
