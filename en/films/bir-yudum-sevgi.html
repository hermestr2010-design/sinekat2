Plot

A drama that won him the Golden Orange for best director and was voted the best Turkish film of 1985 at the Istanbul festival. Known as Bir Yudum Sevgi. English title: A Sip of Love.

Sinekatalog’s take

Atıf Yılmaz's drama, which won the Golden Orange for best director, explores love and loneliness with his usual sympathy for women. Warm and well-crafted, it was voted the best Turkish film of 1985 at Istanbul.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
