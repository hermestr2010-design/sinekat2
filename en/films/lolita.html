Sinekatalog’s take

Stanley Kubrick's adaptation of Nabokov tiptoes around the novel's shocking content. James Mason and Peter Sellers are brilliant, making it a darkly comic study of obsession, though it is tamer than the book.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
