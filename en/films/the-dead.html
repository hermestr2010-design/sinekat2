Plot

A Dublin Christmas party in 1904, based on the James Joyce story, was his last film.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Huston's final film recreates a Dublin Christmas party from Joyce's story. Tender, quiet and moving, with Anjelica Huston superb, its ending is a gorgeous meditation on love, memory and death.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
