Sinekatalog’s take

François Truffaut's third Antoine Doinel film is light, charming and full of New Wave humour. Jean-Pierre Léaud is wonderful as the bumbling dreamer, in a gentle comedy of love and growing up.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
