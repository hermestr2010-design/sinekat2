Sinekatalog’s take

Miyazaki's jaunty adventure has a pig-faced WWI ace flying for hire over the Adriatic. Light, romantic and loaded with aerial charm, it is a more adult, melancholy Ghibli entry.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
