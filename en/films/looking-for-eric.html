Sinekatalog’s take

Ken Loach's comedy-drama has a struggling postman advised by an imaginary Eric Cantona. Warm, funny and sweetly political, it is one of Loach's lightest and most crowd-pleasing films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
