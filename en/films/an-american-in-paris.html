Plot

Jerry Mulligan, a former American soldier, stays in Paris to become a painter. He meets a rich patron who sponsors him and a young French girl, Lise, with whom he falls in love, though she is engaged to someone else. The film ends with a seventeen-minute ballet set to George Gershwin's music, danced by Gene Kelly and Leslie Caron in sets inspired by French painters. Vincente Minnelli's 1951 musical won six Academy Awards, including Best Picture.

Sinekatalog’s take

Vincente Minnelli's Gershwin musical is a glorious Technicolor treat, climaxing in a 17-minute ballet. Gene Kelly's dancing and Leslie Caron's debut make it joyous, even if the story is slight.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
