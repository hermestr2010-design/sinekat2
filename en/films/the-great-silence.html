Sinekatalog’s take

Sergio Corbucci's snowbound spaghetti western is among the genre's bleakest, with Jean-Louis Trintignant's mute gunman against Klaus Kinski. Its brutal ending shocked audiences and has made it a revered cult classic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
