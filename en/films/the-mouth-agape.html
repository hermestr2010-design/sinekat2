Sinekatalog’s take

Maurice Pialat's raw drama follows a family around a dying mother, the husband's infidelities and the son's distance. Unsentimental and painfully honest, with Monique Mélinand, it is a bleak, powerful study of death and indifference.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
