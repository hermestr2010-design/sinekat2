Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Jafar Panahi's playful drama follows a little girl lost on her way home, then breaks the fourth wall midway. Smart and touching, with a wonderfully natural child lead, it is a clever blend of fiction and documentary.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
