Plot

In 1944, at a Catholic boarding school in occupied France, the twelve-year-old Julien Quentin becomes curious about a new pupil, Jean Bonnet, a quiet and gifted boy. Gradually the two become friends and Julien discovers that Jean is Jewish and hidden by the priests. The Gestapo arrives. Louis Malle's 1987 film, based on his own childhood memory, is a restrained and devastating story of friendship and betrayal, and won the Golden Lion in Venice.
