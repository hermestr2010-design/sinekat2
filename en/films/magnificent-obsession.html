Plot

A reckless playboy tries to redeem himself after he is indirectly responsible for the death of a doctor, and for the blindness of the doctor's widow.

Sinekatalog’s take

Douglas Sirk's glossy melodrama has Rock Hudson's playboy seeking redemption by helping the widow he blinded. Lush, absurd and beautifully made, it is a defining weepie of Technicolor excess.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
