Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Vsevolod Pudovkin's silent comedy has a man so obsessed with chess that he neglects his bride, with a cameo by champion Capablanca. Quick, witty and playful, it is a charming slapstick short in the Soviet silent tradition.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
