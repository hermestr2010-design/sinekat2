Atıf Yılmaz's drama follows a woman and the reactions of her society to her sexuality, a bold topic for Turkish cinema. Sympathetic and daring, it was entered in the Moscow festival and is a notable feminist work.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
