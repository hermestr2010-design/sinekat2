Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Josef von Sternberg's silent drama, often called the first gangster film, has a crook adopting a drunken lawyer and a fatal love triangle. Atmospheric and tough, with George Bancroft, it set the template for the genre.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
