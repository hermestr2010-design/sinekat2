Sinekatalog’s take

Steve McQueen's documentary overlays narration of Nazi-occupied Amsterdam onto present-day footage of the same addresses. Long and rigorous, it is a haunting, deadpan meditation on memory, place and history.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
