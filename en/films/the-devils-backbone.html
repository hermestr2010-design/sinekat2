Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Guillermo del Toro's ghost story set in a Spanish Civil War orphanage is haunting and humane. Its eerie ghost and its human villains are equally frightening, and the film is moving and beautiful.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
