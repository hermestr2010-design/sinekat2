Plot

A Slovenian film, Hudodelci, directed and written by Franci Slak in 1987 and entered at the 1988 Berlin Film Festival. It is regarded as a notable work of late-Yugoslav Slovenian cinema, from a director who went on to be an important figure in the country's film scene.
