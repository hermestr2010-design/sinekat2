Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Margarethe von Trotta's documentary surveys Bergman's life and work through interviews, clips and visits to his Fårö, with his collaborators and admirers. Warm and thoughtful, though conventional, it is a good introduction to the great Swede.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
