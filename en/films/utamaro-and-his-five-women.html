Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Kenji Mizoguchi's period drama follows the great woodblock artist Utamaro and the women who inspire and suffer around him. Kinuyo Tanaka and Minosuke Bando star in a graceful, thoughtful portrait of art and freedom under the shogunate.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
