Cast

Plot

A Catalan businessman and a marquess hunt on his estate as Spain moves from dictatorship to democracy.

Sinekatalog’s take

Luis García Berlanga uses a hunting party on a marquess's estate to mock Spain's elite as the dictatorship ends. It is a crowded, noisy farce with bite, a bitter joke about people who learn nothing.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
