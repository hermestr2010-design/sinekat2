La Terra Trema

1948 · 2 h 40 min · Directed by Luchino Visconti
Drama
Watch trailer

Cast

Non-professional fishermen of Aci Trezza

Plot

A Sicilian fishing family tries to break free from the wholesale buyers who exploit them. It was filmed with local fishermen speaking their own dialect.

Sinekatalog’s take

Luchino Visconti's neorealist epic follows Sicilian fishermen fighting exploitative wholesalers. Shot with locals in dialect and bleakly beautiful, it is a monumental, uncompromising portrait of labour and poverty.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
