Plot

In 208 AD, two southern warlords form an alliance to face the army of the northern chancellor Cao Cao at the Battle of Red Cliff.

Sinekatalog’s take

John Woo's epic retelling of the Three Kingdoms battle is vast, colourful and thrilling. Its tactics and spectacle impress, and the heroes' camaraderie gives the huge battles a human heart.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
