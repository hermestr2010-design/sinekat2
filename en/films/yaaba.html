Plot

A boy befriends an old woman who is shunned by her village as a witch, and she comes to his aid when his cousin falls ill.

Sinekatalog’s take

Idrissa Ouédraogo's tender fable follows a boy who befriends an old woman shunned as a witch by her Burkina Faso village. Simple, luminous and humane, it is a beautiful, much-loved classic of African cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
