W.R.: Mysteries of the Organism

1971 · Directed by Dušan Makavejev
DocumentaryComedy
Watch trailer

Cast

Milena Dravić, Jagoda Kaloper, Ivica Vidović

Plot

A collage of documentary footage about the psychoanalyst Wilhelm Reich and his theories of sexual liberation is intercut with a fictional story of a Yugoslav woman, Milena, who preaches free love in Belgrade, and then meets a Soviet skater with violent consequences. Dušan Makavejev's 1971 film is an anarchic, playful and provocative work, which was banned in Yugoslavia.
