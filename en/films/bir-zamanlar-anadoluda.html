Plot

Police, a prosecutor and a doctor search the Anatolian steppe at night for a murder victim's body. It won the Grand Prix at Cannes. English title: Once Upon a Time in Anatolia.

Sinekatalog’s take

Nuri Bilge Ceylan follows a police convoy searching for a buried body across Anatolian hills at night. Slow, dry-humoured and beautifully shot, it turns a procedural into a reflection on guilt and fate.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
