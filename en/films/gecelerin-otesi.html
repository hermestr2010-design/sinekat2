Sinekatalog’s take

Metin Erksan's early drama follows a troubled love and its social pressures in a stark, sharp style. Tense and well-acted, it reveals his talent before the success of Dry Summer and remains well regarded by critics.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
