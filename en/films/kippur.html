Plot

Weinraub and Ruso, two Israeli soldiers, are called up for service at the start of the 1973 Yom Kippur War. They join a helicopter rescue unit and spend the war trying to evacuate the wounded from the front, in a chaotic and frightening experience. Amos Gitai's 2000 film, drawn from his own experience as a helicopter rescue soldier, was in competition at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Amos Gitai's war drama follows two Israeli soldiers in a helicopter rescue unit during the chaotic 1973 Yom Kippur War. Shot with long takes and mud, it is a harrowing, unglamorous and authentic picture of fear and futility.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
