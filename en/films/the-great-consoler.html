Plot

A sound film inspired by the writer O. Henry, in which the writer in prison consoles inmates with stories that deny the truth of their situation.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lev Kuleshov's sound film, inspired by O. Henry, follows a writer in prison who consoles inmates with false stories, exposing the power of illusion. Thoughtful and formal, with striking staging, it is a subtle, bittersweet work from a master of montage.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
