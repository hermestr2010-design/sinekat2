Plot

A drama named best film at the Antalya Golden Orange festival. English title: Destiny.

Sinekatalog’s take

Zeki Demirkubuz's austere drama of doomed love and fate is bleak and compelling. Rooted in his recurring themes of obsession and humiliation, it won the Golden Orange as a stark highlight of Turkish arthouse.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
