Plot

A gambler and his family endure decades of political upheaval in China, from the 1940s to the 1970s. It won the Grand Prix at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Zhang Yimou's family saga follows a couple through decades of Chinese upheaval. Ge You and Gong Li are superb, and the film mixes humour and sorrow into a humane, banned-then-celebrated epic.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
