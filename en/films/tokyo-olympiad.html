Plot

A documentary of the 1964 Tokyo Olympic Games that focuses on the athletes and on the human side of competition.

Sinekatalog’s take

Kon Ichikawa's documentary of the 1964 Games focuses on athletes' faces, nerves and small moments rather than medal counts. Lyrical and humane, with brilliant long-lens photography, it is one of the great sports films.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
