Plot

A young factory girl in a small Czech town, where the girls greatly outnumber the boys, falls for a pianist and travels to Prague to find him.

Sinekatalog’s take

Miloš Forman's Czech New Wave comedy follows a factory girl chasing a pianist to Prague. Gentle, funny and humane, with a hilarious bedroom scene, it captures young longing and awkwardness with warmth.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
