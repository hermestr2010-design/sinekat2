Plot

The life of Vincent van Gogh, from his time among the miners of Belgium to his years in Arles and his death.

Sinekatalog’s take

Vincente Minnelli's biopic of Vincent van Gogh has Kirk Douglas giving a passionate, restless performance. Vibrant colour and Provençal landscapes capture the painter's vision, even if the drama is conventional.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
