Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Rainer Werner Fassbinder's claustrophobic chamber piece traces a fashion designer's obsession with a young model. Stagey, stylised and scathing, with Margit Carstensen superb, it is a brilliant dissection of love as power.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
