Sinekatalog’s take

Abbas Kiarostami's quiet, comic meditation follows a film crew waiting in a Kurdish village for an old woman to die. Lyrical and elusive, it asks about patience, curiosity and what we choose to see.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
