Plot

A poet in modern Paris follows Death, a princess in a black car, into the underworld to find his wife and is drawn to Death herself.

Sinekatalog’s take

Jean Cocteau's modern Orpheus myth is poetic, strange and filled with homemade magic. Its mirror-to-underworld effects, radio-borne poetry and Jean Marais's performance make it a dreamlike masterpiece.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
