Plot

The film follows Chiron, a young Black man in Miami, at three stages of his life: as a quiet boy, called Little, who is bullied, as a teenager, and as an adult named Black. He struggles with his identity, his sexuality and his mother's addiction, and with his friendship with Kevin. Barry Jenkins's 2016 film won the Academy Award for Best Picture.

Sinekatalog’s take

Barry Jenkins tells one man's life in three chapters with remarkable delicacy and visual beauty. Quiet, tender and deeply felt, it says more in glances than most films do in speeches.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
