Plot

Young Bulgarian communists in the anti-fascist resistance during the Second World War, in a film that won the Golden Prize at Moscow in 1962.

Sinekatalog’s take

Binka Zhelyazkova's drama follows young Bulgarian communists in the wartime anti-fascist resistance, with unusual honesty and tenderness.
