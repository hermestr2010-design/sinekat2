Plot

A documentary about people who gather leftover crops and discarded goods, and about Varda's own ageing.

Sinekatalog’s take

Agnès Varda's documentary about people who gather leftovers is witty, humane and personal. Roving across France with a small camera, she turns scavenging into a gentle meditation on waste, community and ageing.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
