Sinekatalog’s take

Jean-Pierre Melville's Cocteau adaptation shuts a brother and sister in a private world of games and destructive devotion. Eerie, theatrical and hypnotic, with Nicole Stéphane unforgettable, it is a stylised, claustrophobic cult favourite.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
