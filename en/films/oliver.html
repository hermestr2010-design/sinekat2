Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Carol Reed's Dickens musical is a big, bright, tuneful crowd-pleaser. Its songs, dances and Ron Moody's Fagin are memorable, though it softens the darker edges of the novel.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
