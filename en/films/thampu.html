Plot

A black-and-white film in a documentary style about the sufferings of a travelling circus troupe, which visits a village in Kerala. The performers, the villagers and the troupe's owners are shown at work, in a village where the circus tent is set up and taken down. G. Aravindan's 1978 film, shot with non-professional actors, won him the National Award for best director.

Sinekatalog’s take

G. Aravindan's black-and-white drama follows a travelling circus troupe visiting a Kerala village, in a semi-documentary style. Honest and beautifully composed, it is a quiet, humane portrait of performers and the poor.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
