The Lodger

1927 · Directed by Alfred Hitchcock
ThrillerMysteryHorror
Watch trailer

Cast

Ivor Novello, June Tripp, Marie Ault, Arthur Chesney

Plot

A mysterious lodger is suspected of being a serial killer stalking London.

Sinekatalog’s take

Hitchcock's early silent thriller has a mysterious lodger suspected of being a London serial killer, with Ivor Novello brooding in the lead. It is the first true Hitchcock film, full of his later obsessions, from wrong men to the fear of what is hidden.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
