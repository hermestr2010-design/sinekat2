Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Two sisters meet gentle forest spirits in rural Japan. Miyazaki's film has almost no conflict, just wonder and warmth, and it is an ideal first Ghibli film for children and adults alike.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
