Sinekatalog’s take

Eric Rohmer's breezy Brittany comedy has a young man torn among three women on holiday. Light, funny and subtle, with Melvil Poupaud and Amanda Langlet, it is a charming study of indecision.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
