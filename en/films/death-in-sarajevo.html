Plot

On the day of the centenary of the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand, a hotel in Sarajevo is on the verge of a strike while a VIP speech is prepared.

Sinekatalog’s take

Danis Tanović's film unfolds on the centenary day of Franz Ferdinand's assassination in a Sarajevo hotel, as staff and VIPs collide. Brisk and layered, it is a talky, ironic look at Europe's past and the Balkan present.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
