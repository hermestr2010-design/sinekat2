Sinekatalog’s take

D.W. Griffith's first sound film follows Lincoln from his youth to assassination, with Walter Huston in the title role. Dignified and earnest, but stiff and episodic, it is more important as history than as drama.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
