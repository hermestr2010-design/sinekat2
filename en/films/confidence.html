Plot

A man and a woman share a room while hiding from the Arrow Cross near the end of the Second World War. It won him best director at Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take

István Szabó's chamber drama shuts a man and a woman in a room as they hide from the Arrow Cross near the war's end. Ildikó Bánsági and Péter Andorai are intense in a tight, intimate study of trust and fear that won Berlin's directing prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
