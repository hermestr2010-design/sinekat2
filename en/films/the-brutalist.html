Plot

László Tóth, a Hungarian-Jewish architect who survived the Holocaust, emigrates to Pennsylvania in 1947, where he is commissioned by a wealthy industrialist, Harrison Lee Van Buren, to design a monumental community centre. Over the decades the relationship between artist and patron is tested. Brady Corbet's 2024 epic, with Adrien Brody, who won an Oscar for his performance, runs more than three and a half hours.

Sinekatalog’s take
