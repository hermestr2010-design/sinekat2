Plot

His debut, an Italian-Spanish-Turkish co-production shown at Cannes in the Directors' Fortnight, which won the Golden Orange for best director and best film.

Sinekatalog’s take

Ferzan Özpetek's debut follows an Italian man who inherits a Istanbul hammam and finds a new life. Sensual and warm, with a gentle Turkish setting, it is a pleasing, bittersweet story of identity and belonging.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
