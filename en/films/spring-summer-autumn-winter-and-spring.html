Cast

Oh Young-soo, Kim Ki-duk, Kim Young-min

Plot

On a floating temple in a mountain lake in Korea, an old Buddhist monk raises a boy through the seasons of life, from childhood innocence, through desire and violence, to maturity, and then back to renewal. The five parts are named after the seasons of the title. Kim Ki-duk's 2003 film is a quiet, visually serene meditation on karma and the cycle of life.
