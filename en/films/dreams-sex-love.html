DramaRomance
Watch trailer

Cast

Ella Øverbye, Selome Emnetu, Ane Dahl Torp

Plot

Johanne, a seventeen-year-old girl in Oslo, falls in love with her French teacher and writes about the experience in a text. Her mother and grandmother read it and discuss it, and their reactions reveal different views about love, desire and writing. Dag Johan Haugerud's 2024 film, the final part of his trilogy, won the Golden Bear in Berlin.

Sinekatalog’s take

Dag Johan Haugerud's drama follows a seventeen-year-old who writes about falling for her French teacher, which her mother and grandmother then read and debate.
