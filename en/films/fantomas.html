Plot

Sinekatalog’s take

Louis Feuillade's 1913 serial begins the five-part saga of the masked master criminal who commits crimes across Paris and eludes the detective Juve. Eerie, playful and atmospheric, it is a landmark of French silent crime fiction beloved by the Surrealists.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
