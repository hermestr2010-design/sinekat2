Sinekatalog’s take

Richard Linklater's rotoscoped dream-film drifts through philosophical talk about reality and free will. Its shimmering animation suits the subject, and its ideas are stimulating, though it is more a conversation than a story.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
