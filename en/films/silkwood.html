Plot

A worker at a nuclear plant in Oklahoma collects evidence of safety violations and dies in a mysterious car crash.

Sinekatalog’s take

Mike Nichols's drama about Karen Silkwood's nuclear plant whistleblowing is quiet and serious. Meryl Streep is superb, and the film works as both a personal story and a chilling warning.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
