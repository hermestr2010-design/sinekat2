Plot

In the 1930s, the legendary concierge Gustave H. runs a famous hotel in the fictional Republic of Zubrowka, with his devoted lobby boy, Zero Moustafa. When a wealthy guest dies and leaves Gustave a priceless painting, he is accused of her murder and the pair go on the run. Wes Anderson's 2014 comedy, with Ralph Fiennes, won four Academy Awards.

Sinekatalog’s take

Wes Anderson's dollhouse caper about a legendary concierge and his lobby boy. Every frame is meticulously composed, with a bittersweet note about a vanished Europe underneath the jokes.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
