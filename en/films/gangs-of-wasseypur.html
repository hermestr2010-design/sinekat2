Plot

A two-part crime saga about the coal mafia of Dhanbad, from the 1940s to 2009, following three generations of the Khan family and their blood feud with the Qureshi family and the rival Ramadhir Singh. Anurag Kashyap's 2012 epic, with Manoj Bajpayee, Nawazuddin Siddiqui and Richa Chadha, mixes violence, dark humour and Bollywood music, and won the Filmfare Critics Award.

Sinekatalog’s take

Anurag Kashyap's sprawling crime saga follows rival families in a coal-mining town across decades. Violent, funny and full of film references, it is a landmark of modern Hindi cinema, in two long parts.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
