Plot

His first feature, which won the first prize at Locarno. Known in French as Charles mort ou vif.

Sinekatalog’s take

Alain Tanner's debut follows a wealthy Swiss businessman who abandons his job and family and takes refuge with two young freethinkers. François Simon is superb in a witty, bracing satire that won Locarno's top prize.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
