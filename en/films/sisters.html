Sisters

“They were joined at birth by the devil and the evil never left them!”

1973 · 1 h 33 min · Directed by Brian De Palma
HorrorMysteryThriller
Watch trailer

Cast

Margot Kidder, Jennifer Salt, Charles Durning

Plot

A journalist witnesses a murder in her neighbour's apartment and tries to prove it to a sceptical police force. The neighbour has a sister, or perhaps not.

Sinekatalog’s take

Brian De Palma's early thriller of a murder witnessed through a window is sly and Hitchcockian. Margot Kidder is excellent, and the split-screen sequence is a stylish highlight in a clever, creepy film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
