Written by: Robert Musil

Plot

At an Austrian boarding school in 1900, two students torment a classmate while a third watches, fascinated and troubled.

Sinekatalog’s take

Volker Schlöndorff's debut adapts Musil's tale of schoolboys torturing a classmate at an Austrian boarding school. Cold, tense and perceptive about cruelty and complicity, it is a landmark of the New German Cinema.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
