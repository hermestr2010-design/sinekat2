Sinekatalog’s take

Robert Altman's Depression-era drama follows young bank robbers and their doomed romance. Keith Carradine and Shelley Duvall are touching, and the film's radio-soaked, quiet melancholy offers a gentler counterpart to Bonnie and Clyde.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
