Plot

The true story of Oskar Schindler, a German industrialist who saved more than a thousand Jews during the Holocaust.

Sinekatalog’s take

Spielberg's restrained, black-and-white account of the Holocaust avoids spectacle and lets small human moments carry the weight. The girl in the red coat and the final scenes are hard to forget. Essential, and not easy viewing.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
