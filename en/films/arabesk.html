Sinekatalog’s take

Ertem Eğilmez's late drama takes on arabesk music, class and migrant longing in Turkey. Melodramatic and sincere, it captures the sorrow and pop culture of a whole social moment.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
