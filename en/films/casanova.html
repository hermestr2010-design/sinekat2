Sinekatalog’s take

Federico Fellini's lavish portrait of the eighteenth-century adventurer turns Casanova into a puppet-like, joyless seducer, played by Donald Sutherland. Stylised, cold and sumptuous, it is a baroque, grotesque and divisive Fellini carnival.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
