Sinekatalog’s take

Brian De Palma's Hitchcock homage, scored by Bernard Herrmann, is a lush, romantic thriller of grief and doubles. Cliff Robertson and Geneviève Bujold are fine, and the twist is both silly and strangely moving.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
