Sinekatalog’s take

René Clair's musical farce chases a lottery ticket left in a lost jacket through Paris. Fast, rhythmic and delightfully silly, with sound used playfully, it is a charming early talkie and a joy of light French comedy.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
