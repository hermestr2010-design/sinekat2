The Sprinkler Sprinkled

Original title: Arroseur et arrosé
1895 · 1 min · Directed by Louis Lumière
Comedy
Watch trailer

Cinematography: Francis Doublier

Plot

A gardener is watering when a boy steps on the hose. When the gardener looks into the nozzle, the boy releases it. One of the earliest comedies.

Sinekatalog’s take

Louis Lumière's 1895 one-minute film shows a boy stepping on a gardener's hose, in one of cinema's first fictional gags. Simple and charming, it is the founding sight gag of film comedy and still wins a smile.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
