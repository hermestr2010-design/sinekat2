Sinekatalog’s take

John Ford's modest western follows two cowboys guiding Mormons across the frontier. Warm, unhurried and lyrical, with beautiful landscapes and little star power, it was one of Ford's own favourites.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
