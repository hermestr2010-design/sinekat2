Plot

Mija, a sixty-six-year-old woman living in a small Korean town and caring for her grandson, takes a poetry class at the same time as she is diagnosed with the early stages of Alzheimer's. She learns that her grandson is involved in a terrible crime, and tries to find the words and the moral strength to respond. Lee Chang-dong's 2010 film won the Best Screenplay prize at Cannes.

Sinekatalog’s take

Lee Chang-dong's quiet drama follows an elderly woman in early dementia joining a poetry class while facing a terrible family secret. Yoon Jeong-hee is extraordinary in a delicate, devastating, profoundly humane film.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
