Sinekatalog’s take

Hong Sang-soo tells one story twice, from the man's view then the woman's, in black and white and with his usual awkward, funny drinking scenes. Small and clever, it is about how memory edits itself to protect us.Sinekatalog’s own editorial review – not a user or critic quote.
