Adventure

Adventure sends heroes out into the unknown: swashbuckling pirates and knights, jungle explorers, treasure hunts and sea voyages. Silent stars like Douglas Fairbanks and Errol Flynn's Warner Bros. films established the model; David Lean and Kurosawa brought epic scale; Spielberg's Raiders of the Lost Ark revived the serial spirit. Location, momentum and a sense of discovery matter more than plot depth.

Where
The United States, Britain, Japan, France
When
From the 1910s; classic peak in the 1930s–60s
Key figures
Douglas Fairbanks, Michael Curtiz, David Lean, Akira Kurosawa, Steven Spielberg
Landmark films
The Adventures of Robin Hood (1938), The African Queen (1951), Lawrence of Arabia (1962), Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981)
Legacy
Made travel, danger and discovery a core pleasure of popular cinema

What to look for

  • Distant, spectacular locations
  • A quest with a clear goal
  • Heroes tested by landscape and rivals
  • Humour alongside danger
  • Music that carries the momentum
