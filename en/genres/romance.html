Romance

Romance puts a relationship at the centre and asks whether it will survive. Silent melodramas like Sunrise and Hollywood's studio-era weepies set the pattern; Lubitsch and the screwball comedies added wit; Wong Kar-wai, Linklater's Before trilogy and Kieslowski's Three Colours made longing the subject itself. The genre is defined less by happy endings than by the chemistry of its leads and the obstacles placed between them.

Where
The United States, France, India, Hong Kong, Japan
When
From the 1910s to today
Key figures
F.W. Murnau, Ernst Lubitsch, Douglas Sirk, David Lean, Wong Kar-wai, Richard Linklater
Landmark films
Sunrise (1927), Casablanca (1942), Brief Encounter (1945), In the Mood for Love (2000)
Legacy
Made desire, restraint and heartbreak into a lasting screen language

What to look for

  • Chemistry between the leads
  • Obstacles of class, duty or timing
  • Looks and silences instead of declarations
  • Music and colour doing emotional work
  • Endings that are earned, happy or not
