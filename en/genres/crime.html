Crime

The crime film follows lawbreakers, investigators and the systems around them. It grew from the gangster cycle of early 1930s Hollywood and from French poetic realism, then found new forms in Melville's cool procedurals, the New Hollywood of Coppola and Scorsese, Hong Kong's heroic bloodshed and Korean revenge dramas. At its best it uses crime to examine loyalty, class and the price of ambition.

Where
The United States, France, Japan, Hong Kong, South Korea, Italy
When
From the 1900s; the gangster cycle begins around 1930
Key figures

Landmark films
Scarface (1932), The Godfather (1972), Goodfellas (1990), Infernal Affairs (2002)
Legacy
Made the criminal a tragic, sometimes sympathetic protagonist

What to look for

  • Loyalty and betrayal inside a closed group
  • Cities as moral landscapes
  • Heists planned and executed in detail
  • Cops and criminals as mirror images
  • Violence with consequences
