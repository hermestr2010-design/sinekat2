Biography

Biographical films, or biopics, dramatise real lives: artists, leaders, scientists, athletes, outlaws. Hollywood's studio biographies of the 1930s–40s gave way to more critical treatments such as Lawrence of Arabia, Raging Bull and Amadeus. The challenge is to find a shape in a life without flattering it, and the best biopics focus on one revealing period or conflict rather than a cradle-to-grave summary.

Where
The United States, Britain, France, Russia, Italy
When
From the 1910s; studio peak 1930s–50s
Key figures

Legacy
Brought history and celebrity together as a prestige genre and awards favourite

What to look for

  • One decisive period instead of the full life
  • Performances that capture voice and manner
  • Period design and costume
  • Tension between legend and fact
  • Endings that reflect on legacy
