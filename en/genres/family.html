Family

Family films are made to be watched across generations. Disney's animated features, MGM's Technicolor adventures, British children's classics and later Pixar and Studio Ghibli titles set the tone: stories of growing up, friendship and courage, told with humour and heart. The best do not talk down to children and give adults something to enjoy as well.

Where
The United States, Britain, Japan, Germany
When
From the 1930s to today
Key figures
Walt Disney, Victor Fleming, Hayao Miyazaki, Steven Spielberg, Brad Bird
Landmark films
Snow White (1937), The Wizard of Oz (1939), E.T. (1982), My Neighbor Totoro (1988)
Legacy
Created a shared cinema experience for children and parents together

What to look for

  • Child or animal protagonists
  • Humour that works at two levels
  • Clear moral stakes
  • Music and memorable characters
  • Warm, hopeful endings
