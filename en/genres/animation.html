Animation

Animation builds worlds frame by frame, whether drawn, painted, sculpted or digital. Emile Cohl and Winsor McCay worked in the silent era; Disney's Snow White (1937) proved the feature-length form; Japan's Miyazaki and Takahata at Studio Ghibli, Eastern Europe's puppet and stop-motion artists, and Pixar's computer-animated features each created major traditions. Because nothing has to resemble reality, animation can be the most imaginative form of cinema.

Where
The United States, Japan, France, Czech lands, Russia
When
From 1908; feature-length from 1937
Key figures
Walt Disney, Hayao Miyazaki, Isao Takahata, Jan Švankmajer, Satoshi Kon
Landmark films
Snow White (1937), Akira (1988), Spirited Away (2001), Toy Story (1995)
Legacy
Proved that animation is a full art form for all ages, not just children's entertainment

What to look for

  • Movement designed rather than filmed
  • Distinct visual styles for each film
  • Transformation as a storytelling tool
  • Music and sound built with the picture
  • Emotion carried by simple shapes
