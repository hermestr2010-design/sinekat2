Metin Erksan

Turkey · The Modern Masters

Turkish Cinema

Born in Çanakkale in 1929 and dying in Istanbul in 2012, Metin Erksan was one of the leading directors of Turkish cinema of the 1960s. Dry Summer (Susuz Yaz, 1963), a harsh story of a farmer who dams a spring so that only his own land is watered, won the Golden Bear at Berlin in 1964, the first Turkish film to win a major European prize. Time to Love (Sevmek Zamanı, 1965) is a fantasy of a painter in love with the portrait of a woman. He was also a film critic and a teacher.
