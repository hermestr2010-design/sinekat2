Jacques Tati

France · The Modern Masters

Visual Comedy

Born in 1907 in France and died in 1982, Tati was a mime and comic who created the gentle, bumbling Monsieur Hulot. His films use very little dialogue and rely on carefully staged visual gags about modern life and technology.
