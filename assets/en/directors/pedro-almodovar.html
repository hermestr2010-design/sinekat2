Pedro Almodóvar

Spain · The New School

Spanish Cinema

Born in Spain in 1949, Almodóvar emerged in the post-Franco cultural explosion with colourful, emotional films about women, desire and family. He has won Oscars for All About My Mother and for the screenplay of Talk to Her.
