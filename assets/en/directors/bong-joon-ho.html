Bong Joon-ho

South Korea · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in Daegu, South Korea, in 1969, Bong blends genres, social satire and sudden shifts of tone. Parasite won the Palme d'Or and became the first non-English-language film to win the Oscar for Best Picture.

Filmography

  • Memories of Murder (2003)
  • The Host (2006)
  • Mother (2009)
  • Snowpiercer (2013)
  • Okja (2017)
  • Parasite (2019)
