Robert Altman

USA · The Modern Masters

New Hollywood

Born in Kansas City in 1925 and died in 2006, Altman was known for overlapping dialogue, roaming cameras and large ensemble casts. His films use genre as a way to explore American society in a wry, skeptical way.
