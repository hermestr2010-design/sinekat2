Leopoldo Torre Nilsson

Argentina · The Golden Age

Latin American Cinema

Born in Buenos Aires in 1924, Leopoldo Torre Nilsson was one of the most important Argentine directors of the postwar years, known for dark psychological dramas of family, class and repression. Works include El crimen de Oribe (1950), Días de odio (1954), Graciela (1956), Martín Fierro (1968) and Los siete locos (1973), which won a Silver Bear at Berlin. He died in 1978.
