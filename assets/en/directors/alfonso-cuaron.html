Alfonso Cuarón

Mexico · The New School

Latin American Cinema

Born in Mexico City in 1961, Cuarón has worked in Mexico, Hollywood and Britain, moving between genres with long, technically ambitious takes. He won the Oscar for Best Director for Gravity and for Roma.
