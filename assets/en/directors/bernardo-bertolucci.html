Bernardo Bertolucci

Italy · The Modern Masters

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Parma in 1941 and died in 2018, Bertolucci was a poet's son who became an admired Italian director of politically and visually rich films. Last Tango in Paris was famous and controversial, and the way a scene was filmed with Maria Schneider has been widely criticised.
