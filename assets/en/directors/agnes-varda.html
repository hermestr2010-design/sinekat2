Agnès Varda

France · The Modern Masters

French New Wave

Born in Brussels in 1928 and died in Paris in 2019, Varda was a photographer before becoming a pioneer of the French New Wave. She moved freely between fiction and documentary with warmth, curiosity and a strong feminist eye.
