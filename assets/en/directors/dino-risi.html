Dino Risi

Italy · The Modern Masters

Commedia all'Italiana

Born in Milan in 1916 and died in 2008, Risi trained as a psychiatrist before becoming one of the masters of commedia all'italiana. His comedies observe Italian vanity and weakness with a bitter smile, and Il Sorpasso is one of the classics of the genre.
