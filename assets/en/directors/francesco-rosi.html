Francesco Rosi

Italy · The Modern Masters

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Naples in 1922 and died in Rome in 2015, Francesco Rosi worked as an assistant to Visconti before directing his own films. He built a form of investigative cinema in which a story is reconstructed from documents, witnesses and contradictory accounts, probing the links between power, crime and politics in Italy. Salvatore Giuliano (1962) became a model for political filmmaking. The Mattei Affair (1972) won the Palme d'Or at Cannes, shared with The Working Class Goes to Heaven.
