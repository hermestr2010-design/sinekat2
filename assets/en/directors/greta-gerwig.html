Greta Gerwig

USA · The New School

Women Directors

Born in Sacramento in 1983, Greta Gerwig first made her name as an actress in the independent films of Joe Swanberg and Noah Baumbach, which she co-wrote, including Frances Ha (2012). She made her solo directing debut with Lady Bird (2017), a funny and affecting story of a teenager's last year in school in Sacramento, which earned her an Oscar nomination for directing. Little Women (2019) was a modern adaptation of Louisa May Alcott. Barbie (2023) became the highest-grossing film of the year and the highest-grossing film ever directed by a woman.
