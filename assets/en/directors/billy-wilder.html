Billy Wilder

USA · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in 1906 in what is now Poland and died in 2002, Wilder worked as a screenwriter in Berlin before fleeing the Nazis for Hollywood. He moved between film noir, satire and comedy with a sharp, cynical wit and won Oscars for directing and writing.
