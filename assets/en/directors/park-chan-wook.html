Park Chan-wook

South Korea · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in Seoul in 1963, Park is known for stylish, violent and darkly witty films about revenge, desire and moral decay. Oldboy won the Grand Prix at Cannes, and he won Best Director there for Decision to Leave.
