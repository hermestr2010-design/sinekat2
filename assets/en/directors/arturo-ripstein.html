Arturo Ripstein

Mexico · The Modern Masters

Latin American Cinema

Born in Mexico City in 1943, Arturo Ripstein began as an assistant to Luis Buñuel and made Tiempo de morir (1965) at twenty-one. He is known for bleak melodramas, often written with Paz Alicia Garciadiego: The Castle of Purity (1973), The Place Without Limits (1978), Deep Crimson (1996), Such Is Life (2000) and Bleak Street (2015). Several have been in competition at major festivals.
