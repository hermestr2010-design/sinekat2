Quentin Tarantino

USA · The New School

American Independent Cinema

Born in Knoxville, Tennessee, in 1963, Tarantino worked in a video store before making Reservoir Dogs. His films mix crime, pop culture and stylised violence with sharp, talkative dialogue and non-linear storytelling.
