Masaki Kobayashi

Japan · The Golden Age

Japanese Golden Age

Born in Hokkaido in 1916 and died in 1996, Kobayashi was a pacifist who served in the Japanese army in the Second World War, and his films are fierce critiques of authority, war and conformity.

Filmography

  • The Human Condition (1959)
  • Harakiri (1962)
  • Kwaidan (1964)
  • Samurai Rebellion (1967)
  • Fossils (1975)
  • Tokyo Trial (1983)
