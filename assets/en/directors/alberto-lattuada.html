Alberto Lattuada

Italy · The Golden Age

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Milan in 1914 and died in 1991, Lattuada was a director of the post-war generation who moved between neorealist drama, literary adaptations and satirical comedy. He co-directed Variety Lights with Federico Fellini, who made his debut as a director on it.
