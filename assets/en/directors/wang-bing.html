Wang Bing

China · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in 1967, Wang Bing is a Chinese documentary filmmaker who films with a hand-held digital camera and long takes. His nine-hour West of the Tracks (2003) records the decline of the industrial district of Tiexi in Shenyang. He also made Fengming: A Chinese Memoir (2007), a woman's account of the anti-rightist campaign, Man with No Name, Three Sisters (2012) about children in a Yunnan village, 'Til Madness Do Us Part (2013) in a psychiatric hospital, Dead Souls (2018) and Youth (Spring) (2023).
