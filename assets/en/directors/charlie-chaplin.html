Charlie Chaplin

UK · The Silent Era

Visual Comedy

Born in London in 1889 and died in 1977, Chaplin rose from a poverty-stricken childhood to become the most famous face of the silent era. As the Tramp he mixed slapstick with sentiment and social comment, and he wrote, directed, starred in and scored his own films.
