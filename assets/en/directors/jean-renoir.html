Jean Renoir

France · The Golden Age

French Poetic Realism and Classical Cinema

Born in Paris in 1894 and died in 1979, Renoir was the son of the painter Pierre-Auguste Renoir. His humane, fluid films of the 1930s made him the leading figure of French cinema before he worked in Hollywood and India during and after the war.
