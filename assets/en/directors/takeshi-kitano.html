Takeshi Kitano

Japan · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in Tokyo in 1947, Takeshi Kitano is a comedian, television host and actor, known to Japanese audiences as 'Beat' Takeshi. As a film director he took over Violent Cop (1989) and made a name with a series of deadpan yakuza films, such as Sonatine (1993), in which violence erupts suddenly in a quiet, still frame. He also paints, and his own paintings appear in Hana-bi (1997), which won the Golden Lion at Venice. A motorcycle accident in 1994 left his face partly paralysed. He later won the Silver Lion for best director for Zatoichi.
