The Coen Brothers

USA · The New School

American Independent Cinema

Joel (born 1954) and Ethan Coen (born 1957) grew up in Minnesota and have made films together for decades, mixing dark comedy, crime and unlikely heroes in one of the most distinctive American partnerships in film.
