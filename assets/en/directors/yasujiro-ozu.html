Yasujiro Ozu

Japan · The Golden Age

Japanese Golden Age

Born in Tokyo in 1903 and died in 1963, Ozu made quiet domestic dramas about family, marriage and the passing of generations. He was known for a low, static camera and calm, balanced compositions that influenced filmmakers around the world.
