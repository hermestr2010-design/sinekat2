Sergio Leone

Italy · The Modern Masters

Spaghetti Western

Born in Rome in 1929 and died in 1989, Leone reinvented the western with long silences, extreme close-ups and grand operatic scores by Ennio Morricone. His spaghetti westerns made Clint Eastwood a star, and he later made an epic gangster saga.

Filmography

  • A Fistful of Dollars (1964)
  • For a Few Dollars More (1965)
  • The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966)
  • Once Upon a Time in the West (1968)
  • A Fistful of Dynamite (1971)
  • Once Upon a Time in America (1984)
