Vishal Bhardwaj

India · The New School

Popular Indian Cinema

Born in Chandpur, Uttar Pradesh, in 1965, Vishal Bhardwaj is a director, screenwriter and music composer who adapts Shakespeare to Indian settings. He began as a composer, made his directing debut with the children's film Makdee (2002), and then made a trilogy of Shakespeare adaptations: Maqbool (2003), from Macbeth, Omkara (2006), from Othello, and Haider (2014), from Hamlet, set in Kashmir in 1995. His other films include The Blue Umbrella, Kaminey, 7 Khoon Maaf and Rangoon. He has won National Film Awards for music, children's film, screenplay and a special jury award.
