Paul Thomas Anderson

USA · The New School

American Independent Cinema

Born in Studio City, California, in 1970, Anderson began with sprawling ensemble films about Los Angeles and moved toward darker character studies. He often works with the actors Philip Seymour Hoffman and Daniel Day-Lewis.
