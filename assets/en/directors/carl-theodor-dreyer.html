Carl Theodor Dreyer

Denmark · The Golden Age

Scandinavian Cinema

Born in Copenhagen in 1889 and died in 1968, Dreyer made only a handful of feature films, each austere, spiritual and intense, with close-ups of faces and a slow, deliberate pace.

Filmography

  • The Parson's Widow (1920)
  • Leaves from Satan's Book (1921)
  • Michael (1924)
  • Master of the House (1925)
  • The Passion of Joan of Arc (1928)
  • Vampyr (1932)
  • Day of Wrath (1943)
  • Two People (1945)
  • Ordet (1955)
  • Gertrud (1964)
