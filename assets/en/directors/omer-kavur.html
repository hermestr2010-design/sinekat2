Ömer Kavur

Turkey · The Modern Masters

Turkish Cinema

Born in Ankara in 1944 and dying in Istanbul in 2005, Ömer Kavur was an intellectual film-maker of the Turkish new cinema, whose films were mood-driven, literate and visually precise, often adapting modern Turkish novels and exploring loneliness, memory and identity. He began with Yatık Emine (1974), and Yusuf ile Kenan (1979) later won a Golden Orange. Kırık Bir Aşk Hikayesi (1981) and Gece Yolculuğu (1987) were followed by Anayurt Oteli (1987), the story of a lonely hotelkeeper, based on Yusuf Atılgan's novel, which is his best known film. Gece Yolculuğu and Akrebin Yolculuğu (1997) were shown at Cannes in Un Certain Regard.
