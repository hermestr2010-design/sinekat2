Hou Hsiao-hsien

Taiwan · The New School

Taiwan New Cinema

Born in Guangdong, China, in 1947 and raised in Taiwan, Hou is a leading figure of Taiwan New Cinema. His films use long takes, distant camera positions and elliptical stories to portray memory, family and the history of Taiwan. A City of Sadness won the Golden Lion at Venice and The Assassin won him the Best Director prize at Cannes.
