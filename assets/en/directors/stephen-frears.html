Stephen Frears

UK · The New School

British Cinema

Born in Leicester in 1941, Stephen Frears worked in television and with Karel Reisz and Lindsay Anderson before directing films. My Beautiful Laundrette (1985), written by Hanif Kureishi, was a lively portrait of a young Pakistani man in Thatcher's London. He then went to Hollywood for Dangerous Liaisons and The Grifters, and returned with a series of British films about real people: The Queen (2006), which won Helen Mirren an Oscar, and Philomena (2013). He is admired for his versatility and wit.
