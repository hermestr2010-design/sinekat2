Denis Villeneuve

Canada · The New School

Blockbuster Auteurs

Born in Gentilly, Quebec, in 1967, Denis Villeneuve made his name in Québécois cinema with Polytechnique (2009), about the 1989 Montreal massacre, and Incendies (2010), an Oscar-nominated drama about twins tracing their mother's past in the Middle East. He moved to Hollywood with Prisoners (2013) and Sicario (2015), before turning to large-scale science fiction: Arrival (2016), Blade Runner 2049 (2017), and the two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's Dune (2021, 2024). He is known for slow, atmospheric, large-scale films and for working with the cinematographer Roger Deakins and the composer Hans Zimmer.
