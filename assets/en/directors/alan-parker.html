Alan Parker

UK · The Modern Masters

British Cinema

Born in Islington, London, in 1944 and died in 2020, Alan Parker came from advertising, where he met the producer David Puttnam. His first feature Bugsy Malone (1976) was a gangster musical with a cast of children. Midnight Express (1978), about an American held in a Turkish prison, brought him wide attention and a BAFTA for directing, and he went on to make Fame (1980), Pink Floyd - The Wall, Mississippi Burning (1988) and The Commitments (1991), which also won him the BAFTA. He was a leading figure in the British film industry, and chaired the British Film Institute and the Film Council.
