Rainer Werner Fassbinder

Germany · The Modern Masters

New German Cinema

Born in Bavaria in 1945 and died in Munich in 1982 at the age of thirty-seven, Fassbinder made more than forty films in about fifteen years. His melodramas of power and exploitation in love and society were central to New German Cinema.
