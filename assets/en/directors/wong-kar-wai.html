Wong Kar-wai

Hong Kong · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in Shanghai in 1958 and raised in Hong Kong, Wong makes romantic, stylish films about missed connections and longing, shot with Christopher Doyle and with Tony Leung in many leading roles.
