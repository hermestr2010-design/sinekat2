Yorgos Lanthimos

Greece · The New School

Balkan Cinema

Born in Athens in 1973, Yorgos Lanthimos directed commercials, theatre and music videos before his breakthrough Dogtooth (2009), about parents who keep their adult children in a controlled isolation. It became a leading work of the Greek 'weird wave'. He then made English-language films in a deadpan, absurd style: The Lobster (2015), The Killing of a Sacred Deer (2017), The Favourite (2018), a costume comedy of court intrigue in Queen Anne's England, and Poor Things (2023), which won the Golden Lion at Venice. Emma Stone has won Oscars in his films.
