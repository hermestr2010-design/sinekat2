Jorge Sanjinés

Bolivia · The Modern Masters

Latin American Cinema

Born in La Paz in 1936, Jorge Sanjinés founded the Ukamau group and made films with indigenous actors and communities, often in Quechua and Aymara. His films include Ukamau (1966), Yawar Mallku (Blood of the Condor, 1969), El coraje del pueblo (1971), El enemigo principal (1973) and ¡Fuera de aquí! (1977).
