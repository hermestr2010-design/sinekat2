Luis Buñuel

Spain · The Golden Age

Surrealism and the Avant-Garde

Born in Calanda, Spain, in 1900 and died in Mexico City in 1983, Buñuel began as a surrealist, collaborating with Salvador Dalí, and kept a dark, satirical view of religion, class and desire throughout a career in France, Mexico and Spain.
