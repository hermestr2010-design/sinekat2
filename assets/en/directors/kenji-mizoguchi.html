Kenji Mizoguchi

Japan · The Golden Age

Japanese Golden Age

Born in Tokyo in 1898 and died in 1956, Mizoguchi is known for long, flowing takes and for sensitive portraits of women in conflict with a harsh, male-dominated society. His work reached Western audiences when Ugetsu won a prize at Venice.
