David Lynch

USA · The New School

Surrealism and the Avant-Garde

Born in Missoula, Montana, in 1946 and died in 2025, Lynch was a painter before becoming a filmmaker. His work, including the series Twin Peaks, merges dream logic, small-town America and the unsettling underside of ordinary life.
