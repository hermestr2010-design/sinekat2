Alejandro G. Iñárritu

Mexico · The New School

Latin American Cinema

Born in Mexico City in 1963, Alejandro González Iñárritu was a radio host and television director before making Amores perros (1999), which burst onto the international scene. With the screenwriter Guillermo Arriaga he made 21 Grams and Babel, intertwined stories across different places. Birdman (2014), shot to look like one continuous take, won the Oscars for best picture and best director, and The Revenant (2015) won him a second directing Oscar.
