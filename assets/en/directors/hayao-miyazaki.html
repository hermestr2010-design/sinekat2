Hayao Miyazaki

Japan · The New School

Contemporary Asian Cinema

Born in Tokyo in 1941, Miyazaki co-founded Studio Ghibli and became the most celebrated Japanese animator. His films mix fantasy, environmental themes and strong young heroines. Spirited Away and The Boy and the Heron both won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.
