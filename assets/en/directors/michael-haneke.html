Michael Haneke

Austria · The New School

Contemporary European Art Cinema

Born in Munich in 1942 and based in Austria, Haneke makes cold, rigorous films that confront viewers with violence, guilt and the way media shapes what we see. He has won the Palme d'Or twice, for The White Ribbon and Amour.
