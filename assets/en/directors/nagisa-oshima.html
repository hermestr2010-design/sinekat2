Nagisa Oshima

Japan · The Modern Masters

Japanese New Wave

Born in Kyoto in 1932 and died in 2013, Oshima was the leading voice of the Japanese New Wave. He left the Shochiku studio to make provocative, political and formally daring independent films about youth, sexuality, authority and Japan's relationship with Korea.
