Michelangelo Antonioni

Italy · The Modern Masters

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Ferrara in 1912 and died in Rome in 2007, Antonioni made cool, visually precise films about alienation and the emptiness of modern life, with plots that deliberately resist neat resolutions.
