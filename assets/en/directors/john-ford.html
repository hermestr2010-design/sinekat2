John Ford

USA · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in Maine in 1894 and died in 1973, Ford won four Academy Awards for Best Director and is closely linked with the western and the landscape of Monument Valley. His films with John Wayne helped define American screen myths, though his later work looked at those myths more critically.
