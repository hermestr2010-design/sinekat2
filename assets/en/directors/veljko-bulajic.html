Veljko Bulajić

Croatia · The Modern Masters

Balkan Cinema

Born in Vilusi, Montenegro, in 1928 and dying in Zagreb in 2015, Veljko Bulajić made the partisan epics of Yugoslav cinema, with state support. His war film Kozara (1962) was a big success. The Battle of Neretva (1969), the biggest Yugoslav production of its time, with Yul Brynner, Orson Welles and Franco Nero, was a partisan epic of the 1943 battle against German and Italian forces, and was nominated for the Oscar for foreign language film. It was supported personally by Tito.
