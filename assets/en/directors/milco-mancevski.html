Milčo Mančevski

North Macedonia · The New School

Balkan Cinema

Born in Skopje in 1959, Milčo Mančevski studied film in the United States and worked as a director of commercials and music videos. His debut Before the Rain (1994), a three-part story of a monk, a photographer and a village killing in Macedonia and London, won the Golden Lion at Venice and an Oscar nomination. It was the first film from independent Macedonia to be widely seen abroad. Dust (2001) and Shadows (2007) followed.
