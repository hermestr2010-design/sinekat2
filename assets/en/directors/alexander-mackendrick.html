Alexander Mackendrick

UK · The Golden Age

British Cinema

Born in Boston in 1912 to Scottish parents, Alexander Mackendrick grew up in Glasgow and began in advertising and animation. At Ealing Studios he directed four of its best-loved comedies, Whisky Galore!, The Man in the White Suit, Mandy and The Ladykillers, combining satire with a dark undertone. He then went to Hollywood to make Sweet Smell of Success (1957), a bitter portrait of a gossip columnist and a press agent in New York. From 1969 he headed the film programme at CalArts, where he taught a generation of directors.
