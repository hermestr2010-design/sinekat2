Ernst Lubitsch

Germany · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in Berlin in 1892 and died in 1947, Lubitsch began as an actor and director in Germany and then moved to Hollywood. His sophisticated comedies are famous for the Lubitsch touch, a way of hinting at things with wit and economy instead of saying them.
