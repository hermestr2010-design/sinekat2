Theo Angelopoulos

Greece · The New School

Balkan Cinema

Born in Athens in 1935 and died in 2012 after being struck by a motorcycle while filming, Angelopoulos was the leading Greek director of his generation. His slow, majestic films use long takes to explore Greek history, exile and memory.
