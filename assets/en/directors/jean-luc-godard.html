Jean-Luc Godard

France · The Modern Masters

French New Wave

Born in Paris in 1930, Godard was a critic at Cahiers du cinéma before directing Breathless, one of the defining films of the French New Wave. He broke the rules of editing and storytelling, and his later work became more political and experimental.
