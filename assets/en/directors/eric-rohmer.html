Eric Rohmer

France · The Modern Masters

French New Wave

Born in 1920 and died in 2010, Rohmer was a critic and editor of Cahiers du cinéma before becoming a New Wave director. His talkative, sunlit series of Moral Tales and Comedies and Proverbs follow people debating love and desire.
