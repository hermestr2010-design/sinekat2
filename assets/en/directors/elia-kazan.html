Elia Kazan

USA · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in Constantinople (now Istanbul) in 1909 and died in 1999, Kazan co-founded the Actors Studio and brought psychological, naturalistic acting to the screen. His reputation remains controversial because he named former colleagues to the House Un-American Activities Committee in 1952.
