Ingmar Bergman

Sweden · The Golden Age

Scandinavian Cinema

Born in Uppsala in 1918 and died on the island of Fårö in 2007, Bergman was the leading figure of Swedish cinema, known for intense chamber dramas about faith, death, love and the fragility of identity, often with a loyal group of actors.
