Michael Curtiz

Hungary · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in Budapest in 1886 and died in 1962, Curtiz worked in Hungary, Austria and Germany before becoming one of the most prolific directors at Warner Bros. in Hollywood, moving easily between adventure, melodrama, musicals and film noir.
