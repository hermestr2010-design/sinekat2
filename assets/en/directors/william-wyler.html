William Wyler

USA · The Golden Age

Classical Hollywood

Born in Mulhouse, Alsace, in 1902 and died in 1981, William Wyler came to the United States as a young man and became one of the most respected directors of Hollywood's classical period. A perfectionist known for his many takes, he directed Bette Davis in Jezebel and The Little Foxes and won Oscars for Mrs. Miniver, The Best Years of Our Lives and Ben-Hur. He made films in nearly every genre, always with careful composition in depth, and was nominated for the best director Oscar a record twelve times.
