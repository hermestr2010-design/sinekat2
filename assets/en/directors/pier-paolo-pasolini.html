Pier Paolo Pasolini

Italy · The Modern Masters

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Bologna in 1922 and murdered in 1975, Pasolini was a poet, novelist and political thinker as well as a filmmaker. His work often combined Marxism, Catholicism and the lives of the poor, and several of his films caused scandal.
