Hany Abu-Assad

Palestine · The New School

Israeli and Palestinian Cinema

Born in Nazareth in 1961 and trained as an aircraft engineer in the Netherlands, Hany Abu-Assad directed Rana's Wedding (2002) and Ford Transit (2002) before Paradise Now (2005), about two friends recruited as suicide bombers, which won a Golden Globe and was nominated for an Academy Award. Omar (2013) won the Jury Prize in Un Certain Regard at Cannes and was also Oscar-nominated.
