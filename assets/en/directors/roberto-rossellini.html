Roberto Rossellini

Italy · The Golden Age

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Rome in 1906 and died in 1977, Rossellini was a founding figure of Italian neorealism. His war films shot among the ruins of 1945 and 1946 used real locations and many non-professional actors, and he later made intimate dramas starring Ingrid Bergman.

Filmography

  • Paisan (1946)
  • Germany Year Zero (1948)
  • The Flowers of Saint Francis (1950)
  • Stromboli (1950)
  • Europe '51 (1952)
  • Journey to Italy (1954)
  • General Della Rovere (1959)
  • The Rise of Louis XIV (1966)
  • Socrates (1971)
