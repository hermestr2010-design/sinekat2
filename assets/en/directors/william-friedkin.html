William Friedkin

USA · The Modern Masters

New Hollywood

Born in Chicago in 1935 and died in 2023, Friedkin began in television documentaries. His thrillers of the early 1970s, The French Connection and The Exorcist, used a rough, realistic style that helped define New Hollywood. He won the Oscar for Best Director for The French Connection.

Filmography

  • The French Connection (1971)
  • The Exorcist (1973)
  • Sorcerer (1977)
  • To Live and Die in L.A. (1985)
