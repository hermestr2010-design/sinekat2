Andrzej Wajda

Poland · The Modern Masters

Polish Cinema

Born in 1926 and died in 2016, Wajda was the leading figure of the Polish Film School. His films about the war, Stalinism and the Solidarity movement made him the conscience of Polish cinema, and he received an honorary Oscar in 2000.
