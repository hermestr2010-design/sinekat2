Chen Kaige

China · The New School

Chinese Fifth Generation

Born in Beijing in 1952, Chen Kaige is a leading figure of China's Fifth Generation, the first class to graduate from the Beijing Film Academy after the Cultural Revolution. As a teenager he was sent to work in Yunnan and served in the army, experiences that shaped his later films. Yellow Earth (1984), shot by Zhang Yimou, announced a new visual language for Chinese cinema. Farewell My Concubine (1993) shared the Palme d'Or at Cannes, the first Chinese film to win it. He has since made large-scale historical dramas and worked in both China and abroad.
