Robert Bresson

France · The Modern Masters

Slow and Contemplative Cinema

Born in 1901 and died in 1999, Bresson developed a spare, spiritual style that used non-professional actors, which he called models, and avoided overt emotional effects. His films are quiet but intense studies of faith, grace and imprisonment.
