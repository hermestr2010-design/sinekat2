Fritz Lang

Germany · The Silent Era

German Expressionism

Born in Vienna in 1890 and died in Los Angeles in 1976, Lang trained as an architect and brought a strong visual and architectural sense to his films. In Germany he made epic fantasies, crime serials and the sound thriller M. After leaving Germany in the 1930s he worked in Hollywood, where he made a string of film noirs.
