Vittorio De Sica

Italy · The Golden Age

Italian Neorealism and After

Born in Sora, Italy, in 1901 and died in Paris in 1974, De Sica was first a popular actor and then a director whose neorealist films with non-professional casts showed the lives of ordinary people in post-war Italy with great compassion.

Filmography

  • Shoeshine (1946)
  • Bicycle Thieves (1948)
  • Miracle in Milan (1951)
  • Umberto D. (1952)
  • Two Women (1960)
  • Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow (1963)
  • Marriage Italian Style (1964)
  • The Garden of the Finzi-Continis (1970)
