Louis Malle

France · The Modern Masters

French New Wave

Born in Thumeries, France, in 1932 and died in 1995, Malle began as an assistant to Jacques Cousteau before directing his first feature at twenty-five. He worked in France and the United States and moved between genres, from thrillers to comedies to memories of the war years, always with a curious, independent eye.
