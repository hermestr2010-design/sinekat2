Martin Scorsese

USA · The Modern Masters

New Hollywood

Born in Queens, New York, in 1942, Scorsese grew up in Little Italy and became one of the central figures of New Hollywood. His films explore guilt, violence and obsession through restless camerawork and a deep love of film history.
