Stephen Daldry

UK · The New School

British Cinema

Born in Dorset in 1960, Stephen Daldry trained in theatre and became artistic director of the Royal Court Theatre in London, where he directed An Inspector Calls, a production that toured the world. His first feature, Billy Elliot (2000), the story of a miner's son in County Durham who wants to be a ballet dancer, was a surprise success and earned him an Oscar nomination for best director. He was nominated again for The Hours (2002) and The Reader (2008), and made Extremely Loud & Incredibly Close (2011). He also directed the stage version of Billy Elliot the Musical and was an executive producer of The Crown.
